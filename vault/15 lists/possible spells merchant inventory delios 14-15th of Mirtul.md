# 4th Level
1. Dimension Door: Instantly teleport yourself up to 500 feet to a location you can visualize.
2. Fire Shield: Surround yourself with flickering flames that provide both offensive and defensive benefits.
3. Polymorph: Transform a creature into a different form, altering its physical characteristics and abilities.
4. Ice Storm: Summon a freezing hailstorm that deals cold and bludgeoning damage to creatures in the area.
5. Leomund's Secret Chest: Create an extradimensional chest that can be summoned and dismissed at will.
6. Evard's Black Tentacles: Conjures writhing black tentacles that grapple and restrain creatures within their reach.
7. Otiluke's Resilient Sphere: Encase yourself or another creature in an indestructible sphere, protecting them from harm.
8. Arcane Eye: Create an invisible magical sensor that can be remotely controlled to see and hear what it observes.
9. Stone Shape: Manipulate stone and rock formations, allowing you to reshape and alter their form.
10. Hallucinatory Terrain: Alter the appearance of natural terrain, making it appear different and deceptive to others.
