 A Tiri Kitor patrol consists of four 2nd-level wild elf rangers (see page 124), each mounted on a giant owl (MM 205). The elves swoop down to observe the PCs and determine what business they have in the Blackfens. If the PCs have already befriended the Tiki Kitor tribe, the elves wish the PCs luck and offer any assis- tance they can. Otherwise, the elves simply circle the group three times, observing them, and then fly off to the nearest Tiri Kitor camp to make a report. If attacked, they flee immediately.
