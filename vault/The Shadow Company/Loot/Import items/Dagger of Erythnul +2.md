The dagger is a +2 magical dagger. 

This dagger is made from a dark iron with a deep crimson tint, it looks like a piece of metal that was torn violently from a larger piece. With jagged and bent edges that glints with a slightly rusted coloration, as if it's covered in dried up blood. The hilt is wrapped in coarse rust colored canvas and it has a round pommel with a face engraved that is half demon half boar.

The blade is heavier than you'd expect it to be. 

The blade has 3 small bloodstones set into it's guard. 

3/long rest, if used to strike a good alligned creature, the user can force that creature to make a DC 15 Con save or take 3d6 necrotic damage. 
The user then heals themselves by half the necrotic damage dealt.
