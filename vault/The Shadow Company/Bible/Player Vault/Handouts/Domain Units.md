# Domain Units
Main:
Related: [[Organisations MCDM - what is it]]

## Units

Each unit in the organization must be controlled by an officer of the organization. Each Officer can command a number of units equal to their proficiency bonus. 

During the Intrigue, players can muster more units. 
Special domain units can only be mustered during Intrigue.

The Army/Units of the Organization obeys the commands of the officers and can be deployed outside of intrigue or warfare for narrative purposes.

As a rule of thumb, an army can perform any activity that an organization's officer could perform but which the characters are too high level to bother with, from escorting a diplomat to quelling and uprising of cultists. Usually these things would fall under the **Opertions skill** 

###### **Special units**
Every type of domain (organization) has an action it can use to summon/muster a special unit of rare and powerful creatures. This can be done once pr Intrigue, if the attempt fails, additional attempts can be made during the same Intrigue until successful or the confrontational battle occurs. The special unit disbands and leaves after the battle it is used *(an Intrigue always leads up to a battle and units are mustered to participate in this battle. Defenses are sabotaged or bolstered for this battle as well.)*  The special unit can be mustered again in a new Intrigue. 

A domain can also make use of other special units, by making alliances with other domains and see if the leaders of those are willing to lend their own special units for a battle. This is one reason to do "quests" for other domains (organizations) to gain their goodwill and perhaps the use of their special unit. 

There are also a third type of special units, monsters from the monster book can also in rare cases find themselves on the battlefield as units.

Army composition has a tier dependency, the highest tier must have one less unit than the tier below it and so on. (3 x Tier one allows 2x Tier two.)

Units have these tiers of experience 
regular

Units can gain these equipment tiers 
light 
medium 
heavy 
super-heavy 

**Experience comes from surviving battles**
• **Regular** units become **veteran** units if they, survive their first battle. Easy!
• **Veteran** units become **elite** units if they survive three more battles. Much harder.
• **Elite** units become **super-elite** units after surviving four more battles (eight total).

**Levies** cannot be improved, as the untrained troops who make up those units are considered to go back to their lives as soon as their task in battle is done.
### Modifying Units
During Intrigue you can use a domain action and the Operations skill to upgrade as many units as are controlled by the officer using the domain action. Upgrades can be made from any level to any lavel you can afford. From light to super-heavy in one go. The costs are cumulative. 

| **Upgrade from…**    | **Cost**                        |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Light to medium      | 500 gp × the tier of the unit   |
| Medium to heavy      | 1,000 gp × the tier of the unit |
| Heavy to super-heavy | 2,000 gp × the tier of the unit |
