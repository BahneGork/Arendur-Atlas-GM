# Aurilia's HQ Report

- Many people have sought out the headquarters of the Shadow Company, in the hopes of earning money the only way they know how, getting revenge on the goblins, fleeing from authority, creating a new life for themselves or any number of other reasons. The people "making camp" at the Fallen star inn are many and diverse. 
- See Organization Diagram appendix
- I need the Senior Officers to decide how to develop the organization. ( You have 8 points to allocate in either a skill or a defense.)
- Current start skill & defense mod/score is highlighted. 
- Organization skills:  diplomacy, espionage, lore, operations are skills that define how your organization interacts with the world and other organizations. 
	- each skill is the summation of "an entire department in the organization", followers, retainers, experts etc.. 
	- the organizations npc's can mount operations on their own by direction of the senior officers, enabling the organization to do things while the pc's are adventuring as well as doing things that are outside the scope of adventuring. 
- Domain defenses:  communications, resolve, resources, the number on the sheet is the DC score an opposed organization has to beat to sabotage that defense 
	- Defenses also come into play in warfare, during intrigue the organization skills are used to either bolster your own defense or sabotage the opposing organizations defenses 
- Each senior officer takes turn allocating 1 point until there are no points left. 
	- either 1 point in a skill or 1 point in a defense 
	- Blank cells counts as a point allocation, that doesn't improve the modifier or score, in essence making the next modifier/score cost extra points. 
	- See the [Compendium](https://discord.com/channels/487691960447139850/1171173157105827882) for more information on skills and defenses

# Structuring the Organization
### Chiefs - Department Managers - Junior Officers
These are your contacts in the organization for the various skills and defenses.
They run the day to day as well as plan and/or carry out operations requested by the senior officers. 
Therefore they do not go on adventures with the party as regular retainers. 
Any senior officer can order them around, even though they may have a specific senior officer they answer to.
