Martial Rally. 
At the beginning of this commander’s turn, a broken unit the commander controls that has not already been rallied can make a DC 13 Morale test. On a success, it gains 1d4 casualties and is returned to the battlefield according to normal deployment rules. Additionally, the unit gets two activations this turn, instead of one.
