# House Varrow of Arendur

House Varrow is aligned with Major House Alvares, Duke Alvares. 

# Emrik Varrow

Late 30's
Middle son 
Sent to serve as bodyguard of House Castilla as a penance for a cousins transgression that he did not stop or report in time.
The punishment was sentenced 10 years ago, and is indeffinent until he is recalled/summoned back to his family.

Part of his training as a young man was initiation into the school of the honed mind, one of the 5 battlemaster schools of Arendur.

Emrik appears observant and cautious, deliberate and thoughtful in his sparse conversation.
Emrik appears methodical in his approach to combat, with precise movements he seemingly thinks three steps ahead. 
He frowns with disdain upon recklessness and punishes this in training with a precise countermove. 
To Emrik, discipline and control are paramount to making conscious intentional combat choices.
Ever is he pointing out situational awareness.

Emrik Varrow is trained in the school of Battlemasters known as The Order of the Methodical Blade, which is noted by the symbol hanging from his belt, a Triangle with an eye in the center.

#### **[[The Order of the Methodical Blade]]
- **Focus**: Battlefield strategy and tactics.
- **Philosophy**: The Order of the Methodical Blade believes that true mastery of battle is achieved through intellect. Warriors of this order are trained to analyze the battlefield, adapting their tactics to exploit the enemy’s weaknesses and disrupt their plans. Combat is a game of foresight and strategic positioning.
- **Techniques**: Methodical Blade Battlemasters employ a broad range of weapons but rely heavily on tactical maneuvering. They are often the leaders or tacticians in larger battles, where they direct allies and position forces with precision.
- **Symbol**: An eye within a triangle


# Handout Til [[Frekka]]

Bodyguard of Lord Davide Castilla, Emrik Varrow. 

Da Frekka træner med Lord Davide's bodyguards og lærer featen at stå bedre fast med sit greatsword, observerer hun at en af de her bodyguard virker meget mere bevidst og kalkuleret med sine bevægelser og handlinger. Han er umiddelbart den af dem der ser ældst ud, måske sidst i 30erne eller på den anden side af 40. 

Emrik Varrow, virker observant, forsigtig, bevidst og betænksom i sine udtalelser og væremåde, en smule reserveret. De andre lader til at se op til ham selv om han ikke virker som om han gør noget som helst for at være nogen form for lederrolle.

Emrik er metodisk i hans tilgang til kamp og med præcise bevægelser lader han til at tænke 3 skridt frem foran sin modstander.
Han har ikke smag for letsindighed og tankeløs kamp, i sin træning af Frekka bliver det straffet med præcise modspil, ofte i samspil med bemærkninger om at skærpe sin situationelle årvågenhed. Emrik sætter disciplin og kontrol meget højt, de er nøglen til bevidste kamp valg imod et beslutsomt mål.

Emrik Varrow er trænet i Battlemaster skolen Den Metodiske Klinge.
