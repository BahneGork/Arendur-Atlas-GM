# THE Necromancer
![[THE necromancer image.png]]

Hayden Bisset is a merchant from Kvalec who is obsessed with gathering arcane occult power.

He was a member of an occult society for a long time, deeply curious about secrets of the universe. He came across historical documents that mentions  [[the sapphiretail staff]] [The Staff of Sapphiretail](https://www.notion.so/The-Staff-of-Sapphiretail-4b77c08881df490bb93cbb90a200905e) and since then became super focused on this one item. To him everything else was nick nacks. During his research and investigation he made a pact with an old one and gained knowledge of something known as the [Whisper Skulls](https://www.notion.so/Whisper-Skulls-c3e761adbec44db196611101c61a1a07) that promised him knowledge and power of the undead and he knew of a long dead man who could give him more information of where the Sapphiretail was last known to be.

has been searching for various sources of power over the years, his journal shows a great lust for knowledge of power and little in terms of inhabitions. This was the reason he got into an occult society in Kvalec, it was in this society he found some old documents and read about the Sapphiretail staff.

All other objects of desire seem to fade away once he learned the name of sapphiretail, all his entries after is about his quest to find it. He has scoured libraries, use lots of his merchant fortunes to pay bards for their knowledge, he has sought and bought all manner of books, scrolls and objects from anyone.  Nothing mundane gave him any knowledge. 

At some point he seems to have made a pact with some otherworldly entity he calls Atlach-Nacha, who promised to give him more knowledge. Through his pact he learned of an ancient creation from Tashrans Glory days, the whispers skull and their supposed power of the dead. His notes surmise that if he can gain the power to question the dead, maybe he can find the Tomb of Durso Preiss and ask him where the staff was last seen.

He has been following clues and leads about the whisper skulls and learned one was locked away in an ancient catacomb from an even older empire than Tashran. A secret demon worshipping cult of Orcus had learned of the catacomb, underneath an unknowing monastery and infiltrated the monk order and began doing their secret cult things in the catacombs. Eventually they were discovered and eradicated by The silent sisters. The journal does not go into who they are. He does not mention any dates or years, but it seems to be a long time ago, obscure knowledge people would have forgotten today.
