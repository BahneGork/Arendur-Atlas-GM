# Saint Elowen Brightsword

Title: The Radiant Commander
Saint of Paladine
Description: Saint Elowen Brightsword was a legendary paladin and military genius who defended the realm against darkness and tyranny. She is often depicted clad in shining armor, wielding a brilliant sword that emits a radiant light. Elowen is revered for her unwavering commitment to **justice**, **leadership** skills, and **unyielding faith** in Paladine's ideals.
