# Lady Dazran
The Painkiller Lady Dazran could serve as an ally or enemy for the characters in your game. 
She is a ruthless, efficient killing machine. 

She is a painkiller, contracted with Dispater lord of Dis, city of war.
When hell invades, Dispaters army does the fighting, killing and dying.
We painkillers are the knights, lords and ladies who leads Dispaters armies from the front.
proud, hubris, obsessed with personal appearance
will accept and honor challenges to single combat, but will cheat if loosing, toy if winning.

The lords of the seven cities of hell are easy master, they ask little. As long as we serve the greater good.

Round 1 - by the throat and baleful interdict move 5 feet closer, for aura of despair.
round 2 - summon hell (bone devil) and baleful interdict - another seal 
round 3 - great sword attacks - another seal

Can heal with her own health up to 8d10
Can drain up to 8d10 from unwilling and heal as much
