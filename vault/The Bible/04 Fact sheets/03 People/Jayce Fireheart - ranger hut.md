# Jayce Fireheart

A blue skinned Orc, with fiery red eyes, reminding of the blue Orc encountered in Lady dagger caves, [[Oregg Steeltwister]]. 

Jayce is in fact from the same clan, the white tusk orc clan, last he knew it was led by [[Bonebreaker Dorokor]] a female who showed an interrest in the politics of men. 

Jayce is not his real name, his orc name was Skoth the shadow. 

Jayce was part of a group that tried to take on [[Regilarix - lore]].

**Terrible news**

- his other party members did not survive.

- Magical resistance to acid only works for so long, the power of a dragon can burn through the protection and make it collapse. The protection they had gathered for the fighter lasted for one attack and the next one hit him sqaurely in the chest and burned right through his armor.
