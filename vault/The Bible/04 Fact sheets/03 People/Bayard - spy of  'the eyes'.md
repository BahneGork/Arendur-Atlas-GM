# His Story for Being in the Lady Dagger Caves
“I’ve been investigating the disappearing merchants on the main road from [[Arendur]]. Eventually i found that someone called “[[lady dagger]]” was running a handful of gangs up and down the road. Took me awhile to get myself inserted in this one, I still havent found out where she lived when she was not with the bandits.” - Jeg har kigget nærmere på overfald af handlende mellem [[Arendur]] og grænsen, jeg fandt efter noget tid at nogen der blev kaldt “Lady Dagger” styrede eller koordinerede en håndfuld [[bandit]] grupper langs handels ruten. Det tog mig noget tid at infiltrere dem og nå her til. Jeg mangler stadigvæk at finde ud af hvor hun “bor” når hun ikke opholder sig med banditterne.

“Who i work for? Oh i thought… well maybe not everyone is familiar.. Okay, I suppose there is no harm in telling you lot. I work as a [[spy]] for Snake eyes, [[Eyes of the many headed snake]]? We mostly do work for hire, but we also function as the Thrones intelligence gathering outfit.” - Hvem jeg arbejder for? Øhm jeg troede at… måske skiltet.. Nå men okay, jeg arbejder som spion for “Slangens Øjne” eller “Øjnene af den mange hovedet Slange” også kendt som Medusaerne? Det er hovedsageligt kommisions arbejde, men som de eneste er vi også Tronens Efterretnings organisation. 

“I was looking for a way to get close and alone with Lady Dagger, seemed prudent to get rid of her. Trade was suffering and she’s more than a mere [[thug]], she enjoys it too much i think. There’s something very sinister about her.” - Jeg var igang med at udse mig en måde at komme tæt på Lady Dagger og alene med hende. Hendes bortgang ville være til gavn for [[Arendur]]’s handel og så er hun generelt ubehagelig, alt for engageret i vold frem for almen tyveri. Der er noget vederstyggeligt over hende.
