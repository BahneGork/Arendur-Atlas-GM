# Thurumbaba the Cyclops

Rusted iron fingernails
huge amulet shaped like a flatted capital i or a fallen H 

Normally cyclops shy away from civilized lands and live like hermits or in small groups and are not normally warlike. However, they can be gullable.

Thurumbaba was convinced by one of the Black lilly knights to raid the country side. 

The black lilly knight is [[The tear knight]], who leaves behind nut shells like pistacioes
