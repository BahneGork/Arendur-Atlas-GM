## The Hag Bargain

### Original Deal
**Hexton's Promise**: To become the new Baron of [[Bedegaarn Barony]]  
**Hexton's Payment**: 
1. **Ignore goblin raid reports** - Turn blind eye to supernatural activities
2. **Sacrifice his first newborn** - Personal cost for political power

**Parties Involved**:
- **Regent Hexton** - Ambitious political figure seeking baronial power
- **Green Hag** - Supernatural entity orchestrating the arrangement
- **Elf Woman** - Companion/agent working with the Green Hag

### Deal Fulfillment
✅ **Hexton achieved baronial seat** - Now holds power in [[Bedegaarn Barony]]  
⚠️ **Ongoing obligations** - Must continue ignoring goblin activities  
⚠️ **Personal cost** - Obligation regarding his firstborn child  

## Current Political Dilemma

### Image Management Crisis
**The Problem**: Hexton now worries about his public reputation if he continues completely ignoring goblin threats

**His Solution**: 
- **Issue bounties** for goblin raiders
- **Target the Elf Woman** who accompanied the Green Hag during negotiations
- **Justify targeting** - Claims bounties are only for "attack on the baron's keep"
- **Maintain technicality** - Argues this doesn't violate the original deal

### Bounty Specifications
**Targets**:
1. **Goblin raiders** - Public threat requiring response
2. **The Elf Woman** - Direct connection to the hag bargain

**Justification**: Only targeting the specific attack on the baron's keep, not broader goblin activities

## The Hags' Counter-Strategy

### Chaos Orchestration
**Primary Plan**: Use the original baron's heir to destabilize Hexton's rule

#### Strategic Elements
1. **Heir Preservation**: Hags plan for the original baron's heir to be "saved"
2. **Claim Chaos**: Heir's legitimate claim will create political turmoil
3. **Distraction Effect**: Political crisis will divert attention from goblin operations
4. **Military Opportunity**: Heir might muster an army to reclaim the barony
5. **Timing Advantage**: Strike while Hexton is away in [[Arendur]]

### Hag Benefits
- **Political chaos** provides supernatural sustenance/power
- **Distracted authorities** allow continued goblin operations
- **Hexton's downfall** potentially releases them from bargain obligations
- **Regional instability** serves broader chaotic agenda
