# The Black Lilly Knight order
A former mercenary band, [[Ironhouse]], now made a knight order by the new Baron of Bedegaarn, [[Regent Hexton]].

Their headquarters is located in [[Bedegaarn]] in the [[starchamber]] keep.

The knight order is controlled by [[mind-killers]], implanted by Hexton.
