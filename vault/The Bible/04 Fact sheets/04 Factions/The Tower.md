[[Blue Guardians]] is the guild residing in the old wizard tower of [[Arendur]] 

People often refer to the tower when talking about the blue guardians. But the reference is misguided as The Tower is actually part of the Academy and the school for wizardry. It is however run by the blue guardians guild.
