# Faction Pyramid
**Level one**: **Stormshadow** is the boss ogre warlock who through Ripper gathered the Red Hand horde and through his **Archfey** patron granted goblins warlock powers and mutations to the goblins.

**Level two**: **Grail**, the spirit of a dead wizard who has been advising Stormshadow in all his planning.   
**Ripper** the Ogre general responsiple for mustering the Red Hand Horde and putting Jawbreaker in charge as the Ogres are trying to hide their involvement.   
**Daughters of Meg** a coven of hags serving the Archfey patron, on the surface they are here to grants the goblins warlock powers and aid the general effort by also directing the powerful puppet of Meg \-\> **Sunkiller** who is more or less enslaved to do the evil work of MEG. Sunkiller is a former elven general bladesinger.

**Level three**: **Jawbreaker** is the Warmaster of the Red Hand Horde, he commands all the Warchiefs and the overall strategy of the invasion. He is dragonborn and has secret knowledge from the dragonwars. The secret weapon (Hatchery) is his brainchild.

**Level four**: **Slythe** is the favoured Warchief, he leads the Main horde.   
**Ulwai** is in charge of keeping the Ghostlord in check and on track.   
**Snikkter** used to be assigned to Ratbag, together they were in charge of setting up the forward base in Sunken Citadel, scouting the lands and installing spies. Snikkter is now answering directly to Jawbreaker as his *spymaster*.

**Level five**: Warchief **Saarvith** is entrusted with the Hatchery brainchild of Jawbreaker. He resides over this experiment in the ruins of Castle Blackspear. Each box in the project is another successfull spawning.  
**Ratbag** was promoted to Warchief after the raid on the dwarven mines 5 years ago. He was entrusted with establishing a forward operating post in the sunken citadel, scouting the lands and installing spies. He is now dead, killed by the Shadow company. The sunken citadel completely cleared out.  
**Redcap** has been sent to the eastern mountains to rally more troops, both regular goblinoids and summoning meg monsters.  
**Blacknose** has also been sent to the eastern mountains to rally more troops, regular mortals and let his warlocks summon meg monsters.  
**Rothorn** is a spy in Arendur, recruited by Snikkter and answering to him.
