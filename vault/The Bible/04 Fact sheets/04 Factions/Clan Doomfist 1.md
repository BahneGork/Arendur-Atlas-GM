# Clan Doomfist

The clan has taken over the ancient halls of [[Glitterhame]] and have recently agreed to help the [[red hand horde]] with unlocking the secrets of a stolen dwarven rune of power.

The Duegar also has agreed to supply the goblins with armor and weapons. 

The deals have been negotiated by Saarvith and Regarix.
