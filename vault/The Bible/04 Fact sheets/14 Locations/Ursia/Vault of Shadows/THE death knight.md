# Eric Stolz

The original Death Knight of Diamor.

Noone knows exactly how Eric Stolz fell from grace and became the death knight essence. Noone really knows that there is only one death knight, regardless of which fallen knight they face. 

Eric Stolz was among the very first solamnic knights to settle on Diamor. It is believed some demonlord or archdevil managed to sway him to betray his oaths.
	
Eric Stolz can also manifest as THE death knight.

The [[council of three]] fought and defeated Eric Stolz and a Necromancer over 200 years ago in a small village. 

Dread knights are lesser imitations of Eric Stolz, he has so far failed to create more of his kind.

During the dragonwars Eric Stolz  was petitioned by the cult of dragons to aid them in their efforts. A new knight order was particularly disruptive for them, the moon garde knights. Eric saw the oppertunity to exact revenge on the last remaining member of the [[council of three]], Worlock.

Eric Stolz having an affinity for sensing corruptible knights managed to corrupt Franz Griffin and with his aid the Moon garde was led into an ambush. Something was given to Franz as payment for this betrayal [[Franz Griffins medallion]]

But even worse was his next mission, the capture and corruption of Thunderclap. 

Eric Stolz empowered **[[jawbreaker]]** to earn his nickname, [[jawbreaker]] killed Thunderclaps silver dragon mate and one of her horns was imbued with Necrotic power and presented by Franz to Thunderclap before the truth of his betrayal was revealed.
