The mentions of Worlock and Kiri-jolith are false, to ward of any doo gooders from opening the vault.

## Vault Door

An octagon with 2 other engraved octagon lines inside, it is a double door that now stand ajar slightly, it can be pushed open. At the base of the door, lies a pool of gold. The doors are 6 feet thick, 10 feet wide each and 10 feet tall.

        The gold has been in the engraved octagon lines in the stone doors and was the magical seal keeping the door locked. The magical energies from Erythnul’s punishment for the failed ritual disrupted the magic holding the seal.

        If the door is closed the seal will reform in the grooves of the door, sealing it shut. It will not be opened by KNOCK or any other means than a wish spell. The vault has steel in its walls, as well as the entire vault is unhallowed with the effect of interrupting extradimensional travelling inside.

## Ante Chamber

The first chamber is an octagon shaped chamber, on the western wall is a door similar to the one that was open. This door is sealed and locked with gold filled grooves. The center of the door has a big triangular hole, ca 30cm on each side and a full arms length in depth. The hole is dark and does not go all the way through the door. A wish spell or the triangular key are the only things that can open the door, a wish spell only works for 24 hours after that the door re-seals itself.

## Vault Chamber

Another octagon chamber, all the walls are covered in magical runes warding off the chamber from all kinds of scrying, teleporting, passwall etc

        In the center of the chamber is an octagon shaped plateau with another smaller plateau ontop of it. At the top of the plateau is a pedestal, topped with a metal holder upon which is placed a  2½ft long rod, decorated in black and gold and capped with a golden hawks head.

(Rod)History DC 20: A Count von Kempf who tried to rebel and separate his land from the kingdom of Ursia, many ages ago. His family black and gold standard had the words “I do the devil’s work” underneath a hawks head. He amassed great strength of troops before he began claiming he was king of his country and Ursia had no claims or rights or law there. He was publically executed for sedition.

(Count von Kempf) History DC 25  Apparently he had much of the populace with him. There were odd savage rumours about him involving blood drinking. ## History of the Vault

The 3 triangles are the symbol of a steel knight who was manipulated and eventually turned to evil. This was during the dragon wars, there are no records that detail exactly what happened, only that he used to be a true honorable goodly knight. He was last seen in the wars wearing black armor and gazing at his enemies with eyes of fire, during the wars he earned the name “Death Knight”. His legend grew and told how he was no longer a living mortal man, but consumed completely by evil.
