A cyclops has taken residence in caves at the top of Dead Mountain.

He is a simple being, set on plunder.

When THE necromancer came across his domain and offered his services to him, he took him up on it. 
[[THE necromancer]] created [[skeletons]], [[zombies]] and other undeads for the Cyclops, took his payment and went on his way.

There was something in the mountain the necromancer was looking for, the Cyclops had it and traded it for his minions.

## Cyclops Necro-Tyrant - CR 8 (3,900 XP) - FINAL BOSS
**AC** 16 | **HP** 138 | **Speed** 30 ft  
**STR** 22 | **DEX** 11 | **CON** 20 | **INT** 8 | **WIS** 6 | **CHA** 10

**Senses** passive Perception 8

**Actions:**
- **Multiattack**: Two slam attacks
- **Slam**: +10 to hit, reach 10 ft, 4d8+6 bludgeoning + 1d6 necrotic damage. On critical hit: target knocked prone, DC 18 Str save or pushed 10 feet
- **Corpse Bolt** (Recharge 5-6): Ranged spell attack, +7 to hit, range 60 ft, 8d6 necrotic damage in 10-foot radius. DC 15 Dex save for half damage
- **Reanimation Command** (Bonus Action): One destroyed undead within sight rises again with 50% HP

**Equipment:**
- **Javelin of Lightning**: +10 to hit, range 30/120 ft, 1d6+6 piercing + 4d6 lightning (10-foot wide line, DC 13 Dex save for half)
- **Amulet of Necromantic Tyranny**: Source of undead control. If destroyed or removed, all controlled undead turn to dust

**Features:**
- **Necrotic Miasma**: 30-foot aura. Creatures starting turn in aura make DC 15 Con save or take 7 (2d6) necrotic damage
- **Poor Depth Perception**: Disadvantage on attack rolls against targets more than 30 feet away

**Tactical Notes:**
- Focuses on controlling undead minions while dealing massive area damage
- Vulnerable if amulet is targeted or removed
- Prefers to hang back and use Corpse Bolt, closes for melee when threatened
