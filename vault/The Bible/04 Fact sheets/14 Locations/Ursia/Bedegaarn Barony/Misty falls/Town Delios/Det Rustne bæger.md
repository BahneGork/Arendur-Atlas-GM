The Rusty Goblet: A cozy tavern/inn in the heart of Delios known for its friendly atmosphere, 
affordable accommodations, and delicious food. The rustic establishment features a bustling 
tavern area with a well-stocked bar and a spacious dining hall offering hearty meals. Upstairs, 
comfortable and reasonably priced rooms provide a restful stay for weary travelers. 
The inn also has a common room with a fireplace and various amenities for guests to enjoy. 
The staff is welcoming, and additional services such as horse stables and travel assistance are available. 
The Rusty Goblet is a warm haven for travelers seeking comfort and good company in Delios.

Modest lifestyle: 1gp (food, room)
Run by cheerful and boisterous halfling Glimmerleaf Underbottle
