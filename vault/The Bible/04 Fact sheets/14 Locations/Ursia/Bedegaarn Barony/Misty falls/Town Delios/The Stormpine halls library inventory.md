# Booklist
- "The Chronicles of Eldergrove" - A comprehensive historical account of the ancient elven civilization and their connection to the enchanted groves.
- "The Lumberlord's Legacy" - A meticulously detailed record of the founding and growth of the town, chronicling the rise of the ranchers turned gentlemen of influence.
- "Arcane Arboriculture" - A rare tome delving into the mystical properties of various trees and their magical applications.
- "The Art of Woodcarving" - A beautifully illustrated guide exploring the intricate techniques and designs of woodcarving, showcasing masterpieces from renowned craftsmen.
- "The Secrets of Silverleaf Society" - A confidential volume exclusively accessible to Stormpine Hall members, documenting the clandestine activities and influential figures associated with the renowned society.
- "The Dragon's Whisper" - A legendary scroll rumored to contain ancient draconic wisdom and powerful incantations, guarded under lock and key in the most secure section of the collection.
- "Whimsical Woods: Fables and Folklore" - A collection of enchanting tales and legends passed down through generations, showcasing the magical wonders and mythical creatures dwelling within the woods.
- "The Compendium of Forest Beasts" - An extensive reference guide detailing the flora and fauna of the surrounding forests, including rare and magical creatures that roam the wilderness.
- "The Code of Stormpine" - A leather-bound book containing the official rules, protocols, and traditions of Stormpine Hall, ensuring proper conduct and decorum within the establishment.
- "The Lost Script of Celestialpine" - A partially deciphered ancient script believed to hold celestial knowledge, eagerly studied by scholars and sages within the club's scholarly circle.
- "The Rancher's Handbook" - A practical guide for aspiring ranchers, covering various aspects of livestock management, timber harvesting, and sustainable ranching practices.
- "The Gentleman's Etiquette Compendium" - A comprehensive manual of proper manners, etiquette, and refined social behavior, serving as a guide for members of Stormpine Hall.
- "Mysteries of the Thornbark Grove" - A compilation of accounts and investigations into the hidden magical anomalies and phenomena occurring within the mystical Thornbark Grove.
- "The Silverhalls Family Saga" - A genealogical record tracing the lineage and history of the prominent Silverhalls family, known for their influence in both the lumber industry and Stormpine Hall.
- "The Whispering Winds: Poems and Prose" - A collection of eloquent poetry and literature inspired by the enchanting beauty of nature, written by renowned bards and authors throughout the ages.
- "Misty Falls: A History" - A comprehensive account detailing the origins of Misty Falls, chronicling its establishment as a prosperous settlement built around the breathtaking waterfall that shrouds the area in a perpetual mist.
- "Bedegaarn: Tales of the Barony" - An anthology of stories and legends surrounding the Barony of Bedegaarn, exploring the rise and fall of noble houses, political intrigues, and notable events that have shaped the region's history.
- "The Enigmatic Blackwood: Secrets and Shadows" - An in-depth exploration of the Blackwood, a dense forest where the ranchers of Misty Falls harvest timber. This book unravels the mysteries and dark folklore associated with the ancient woods, including tales of hidden treasures, mythical creatures, and encounters with powerful enchantments.
- "The Lumber Trade of Misty Falls" - A detailed account of the lumber industry in Misty Falls, chronicling the town's transformation from a modest logging camp to a thriving hub of lumber production. It delves into the techniques, challenges, and economic impact of the timber trade, highlighting the influential role of the ranchers-turned-gentlemen in shaping the industry.
- "Legends of the Misty Falls Waterfall" - A collection of enchanting legends and folklore surrounding the majestic Misty Falls waterfall. It includes stories of lost treasures hidden within its cascading waters, haunting melodies said to be sung by water spirits, and the ancient prophecy associated with the mist that veils the falls.
- "The Battle of the Blackwood: Conflict and Conquest" - An account of a historic conflict that occurred within the Blackwood, chronicling a significant battle or territorial dispute that shaped the region's political landscape. It explores the rivalries, strategies, and aftermath of the conflict.
- "Misty Falls' Notable Figures" - A biographical compilation highlighting the influential figures who have played pivotal roles in the development of Misty Falls and the Barony of Bedegaarn. It includes profiles of prominent ranchers, local leaders, and notable personalities who have left their mark on the region's history.
- "The Spirits of Blackwood: Guardians or Harbingers?" - A study of the mystical aspects of the Blackwood, examining the beliefs and practices of the local inhabitants regarding the forest's spiritual significance. It explores tales of guardian spirits, nature deities, and rituals observed by those who revere and respect the woods.
