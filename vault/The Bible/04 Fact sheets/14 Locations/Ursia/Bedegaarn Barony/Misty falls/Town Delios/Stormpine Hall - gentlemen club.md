# Club Description
The club occupies a grand three-story building constructed from sturdy timber,
adorned with intricate carvings that depict scenes of lumbering and ranching. 
The entrance to Stormpine Hall is marked by a large, ornate wooden door, polished to a rich sheen.


Upon entering, guests find themselves in a welcoming **foyer** that exudes a refined rustic charm. 
The foyer features high ceilings with exposed wooden beams and walls adorned with portraits of the club's founding members, showcasing their accomplishments in the lumber industry. 
A massive stone fireplace dominates one wall, crackling with warmth during colder months.

From the foyer, a grand staircase with a luxuriously carpeted runner ascends to the upper floors, offering access to the various rooms and amenities of the club. On the ground floor, several interconnected spaces cater to different aspects of socialization and relaxation.

To the right of the foyer is the Lounge, a cozy yet expansive area with plush leather armchairs and sofas arranged around low tables. The air carries the scent of aged leather and fine cigars. The Lounge is perfect for engaging conversations, card games, and enjoying drinks served by attentive staff from a well-stocked bar.

To the left of the foyer is the Library, a haven of knowledge and tranquility. Tall bookshelves line the walls, filled with a vast collection of books, scrolls, and tomes on various subjects. Comfy reading chairs and reading lamps invite members to immerse themselves in quiet study or to lose themselves in the pages of adventure and knowledge.

Beyond the Lounge and Library, a wide hallway leads to a large Dining Hall. The hall features long, polished wooden tables with comfortable chairs, capable of accommodating the esteemed members and their guests. Exquisite chandeliers cast a warm glow over the room, while a roaring fireplace at one end adds to the inviting ambiance. Here, delectable meals are served by attentive staff, offering a blend of local delicacies and exotic fare.

Ascending the grand staircase to the second floor, members discover a collection of private rooms and chambers, each tastefully decorated with comfortable furnishings, luxurious amenities, and personalized touches. These rooms provide a retreat for intimate conversations, private meetings, or relaxation.

The third floor houses the prestigious Ballroom, a magnificent space with a vaulted ceiling adorned with intricate wooden carvings depicting scenes of nature and grandeur. The expansive dance floor is flanked by balconies overlooking the room, offering a perfect vantage point for musicians or esteemed guests of honor. The Ballroom is often used for grand celebrations, formal gatherings, and lively entertainment.

Throughout Stormpine Hall, the décor blends the rustic charm of the lumber industry with the sophistication of an elite gentlemen's club. Warm wood tones, elegant furnishings, and carefully curated artwork create an atmosphere of refinement and camaraderie. The club's layout reflects a balance between public spaces for socializing and private areas for contemplation or exclusive events, catering to the needs and preferences of its esteemed members.
