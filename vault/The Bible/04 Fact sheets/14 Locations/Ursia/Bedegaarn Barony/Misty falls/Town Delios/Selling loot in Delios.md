# Market Limits

Delios is a town, population in the hundreds.

Rarity purchase limits
Trade goods max: Very Rare 
Gemstones: Rare - but can check with [[Lord Vermil]] for purchase of higher quality. ([[Jago's Jewel]] )
Art objects: Uncommon - higher go to Bedegaarn (nearest)

Delios shops n market can only has 18.000 in ready cash to buy loot from the party.
Delios shops n market only has items with value up to 800gp.
**Spells up to 3rd level** - 500gp - 1 workweek (5 days) if no scroll is available atm. common & uncommon.

## Selling & Buying Magic Items
2 brokers are located in Delios, one is employed at a small auction house in Bedegaarn. The other is employed by [[Lord Vermil]]. The auction house is no secret employer but the Lord's broker will claim to make deals on the behalf of a private collector who wishes to remain anonymous.

The Auction house can afford common and uncommon items.
[[Lord Vermil]]'s broker will make deals on common, uncommon and rare items. Anything rarer he will have to confer with his buyer first.
