# Brainstorm - Training and Networking in [[Delios]]
#anton #cedric #brumor #frekka 

 [[Highlights for theangrygm.com-What Training Looks Like and Why It Matters]] , [[Delios MOC]] , [[Benefits of Training with someone - angryway]] , [[Delios possible trainers]]

Training facilities in Delios where characters can enhance their skills and abilities.
- A retired scholar who offers academic training in history, philosophy, and math.
- A merchant who collects books and texts for sale and offers academic training.
- A scholar hired as a teacher for the children at Ranch Beltath, offering language, history, and basic math skills training.
- Woodriders who offer martial training in shooting, tracking, and sparring.
- Lumberjacks who offer strength training.
- Pit fights in the criminal underground for martial training.
- Drill sessions with the ClickClaw watch or the Shadow Company for martial training.
- A veteran of war who settled in town and offers weapon training.
- Networking opportunities with the Woodriders or a town elder with political experience.
- Izzy the bard who knows many things and can provide networking opportunities.
- Drunken lumberjacks with tales to share for networking opportunities.
- A halfling lass interested in setting up a food place to help the poor offers networking opportunities.
- Hedge wizards or herbalists offering practical training in spellcasting or herbalism.
- Hunters who offer practical training in procuring meat for the town.
- Morgu the carpenter and Mikael the blacksmith offering practical training in their respective trades.
- An alchemist at Ranch Falca offering practical alchemical training. 
- The St. Gaed shrine where a priest offers ritual training. 
- A hermit on the other side of the lake who practices shamanistic/druidic rituals and offers ritualistic training. 
- The old offering stone with icons of saints where characters can engage in their own rituals or meditations.
