# Hexton Regent of Bedegaarn
Main:
Related: [[Bedegaarn Barony Region Sheet]]

Lord Hexton was the Lord Marshall of Bedegaarn.
He was envious of the power of the baron and believed himself more fit to rule. #secretsnclues 

On a trip he was approached by a woman, offering to read his future and through her card reading he was offered a deal, to claim power for himself. Unlock the keep and let [[Sunkiller]] and her goblins inside to kill the baron and his family. #secretsnclues 

In return, he must ignore the goblin army and secure the western border. #secretsnclues
