"The Dusty Tome" is a renowned bookshop in Delios, run by the son of a retired professor. 

Run and owned by [[Agusto Lalonde]] son of [[Marcio Lalonde]] a former professor in Arendur 

It has a rich history of acquiring rare and forgotten books from travelers, peddlers, antiquity hunters, and estate cleanouts. 
Its labyrinthine interior houses a diverse collection of ancient tomes, spell scrolls, bestiaries, and journals of legendary adventurers. 
The knowledgeable shopkeeper curates a fascinating inventory, sharing stories and hosting events that attract scholars and adventurers alike. 

It's a treasure trove where one can uncover forgotten secrets and embark on intellectual journeys through the realms.
