# Notable History
## War of the Tusks
The barony fought a messy and dirty war against [[The white tusk tribe]], blue skinned Orcs, roughly 70 years ago. The current [[Duke Armatage Volantis]] , The black eagle knight himself led the armies against the "savages". 

*Altered timeline: There is a story that the Duke would have died in an ambush if it hadn't been for the intervention of [[The Shadow company]].*

The Orc raids were eventually halted and the Duke managed to track down the Orc warboss  and defeat him, deflating the orc invasions gusto.
*In the unaltered timeline, the Duke was killed before he could defeat the warboss and it was instead a band of heroic sellswords that defeated the orc warboss.*

The Duke bestowed one of his warboss trophies upon the current Baron, [[The Sanguineous Oriflamme]], which to this day still remains in [[the Bedegaarn Keep]].

The war taxed the barony dearly and there has been a deep animosity for Orcs in the barony ever since.
