# Dragon's Breath
This tobacco has a fiery kick, with a blend of spices that tickle the senses. It offers a bold and invigorating experience.

typical cost is 20gp for 4 ounce

# Ingredients
1. Fireleaf: A rare and fiery plant known for its intense heat and smoky flavor.
2. Dragon's Tongue Herb: A mystical herb that adds a hint of spice and potency to the blend.
3. Emberroot: A root with ember-like properties, giving the tobacco a smoldering effect.
4. Ashenwood Bark: The bark of a special tree that infuses the tobacco with a smoky aroma.
5. Inferno Pepper: A rare and extremely spicy pepper that adds a fiery kick to the blend.
6. Scorched Moss: A type of moss that grows around volcanic regions, lending a unique earthy and smoky flavor.
7. Cinderpetal Flower: A vibrant red flower that releases a burst of heat when burned.
8. Smoldering Embergrain: A special grain that has been charred and adds a smoky undertone to the tobacco.
