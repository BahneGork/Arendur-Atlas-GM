# Duchies
Each major house in Arendur & Kvalec is also the seat of a duke, it is a hereditary title.
Each duchy consists of multiple baronies, which can be scattered or lie side by side.

# Arendur
Arendur consist of seveal districts and most districts belong to the throne (yellow) and some belong to the dukes and some belong to guilds. This is mainly determined by who controls which "powernodes" (sort of like a game of risk).

Presently Arendur has 4 major noble houses 
Alvares
Navarre 
Omero
Volantis
