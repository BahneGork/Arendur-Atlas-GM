# Summary
- After fighting a group of stone crabs, [[Bartolo the guide]] pointed out the destination.
- 3 paths: wolves by a dead tree (west), stone door entrance in the middle of the hill, strange megalith collection (east)
- The heroes chose straight ahead in the middle of the hill, the stone door.
- magical darkness awaited on the other side of the entrance, along with two flight of stairs and a shadowooze.
- The first room contained a spire buzzing with lightning and stepping on plates in the room sent lightning bolts into you.
- [[Frekka Flammehår|Frekka]] smashed the spire into bits.
- All light sources (including magical ones) had been extinguished when they came out of the magical darkness.
- [[Brumor stålfod|brumor]] recognized dwarven design, but something was strange about it. Old runes, both in use and the age of the carvings, combined with modern language. Some sentence structure was also off/wrong and on few occassions nonsense.
- The layout of the place did not seem particular dwarven to Brumor, no typical dwarven architectual design choices.
- One door had the words "the heroes of Glitterhame".
	- 6 megaliths each with a complicated carving that hummed with a faint blue light and a soft ringing.
	- 5 sarcophagi with name plates matching names carved on the megaliths
	- various ancestry people carved into the lids, no dwarves. Feline features, Tiefling, human, human, human.
	- 6th megalith was not glowing
	- none of the megaliths had any dwarven craft about them, if this was dwarven the style and craft was something Brumor had never seen, read or heard about.
- Down the stairs, underneath,  there was a pit trap with a gelatinous cube had a strange octagon rod with a red rounded end and a few dusin pieces of gold.
- in the room next to the pit trap, was a shallow pool of water that hid a black pudding an a golden arcane ritual circle, that Anton might be able to work with.
- Strange Bars blocking many doorways.
- Large tomb chamber, with a big statue located inside, was behind some of the strange bars.
- a hall with a pillar of bones with a huge skull containing 3 eye sockets, that blasted a ball of lightning into the room whenever someone moved in it. [[Frekka Flammehår|Frekka]] smashed it.
- another hall with floating armors and a frozen orb on a pillar, Anton's familiar died from frost dmg touching the orb.
- Some stones on a wall became liquid or soft before their eyes.
- a large blob of melted flesh with hundreds eyes and mouths and some ghouls attacked.
- A large orrey with strange warped crystal celestial bodies in another hall.
- A shrine of "strange" causing madness.
- Short rest in "the heroes of Glitterhame" hall.
