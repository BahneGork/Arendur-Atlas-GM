#summary #session41 

Entering an invisible sanctum that had a completely different architecture, our heroes meet the halfling Pippa. A cleric of the golden spear.

They learn the mind controlled adventurerers were her companions, that came here looking for a kidnapped girl.

A marble golem appeared, claiming the mother has requested the attendence of the 4.

Agreeing, but trying to bring Pippa and the dead girl, the heroes are teleported to the pearl fortress.

Here they meet bitter sweet lovers anticipation who asks them to not destroy the stone but instead the raven sword.

The heroes refuse and Pippa is turned into a marble golem in eternal servitude to the star elf.

The heroes defeat a weird energy in the abstract form of a dragon.

The raven sword is released when Cedric plunges the golden avenger into the Pearl Heart.
