# Heksenes Priser - Hag Bargaining Guide

**Created**: 2025-10-03
**Purpose**: Quick reference for hag bargains and prices in Danish (H.C. Andersen style) and English
**Campaign**: The Arendur Rising Campaign
**Usage**: When Daughters of Meg or other hags negotiate with players

---

## The Prisoner Exchange - Four Prices

> *"Four prisoners require four prices:"*

### **From Cedric (Devil-Pacted Warrior):**
**"Your last dying breath"**
- *"Din sidste døende ånde"*
- **Mechanic**: Permanently lose 1 death save (fails at 2 instead of 3)
- **Purpose**: Devil-corrupted warrior's proximity to death has power for breaking celestial bindings

### **From Brumor (The Resurrected):**
**"A drop of blood from one who has crossed the threshold"**
- *"En dråbe blod fra én, der har krydset tærsklen"*
- **Purpose**: Blood of one who died and returned breaks life/death magic in seal
- **Drama**: Brumor doesn't know he died and was resurrected by Cedric's Asmodeus pact

### **From Anton (Time Weaver):**
**"A drop of blood from a time weaver"**
- *"En dråbe blod fra en tidsvæver"*
- **Purpose**: Time wizard's essence corrupts temporal wards in ancient seal

### **From Frekka (Archfey's Child):**
**"A drop of blood from archfey's get"**
- *"En dråbe blod fra alfefyrsten født"*
- **Purpose**: Archfey blood corrupts fey/nature magic bindings of Tree of Life
- **Drama**: May reveal Frekka's heritage if unknown

**Hidden Purpose**: All four components target different aspects of the Hag Queen's seal beneath the Tree of Life in Mercadia - celestial (Cedric), life/death (Brumor), temporal (Anton), and fey/nature (Frekka) magic.

## Heksenes Priser - En Poetisk Fortegnelse

*Når eventyrere forhandler om tjenester, gunst og viden hinsides fangernes udveksling*

### **Første Grad: Småtjenester og Simple Sandheder**
*Ubetydelige til synes, dog med ranker dybt i morgen*

- **"En lok af håret fra den, du holder kær"** (sympathetisk magi)
- **"Dit navn, skrevet med dit eget hjerteblod"** (skaber binding)
- **"Spyt tre gange i denne skål af ben"** (essens-indsamling)
- **"Betro os en hemmelighed, som ingen anden kender"** (viden og løftestang)
- **"Bær dette budskab til [sted], og stil ej spørgsmål"** (ubevidst ritual)
- **"Plant dette agern, hvor vi byder dig"** (fordærvelsens frø)
- **"Tal disse tre ord ved morgengry i morgen"** (forsinket besværgelse)
- **"Bring os noget af jern fra et helligt sted"** (vanhelligelse ved stedfortræder)
- **"En negl, revet af - ej klippet"** (smertefuld men mulig)
- **"Navnet på din kæreste ven"** (mål for fremtidige rænker)

### **Anden Grad: Betydelige Tjenester og Værdifuld Viden**
*Virkelig omkostning, dog tilsyneladende retfærdig handel*

- **"Et år og en dag skal sandhed herske på dine læber - ingen løgn må du tale"** (adfærds-forbandelse)
- **"Dit spejlbillede - aldrig mer' skal du se dig selv i blanke flader"** (fe-pris)
- **"Mindet om dit første kys skal visne bort"** (stjålen følelsesmæssig essens)
- **"Tre tjenester, unavngivne, når vi kalder på dig"** (blank check)
- **"En tand, frivilligt givet"** (permanent fysisk offer)
- **"Begrav denne æske, hvor vi befaler - spørg ej hvad den gemmer"** (blind tjeneste)
- **"Din skygge ved midnatstide"** (bogstavelig skygge-adskillelse)
- **"Overgiv vor gave til [person] og vær vidne til dens åbning"** (vidne til forbandelse)
- **"Sov én nat i vort leje, drømmende vore drømme"** (mental forurening)
- **"En flaske tårer, grædt af ægte sorg"** (følelsesmæssig høst)

### **Tredje Grad: Store Tjenester og Farlige Hemmeligheder**
*Alvorlige konsekvenser for livsvigtig viden*

- **"Din evne til at elske - aldrig mer' skal varme fylde dit bryst"** (følelsesmæssig adskillelse)
- **"Syv års tjeneste, én nat ved hver fuld måne"** (langvarig binding)
- **"Noget uskyldigt skal dø ved din hånd"** (moralsk fordærvelse)
- **"Dit førstefødte barns navn, når det når myndighed"** (generationskrav)
- **"Svig én som stoler på dig, og bring os beviset"** (aktiv fordærvelse)
- **"Din stemme - aldrig mer' skal ord forlade dine læber"** (permanent handikap)
- **"Ét minde efter dit valg - fortabt for evigt"** (selvpåført hukommelsestab)
- **"Ti år af dit livs spind"** (temporal tyveri)
- **"Din største skam, fortalt med høj røst for alle at høre"** (ødelæggende åbenbaring)

### **Eskalerende Handel**
*For vedholdende forhandlere - hver handel gør den næste værre*

**Første tjeneste:** "Blot bring os denne svamp fra sumpen dyb"
**Anden tjeneste:** "Nu skal du bringe os et barns legetøj fra Delios' gader"
**Tredje tjeneste:** "Nu skal du bringe os det barn, som ejed' legen kær"

### **Betingede Priser**
*Udløst af fremtidige hændelser*

- **"Når næste gang du redder liv, skylder du os én død til gengæld"**
- **"Den første guldmønt du tjener ved heltedåd tilhører os"**
- **"Gifter du dig én gang, skal din viv tilbringe én nat hos os"**
- **"Får du nogensinde børn, det tredje er vort at navngive"**
- **"Når næste magt dig når, skal den komme gennem os"** (permanent forbindelse)

### **Listige Afslag der Fanger**
*Når spillere forsøger at afvise eller forhandle ned*

**Heksen:** "Åh, for dyrt? Så lovers blot at komme, når vi kalder - det koster da intet?"
*(Skaber forpligtelse uden definerede grænser)*

**Heksen:** "Ingen tjenester? Vel - så blot lad vær' med at hindre os, når vi virker i dit land."
*(Tilladelse til frit at operere)*

**Heksen:** "Behold dit blod da. Blot fortæl ingen, at du så os her i nat."
*(Tvungen tavshed om heksenes tilstedeværelse)*

- **"Den sang du sang som barn, før sorgen kom"**

## English Translation - Generic Hag Prices

### **Tier 1: Minor Favors/Simple Information**
*Seem trivial, hide long-term hooks*

- **"A lock of hair from someone you love"** (sympathetic magic leverage)
- **"Your name, written in your own blood"** (creates binding connection)
- **"Spit in this bowl three times"** (essence collection)
- **"Tell us a secret no one else knows"** (information + leverage)
- **"Carry this message to [location]"** (unwitting ritual trigger)
- **"Plant this acorn where we tell you"** (corruption seed)
- **"Speak these three words at dawn tomorrow"** (delayed ritual component)
- **"Bring us something iron from a holy place"** (desecration by proxy)
- **"A fingernail, pulled not cut"** (painful but doable)
- **"The name of your dearest friend"** (targeting leverage)

### **Tier 2: Moderate Favors/Valuable Information**
*Real cost, but seem fair trade*

- **"A year and a day of truthfulness - you cannot lie"** (behavioral curse)
- **"Your reflection - you'll cast none in mirrors"** (fey-style price)
- **"The memory of your first kiss"** (stolen emotional essence)
- **"Three tasks, unnamed, to be called upon later"** (blank check service)
- **"A tooth, willing given"** (permanent physical sacrifice)
- **"Bury this box where we command, ask not what's inside"** (blind service)
- **"Your shadow at midnight"** (literal shadow severing)
- **"Deliver our gift to [person] and watch them open it"** (witness to curse)
- **"Sleep one night in our lair, dreaming our dreams"** (mental contamination)
- **"A vial of tears, cried for true sorrow"** (emotional harvest)

### **Tier 3: Major Favors/Dangerous Secrets**
*Serious consequences, might seem worth it for critical information*

- **"Your ability to love - you'll feel affection no more"** (emotional severing)
- **"Seven years of service, one night per full moon"** (long-term binding)
- **"The life of something innocent, slain by your hand"** (moral corruption)
- **"Your firstborn's name, when they come of age"** (generational claim)
- **"Betray someone who trusts you, and bring us proof"** (active corruption)
- **"Your voice - you may never speak again"** (permanent disability)
- **"A memory of your choosing - gone forever"** (self-inflicted amnesia)
- **"Ten years from your lifespan"** (temporal theft)
- **"The truth of your greatest shame, told aloud"** (devastating revelation + leverage)

### **Escalating Bargains**
*For persistent negotiators - each deal makes the next worse*

**First favor:** "Just bring us this mushroom from the swamp"
**Second favor:** "Now bring us a child's toy from Delios"
**Third favor:** "Now bring us the child who owned that toy"

### **Conditional Prices**
*Triggered by future events*

- **"When next you save a life, you owe us a death"**
- **"The first gold coin you earn from heroism belongs to us"**
- **"If you ever marry, your spouse must spend one night with us"**
- **"Should you ever have children, the third is ours to name"**
- **"When you next level up, the power comes through us"** (permanent connection)

### **Clever Refusals That Trap**
*When players try to refuse or negotiate down*

**Hag:** "Oh, too steep? Then just promise to return if we call - surely that costs nothing?"
*(Creates obligation without defined limits)*

**Hag:** "No tasks? Fine - just don't interfere when we work our craft in your lands."
*(Permission to operate freely)*

**Hag:** "Keep your blood then. Just tell no one you saw us here."
*(Enforced secrecy about hag presence)*

## GM Notes

### **Hag Bargaining Philosophy**
- Hags always ask for slightly more than item/information is worth
- Prices often have hidden secondary purposes (sympathy links, future leverage, corruption)
- The more desperate the PC, the worse the deal
- Always include escape clauses that seem impossible (break true love's curse, etc.)

### **The Daughters of Meg Specifically**
- **Primary Motivation**: Free the Hag Queen imprisoned beneath Tree of Life in Mercadia
- **Long-term Strategy**: Gathering components over millennia to break ancient seal
- **Current Need**: Specific essences that are extraordinarily rare (devil-pact warrior, resurrected cleric, time mage, archfey-born)
- **Bargaining Style**: Grandmotherly facade, make it seem fair and helpful
- **Hidden Agenda**: Every bargain advances plan to corrupt/break the Tree of Life seal

### **Why Millennia of Failure?**
The seal beneath the Tree of Life requires breaking four types of ancient magic:
1. **Celestial/Divine bindings** (Cedric's devil-corrupted blood)
2. **Life/Death wards** (Brumor's resurrected blood)
3. **Temporal locks** (Anton's time magic essence)
4. **Fey/Nature magic** (Frekka's archfey blood)

This specific combination is nearly impossible to find - until now.
