# The death of a character in Arendur Rising 

- When a character begins rolling death saves, the player does so in secret. Nobody knows if a character is dead or alive until they interact with the body. 
- Death is not a punishment, it is a thing that happens 
- Death is a setback and it should cost the party, not just the player. 
- The player of the dead character gets a one-on-one with GM to discuss if resurrection is possible will they continue playing the character or prefer to start a new character? 
	- Does the player already have ideas about a new character? (helpful)

## what's next?
- Players, except the player with the dead character, has to decide what to do next? 
	- what is their plan for the current adventure?
	- how are they going to refill their roster? a direct deliberate action like returning to civilization or performing a ritual to raise the dead 
- If the party's plans leaves a player sidelined for what the GM considers an unacceptable length of time, GM will veto the action 
The dead characters player is essentially not playing anymore and has no say on what the party should do next.
