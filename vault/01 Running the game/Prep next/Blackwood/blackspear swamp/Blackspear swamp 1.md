# Swamp origins 
Before the dragon Regilarix settled into the Blackspear ruins, there was no swamp. The current swamp is not natural and Regilarix had help from the Hag coven - [[Daughters of Meg]] to create it. 

3 anchors exists in Castle Blackspear that serves to "spawn" a swamp with Blackspear as its center.

	- ## special resources
		- [ ] **The Peaceful Clan (Lizardfolk):** A specific camp on the eastern edge despises the Red Hand horde. If approached peacefully with gifts of iron weapons, these lizardfolk will provide a heavy dugout boat and safe passage through their territory.
		- [ ] **Marsh-Eel Grease:** The fat of giant marsh eels can be harvested and coated onto gear. This coating grants **Resistance to Acid damage against the very first time a creature is hit by a dragon's breath weapon** before the grease completely melts away.

	
	- ## shortcuts
		- [ ] **The Sunken Limestone Ridge:** An ancient, submerged stone highway sits just 1 foot beneath the mud. Following this precise path allows travelers to ignore Difficult Terrain while crossing the soggy outer forest hexes, cutting travel time to the castle in half.
		- [ ] **The Submerged Breach:** A giant underwater fissure cracked open the castle's subterranean foundation stone. Creatures utilizing *Water Breathing* can swim through the swamp directly into the C15 Cellar, bypassing the courtyard defenses entirely.

	
	- ## dangerous roads/trails
		- [ ] **The Sunken Alleys of Rhest:** The main waterways directly surrounding the ruins are heavily patrolled by goblin scouts on rafts. Entering through these channels triggers mandatory combat encounters or high-DC stealth tests.
