# Ozzyandor - "Ozzy the Green Dragon"

**Created**: 2025-01-30  
**Type**: Major Antagonist (Alliance Test)  
**CR**: 8 (Young Green Dragon)  
**Integration**: MCDM Kingdoms & Warfare Orchid Court Challenge

## Basic Profile

**Name**: Ozzyandor  
**Nickname**: "Ozzy" (used by former kobold keeper)  
**Species**: Young Green Dragon  
**Age**: Recently matured from wyrmling to young dragon  
**Alignment**: Lawful Evil  
**Size**: Large

## Background

### Origin Story
- **Former Status**: Pet dragon of Meepo the Dragonkeeper (kobold)
- **Liberation**: Kobold colony exterminated by Red Hand forces
- **Current Status**: Independent dragon claiming forest territory
- **Motivation**: Establishing dominance after years of servitude

### Connection to Campaign
- **Red Hand Link**: Freed when goblins destroyed kobold colony
- **Elven Conflict**: Claims territory overlapping with Tiri Kitor lands
- **Strategic Importance**: Must be dealt with for full elven alliance

## Dragon Personality

### Core Traits
- **Intelligent but Inexperienced**: Smart but lacks wisdom of older dragons
- **Resentful**: Bitter about years spent as kobold "pet"
- **Territorial**: Aggressively defends claimed forest domain
- **Cunning**: Prefers ambush and manipulation over direct confrontation

### Motivations
1. **Prove Independence**: Never be controlled again
2. **Establish Territory**: Build a proper dragon domain
3. **Gain Respect**: Force others to acknowledge his power
4. **Accumulate Wealth**: Build hoard worthy of true dragon

## Combat Statistics (Quick Reference)

**AC**: 18 (Natural Armor)  
**HP**: 136 (16d10 + 48)  
**Speed**: 40 ft., fly 80 ft., swim 40 ft.

**STR**: 19 (+4), **DEX**: 12 (+1), **CON**: 17 (+3)  
**INT**: 16 (+3), **WIS**: 13 (+1), **CHA**: 15 (+2)

**Key Abilities**:
- **Poison Breath** (Recharge 5-6): 45 ft. cone, DC 16 Con save, 42 (12d6) poison damage
- **Forest Corruption**: Can poison water sources and corrupt plant life
- **Ambush Tactics**: Uses forest cover for surprise attacks
