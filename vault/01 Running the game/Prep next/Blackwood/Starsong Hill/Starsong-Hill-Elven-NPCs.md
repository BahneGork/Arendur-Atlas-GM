# Starsong Hill - Key Elven NPCs

**Created**: 2025-01-30  
**Type**: NPC Reference Sheet  
**Location**: Tiri Kitor Stronghold  
**Context**: Alliance negotiations and encounter personalities

## Primary NPCs

### Killiar Arrowswift
**Role**: Elven Noble, Primary Negotiator  
**CR**: 5 (Elf Noble stat block)  
**Personality**: Ancient, wise, cautiously diplomatic

**Background**:
- **Age**: Over 800 years old, has seen multiple ages
- **Position**: Senior diplomat and military advisor
- **Experience**: Survived the Red Hand's initial assault on elven territories
- **Motivation**: Protect his people while seeking genuine allies

**Key Traits**:
- **Perceptive**: Immediately recognizes each character's supernatural nature
- **Cautious**: Slow to trust but capable of genuine friendship
- **Tactical**: Thinks in terms of long-term consequences
- **Honorable**: Keeps promises but expects same in return

**Speaking Style**:
- Formal but not cold
- Uses cosmic/immortal perspective in explanations
- Direct about consequences and expectations

### Illian Snowmantle  
**Role**: High Priest, Healer, Spiritual Advisor  
**CR**: 3 (Modified Priest stat block with elven traits)  
**Personality**: Compassionate but gravely concerned

**Background**:
- **Position**: Chief spiritual leader of Starsong Hill community
- **Expertise**: Divine magic, cosmic balance, healing arts
- **Concern**: Deeply troubled by celestial elf murder and cosmic implications
- **Role**: Available for healing and spiritual guidance

**Key Traits**:
- **Empathetic**: Understands the party's struggle with redemption
- **Knowledgeable**: Provides insight into cosmic balance and divine matters
- **Supportive**: Offers healing and sanctuary regardless of political decisions
- **Worried**: Fears cosmic consequences if balance isn't restored

### Captain Silvyr Moonwhisper
**Role**: Military Commander (Orchid Court Unit Leader)  
**CR**: 5 (Elf Noble with military focus)  
**Personality**: Professional warrior, honor-bound

**Background**:
- **Position**: Commands the Orchid Court Archers unit
- **Experience**: Veteran of forest warfare and anti-dragon tactics
- **Loyalty**: Devoted to protecting elven territories and allies
- **Availability**: Only appears if Stage 2 alliance achieved

**Key Traits**:
- **Disciplined**: Military precision in speech and action
- **Respectful**: Acknowledges proven warriors regardless of species
- **Strategic**: Thinks in battlefield terms and tactical advantages
- **Proud**: Takes pride in elven military traditions

## NPC Relationship Dynamics

### Killiar & Illian
- **Working Relationship**: Military leader and spiritual advisor
- **Complement**: Killiar handles politics, Illian handles moral/spiritual aspects
- **Agreement**: Both concerned about cosmic implications of celestial murder

### Killiar & Silvyr  
- **Chain of Command**: Killiar gives orders, Silvyr executes
- **Trust**: Long military partnership, mutual respect
- **Availability**: Silvyr only appears after dragon challenge completed

### Party Relationship Progression
**Initial**: Hostile due to celestial murder (disadvantage on social rolls)  
**Post-Redemption**: Neutral to friendly (normal social interactions)  
**Post-Dragon**: Respectful allies (advantage on future interactions)
