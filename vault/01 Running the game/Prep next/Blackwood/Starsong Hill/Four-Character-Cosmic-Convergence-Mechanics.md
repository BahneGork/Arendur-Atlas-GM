# Four-Character Cosmic Convergence Mechanics

**Created**: 2025-01-30  
**Type**: Campaign System  
**Context**: Starsong Hill Redemption Ritual  
**Integration**: Character abilities + cosmic themes

## Overview

The four-character convergence represents the combination of Time, Fey, Divine, and Infernal magical forces. When these elements work together, they can achieve cosmic-level effects including breaking infernal pacts, restoring planar balance, and bridging realm boundaries.

## Character Element Mapping

### Anton Keene - TIME (Chronurgy Magic)
**Cosmic Role**: Master of Causality and Temporal Flow  
**Element**: Understanding consequences across time
**Mechanical Representation**:
- Uses chronurgy spells to see temporal ripples
- Provides insight into cause-and-effect chains
- Can glimpse multiple timeline outcomes

### Frekka Flammehår - FEY (Planar Heritage)
**Cosmic Role**: Bridge Between Realms  
**Element**: Connection across planar boundaries
**Mechanical Representation**:
- Half-fey heritage allows planar communication
- Can reach across the veil to contact celestial essence
- Siofrah provides fey magic amplification

### Brumor Stålfod - DIVINE (Sacred Authority)
**Cosmic Role**: Representative of Lawful Good Order  
**Element**: Spiritual power to cleanse corruption
**Mechanical Representation**:
- War domain cleric with divine mandate
- Recent resurrection grants spiritual authority
- Silverbeard's blessing provides cleansing power

### Cedric - INFERNAL (Willing Sacrifice)
**Cosmic Role**: Corruption Bearer Seeking Redemption  
**Element**: Choice and sacrifice necessary for change
**Mechanical Representation**:
- Must genuinely desire redemption (not just tactical)
- Willingness to weaken himself to break pact
- Represents free will over cosmic predestination

## Convergence Ritual Mechanics

### Requirements for Success
All four elements must be present and working in harmony:

1. **Temporal Understanding** (Anton)
2. **Planar Bridge** (Frekka)  
3. **Divine Authority** (Brumor)
4. **Willing Sacrifice** (Cedric)

### Ritual Structure

#### Phase 1: Temporal Insight (Anton)
**Challenge**: DC 15 Arcana Check + Chronurgy Spell  
**Options**:
- *Divination* to see consequences of redemption
- *Fortune's Favor* to glimpse positive timeline
- Any chronurgy spell that reveals temporal information

**Success**: Reveals how redemption affects future timelines
**Failure**: Unclear consequences, +2 DC to subsequent phases

#### Phase 2: Planar Bridge (Frekka)  
**Challenge**: DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion) + Fey Heritage
**Requirements**:
- Must speak in Sylvan to reach across realms
- Siofrah assists, granting advantage
- Call to the murdered celestial's essence

**Success**: Makes contact with Bittersweet Lovers Anticipation's spirit
**Failure**: Connection fails, ritual cannot proceed

#### Phase 3: Divine Cleansing (Brumor)
**Challenge**: DC 15 Religion Check + Divine Magic
**Options**:
- *Dispel Evil and Good* (ideal choice)
- *Remove Curse* with divine focus
- Channel Divinity: War Domain blessing

**Success**: Divine power begins breaking infernal bonds
**Failure**: Corruption resists, Cedric takes 2d6 necrotic damage

#### Phase 4: Willing Sacrifice (Cedric)
**Challenge**: No dice roll - Pure role-play requirement
**Requirements**:
- Must genuinely express remorse for celestial murder
- Accept responsibility for cosmic wound
- Willingly sacrifice power to heal the damage

**Success**: Redemption achieved, pact partially broken
**Failure**: Ritual fails, corruption strengthens

### Convergence Effects

#### Total Success (All Four Phases Pass)
**Immediate Effects**:
- Xerathal pact reduced from permanent to attunement-based
- Cosmic wound partially healed (elven relations restored)
- All party members gain temporary cosmic insight

**Mechanical Benefits**:
- Cedric can break attunement to Xerathal (but keeps Emergency Pact)
- No disadvantage on future elven encounters
- Each character gains one-time cosmic blessing:
  - **Anton**: Advantage on next chronurgy spell
  - **Frekka**: Prophecy insight (DM provides hint about future)
  - **Brumor**: Divine favor (inspiration from Silverbeard)
  - **Cedric**: Temporary redemption (advantage on next saving throw)

#### Partial Success (3/4 Phases Pass)
**Effects**: Minor improvement but incomplete healing
- Cosmic wound remains but slightly diminished
- Elven relations improve to neutral
- Xerathal corruption reduced but not eliminated

#### Failure (2 or fewer phases pass)
**Effects**: Ritual backfires
- Cosmic wound worsens
- Xerathal corruption grows stronger
- All characters take 1d6 psychic damage from cosmic backlash

## DM Notes

### Pacing
- Allow time for each character to shine in their phase
- Build tension through escalating cosmic energy
- Make failure meaningful but not campaign-ending

### Flexibility
- If players suggest creative approaches, work them into the framework
- Allow substitution of spells/abilities that fit thematic role
- Emphasize collaboration over individual success

### Consequences
- Successful convergence should feel earned and meaningful
- Partial success creates ongoing tension and motivation
- Failure opens new story paths rather than closing them

### Visual/Narrative Cues
- Describe cosmic energy building as each phase completes
- Show elven observers recognizing the significance
- End with tangible change in Cedric's corruption aura
