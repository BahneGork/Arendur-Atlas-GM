# Ozzyandor's Lair - The Poisoned Grove

**Created**: 2025-01-30  
**Type**: Dragon Lair Encounter  
**Location**: Northern Blackwood, 5 miles from Starsong Hill  
**CR**: 8 encounter environment

## Overview

Ozzyandor has claimed an ancient elven grove, corrupting it with his poison breath and presence. The once-sacred site now serves as his territorial headquarters and treasure cache.

## Lair Description

### The Approach
- **Corrupted Forest**: Trees show signs of poison damage - yellowed leaves, blackened bark
- **Unnatural Silence**: Normal forest sounds fade as party approaches
- **Acidic Pools**: Small pools of greenish water dot the landscape
- **Warning Signs**: Territorial markings scraped into tree trunks

### Central Grove
- **Ancient Circle**: Ring of massive oak trees, now corrupted and twisted
- **Poison Fog**: Light green mist drifts through the area
- **Dragon's Perch**: Largest central tree has been hollowed for dragon to rest inside
- **Corrupted Spring**: Former sacred water source now bubbles with toxic liquid

### Tactical Features
- **Dense Canopy**: Limits flying movement, provides cover
- **Difficult Terrain**: Roots, poison pools, and debris slow movement
- **Concealment**: Heavy undergrowth allows for ambush tactics
- **Environmental Hazards**: Poison effects from corrupted vegetation

## Combat Encounter

### Dragon Tactics
1. **Ambush Start**: Attacks from concealment when party enters grove center
2. **Poison Breath**: Uses terrain to channel breath weapon effectively  
3. **Hit and Run**: Uses forest cover to break line of sight between attacks
4. **Environmental Leverage**: Drives enemies toward poison pools and hazards

### Lair Actions (Initiative 20)
- **Poison Spores**: All creatures in lair make DC 15 Con save or poisoned until end of next turn
- **Grasping Roots**: One creature restrained by corrupted vegetation until escape (DC 15 Athletics/Acrobatics)
- **Toxic Mist**: 20-foot radius area becomes heavily obscured until start of next turn

### Environmental Hazards
- **Poison Pools**: 5 feet deep, DC 15 Con save or 1d6 poison damage per turn
- **Corrupted Air**: After 10 minutes, DC 12 Con save or gain one level of exhaustion
- **Unstable Ground**: Certain areas collapse into hidden poison reservoirs

*Lair provides challenging but fair encounter for 9th level party.*
