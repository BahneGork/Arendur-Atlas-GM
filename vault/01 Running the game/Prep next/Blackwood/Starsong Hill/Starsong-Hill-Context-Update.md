## Major Plot Elements Established

### **Celestial Elf Murder Consequences**
- **Global Impact**: All elves across all planes permanently feel the death of "Bittersweet Lovers Anticipation"
- **Metaphysical Wound**: Eternal death-scream echoes through elven consciousness
- **Cosmic Balance Shift**: The murder strengthened Law's dominion over a Far Realm region
- **Campaign Integration**: This affects ALL future elven encounters, not just Starsong Hill

### **Xerathal/Asmodeus Pact Details**
**Two Separate Pacts Established**:
1. **Transformation Pact**: Level switching, oath transfer from Saint Elowen to Xerathal
2. **Emergency Pact**: Teleport power to save Brumor (cost: one level to Cedric)

**Pact Structure**:
- **Attunement Effects** (breakable): Cursed possession, sword powers, mental whispers
- **Soul Binding** (permanent): Level configuration, oath transfer, spiritual debt to Asmodeus
- **Breaking attunement** ≠ breaking soul binding

### **Redemption Path Framework**
- **No formal structure** (too lawful for chaotic elves)
- **Choice-based progression** - each moment offers freedom vs. certainty
- **Integrated with campaign** - main objectives serve redemption goals
- **Key stages**: Bahamut shrine → Dragon fight as test → Future cosmic consequences
