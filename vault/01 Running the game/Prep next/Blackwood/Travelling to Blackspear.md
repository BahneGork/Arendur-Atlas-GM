### Travelling to Blackspear
- [[Blackspear expedition]]
- The Travel Turn 
	- plot the route on hex map 
		- **Secret Wisdom(Survival) check from Navigator**
			- if they choose one of the 3 roads, it cannot fail. 
			- if they chose the trail from the ranch, it can fail. 
			- speed affects: slow=+5, normal=0, fast=-5
			- fails = lost. **1d6 hours in wrong direction** -> new **Wisdom(survival)** (rinse repeat)
				- When party realize they are lost, travel turn ends. Start a new one. 
			- Missing destionation? can they miss it? Passive perception checks vs DC 
				- if missed, continue in the travelling direction until "somethings not right"
		- Roll time pool once pr day, complications happen at random hours 
			- Travelling by night is RECKLESS - roll 4 tension dice 
				- roll 3 tension dice for any reckless actions during the day 
			- check passive perception for discovering encounters
