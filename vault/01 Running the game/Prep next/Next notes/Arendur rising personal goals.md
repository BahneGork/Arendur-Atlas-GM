#pc #personalgoals 
[[Goal oriented rewards]]  [[goals]] is about encouraging behavior, rewarding specific behavior. By visualising a specific goal be it individual or group goal and visualising that there is a reward for it, you present the goal as something actionable and a carrot/reward for doing it. 
	The idea is to encourage inquisitive behavior, to show players that it is allowed to be pro-active. Emerging behavior is the behavior that is rewarded.
