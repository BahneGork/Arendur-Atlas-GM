# World Reactions - Logical Responses to PC Actions

## Immediate Reactions (Current Sessions)

### Despotic Authority Figures

**Baron Hexton (Regent of Bedegaarn)**
- **Personality**: Despotic ruler who seized power, controls through fear and Black Lilly Knights
- **Awareness**: Shadow Company defended Delios during Dead Mountain attack, gaining local hero status
- **Goal**: Maintain control and legitimacy while negotiating with Duke Alvares
- **Reaction**: Views their heroism as both useful (proves his realm can be defended) and threatening (independent power base). Will attempt to co-opt them through official recognition before resorting to force.

**Lord Wolfgang Landor (Baron's Cousin)**
- **Personality**: Baron's voice in Delios, enforcing his authority
- **Awareness**: Shadow Company operating independently in "his" town
- **Goal**: Assert Baron's control while maintaining order
- **Reaction**: Will test their loyalty to Baron Hexton, offering rewards for compliance and veiled threats for independence.

### Corrupt/Mysterious Nobles

**Lord Vermil (Ancient Vampire)**
- **Personality**: Charming, mysterious descendant of Holtz family with hidden supernatural nature
- **Awareness**: Shadow Company fought undead effectively, may have supernatural connections (Cedric's sword)
- **Goal**: Maintain his vampire cover while expanding influence
- **Reaction**: Intrigued by their undead-fighting capability and supernatural artifacts. Will approach cautiously to determine if they're threats or potential assets to his schemes.

**Mace Martelli (Ranch Falca Patriarch)**
- **Personality**: Biggest political power in Delios, controls lumber rights
- **Awareness**: Emergency taxation threatens his family's wealth and power
- **Goal**: Resist Baron Hexton's illegal taxation while maintaining business operations
- **Reaction**: Views Shadow Company as potential allies against Baron Hexton's overreach. Will offer information and resources in exchange for protection from the Baron's forces.

### Intelligence Networks

**Lord Davide Castilla (Cautier School)**
- **Personality**: King's intelligence operative, pragmatic and calculating
- **Awareness**: Shadow Company captured Miha alive, proving competence and restraint
- **Goal**: Strengthen the crown's intelligence network against threats
- **Reaction**: Impressed by their professionalism. Will offer formal alliance with intelligence sharing, resources, and authority in exchange for continued cooperation against Red Hand and Baron Hexton.

**Bayard (Eyes of Many Headed Snake)**
- **Personality**: Professional spy loyal to the throne
- **Awareness**: Shadow Company proved effective against threats to the kingdom
- **Goal**: Monitor and potentially recruit useful assets for the spy network
- **Reaction**: Will establish ongoing contact, providing intelligence and testing their reliability with small tasks before offering deeper network access.

**Carlotta Baras (Blue Guardians)**
- **Personality**: Academic wizard focused on magical threats
- **Awareness**: Received intelligence about Red Hand magical operations
- **Goal**: Gather information about magical threats and protect the Academy
- **Reaction**: Grateful for intelligence provided. Will offer magical resources and Academy access in exchange for continued reports on supernatural threats.

### Criminal Elements

**The Abbesses (Hart House Operators)**
- **Personality**: Criminal organization from Arendur running kidnapping operation
- **Awareness**: Dead Mountain attack may have disrupted their operations or exposed vulnerabilities
- **Goal**: Maintain profitable criminal enterprise while avoiding detection
- **Reaction**: Suspicious of any heroes gaining influence in Delios. Will quietly investigate if Shadow Company poses a threat to their activities.

**The Orlocks (Local Gang)**
- **Personality**: Local "biker gang" with missing member TomTom
- **Awareness**: Shadow Company has reputation for finding missing people and solving problems
- **Goal**: Find their missing member and maintain gang solidarity
- **Reaction**: Will approach with a mixture of respect and desperation, offering payment and future favors for help finding TomTom.

### Religious Figures

**Father Belderone (Temple of Gaed)**
- **Personality**: Compassionate priest serving the community
- **Awareness**: Brumor runs soup kitchen and party fought undead effectively
- **Goal**: Serve his community and combat evil
- **Reaction**: Genuinely grateful for their charitable works and defense against undead. Will offer healing, sanctuary, and religious guidance without expecting payment.

### Merchants and Traders

**Ashley Seidel (Ravenscrest Emporium)**
- **Personality**: Business-focused merchant with generous offers
- **Awareness**: Shadow Company has valuable loot and growing reputation
- **Goal**: Establish profitable long-term business relationship
- **Reaction**: Will press for deeper business partnership, offering increasingly valuable services while subtly creating dependency on her resources.

**Robert Abrami (Ruby Throne in Delios)**
- **Personality**: Represents major merchant guild interests
- **Awareness**: Baron Hexton's illegal taxation threatens trade stability
- **Goal**: Protect guild interests and maintain profitable trade relationships
- **Reaction**: Views Shadow Company as potential allies against Baron Hexton's economic policies. Will offer guild resources and protection in exchange for intelligence about the Baron's activities.

## Developing Reactions (Future Sessions)

### Supernatural Entities

**The Bone Queen (Court of All Flesh)**
- **Personality**: Undead entity advocating chaos, helped party in Far Realm
- **Awareness**: Party used her assistance but hasn't acknowledged the debt
- **Goal**: Advance chaos and collect on favors owed
- **Reaction**: Growing impatient with lack of acknowledgment. Will send increasingly obvious undead messengers demanding recognition of their debt.

**Xerathal (Asmodeus-Bound Sword)**
- **Personality**: Devilish artifact with its own agenda
- **Awareness**: Bonded to Cedric through Asmodeus pact
- **Goal**: Corrupt Cedric while advancing infernal interests
- **Reaction**: Will whisper guidance that seems helpful but gradually pushes Cedric toward more ruthless, ends-justify-means decisions.

### Factional Leaders

**Duke Alvares (House Alvares)**
- **Personality**: Major noble house leader with king's intelligence connections
- **Awareness**: Shadow Company operates in region he's negotiating to influence
- **Goal**: Expand house influence while serving crown interests
- **Reaction**: Will evaluate them as potential assets or obstacles to his plans for the region. May offer recruitment or demand submission.

**Duchess Elvira Volantis (Acting House Head)**
- **Personality**: Refusing duties until missing husband returns, creating political crisis
- **Awareness**: Major political upheaval affecting the kingdom
- **Goal**: Find her husband and restore house stability
- **Reaction**: May approach Shadow Company as independent agents capable of investigating her husband's disappearance where official channels have failed.

### Criminal Organizations

**Kryx Bloodgnash (Charnel House Leader)**
- **Personality**: Mercenary leader with personal vendetta
- **Awareness**: Shadow Company defeated his lieutenant and repelled his attack
- **Goal**: Restore mercenary company's reputation and settle personal scores
- **Reaction**: Will escalate attacks, targeting Shadow Company's allies and resources to force a confrontation on his terms.

**"Mister" (Hart House Criminal Contact)**
- **Personality**: Monthly visitor managing kidnapping operations
- **Awareness**: May become aware if operations are disrupted
- **Goal**: Maintain profitable criminal enterprise
- **Reaction**: If operations are threatened, will investigate and potentially eliminate threats to maintain business security.

### Dwarven Networks

**Duerif Redblade (Ancient Axe Wielder)**
- **Personality**: Powerful dwarf with mysterious agenda
- **Awareness**: Another dwarf (Brumor) carries Silverbeard's mark
- **Goal**: Unknown, but likely involves ancient dwarven matters
- **Reaction**: Will test Brumor's worthiness and potentially recruit him for dwarven concerns that transcend human politics.

**Throg'was Dwarven Search Party**
- **Personality**: Secretive group seeking stolen rune of power
- **Awareness**: May mistake Brumor for one of their operatives
- **Goal**: Recover rune before humans discover its existence
- **Reaction**: Will approach with mixture of relief and suspicion, potentially drawing Shadow Company into dangerous dwarven politics.

## Long-Term Consequences

### Political Ramifications

**King of Ursia**
- **Personality**: Monarch facing political crisis with missing taxes and guild-noble tensions
- **Awareness**: Reports of effective independent operators in troubled regions
- **Goal**: Maintain royal authority while managing political crisis
- **Reaction**: May offer royal charter and authority in exchange for service, or demand submission to royal authority if they become too independent.

**Major Guild Leaders (Ruby Throne, etc.)**
- **Personality**: "New money" seeking political influence through economic power
- **Awareness**: Independent operators affecting regional stability
- **Goal**: Expand guild influence at nobles' expense
- **Reaction**: Will attempt to recruit or buy Shadow Company's services to advance guild interests against traditional nobility.

### Cult and Religious Orders

**Carthach ó Caskill (Loz Tdrot Cult Master)**
- **Personality**: Cult leader with otherworldly knowledge
- **Awareness**: Anton has provided valuable Far Realm intelligence
- **Goal**: Advance cult interests through forbidden knowledge
- **Reaction**: Will increase Anton's responsibilities and share more dangerous secrets, potentially putting him at risk from cult enemies.

**Silverbeard (Divine Entity)**
- **Personality**: Dwarven deity/saint figure
- **Awareness**: Brumor serves as Silver Sentinel
- **Goal**: Combat darkness through mortal champions
- **Reaction**: Will provide increasingly difficult divine missions testing Brumor's commitment to righteousness.

### Regional Powers

**Neighboring Barons and Lords**
- **Personality**: Various nobles watching Baron Hexton's situation
- **Awareness**: Independent force operating in Bedegaarn
- **Goal**: Advance their own territorial or political interests
- **Reaction**: Some will seek to recruit Shadow Company against Hexton, others will support Hexton to maintain baronial authority.
