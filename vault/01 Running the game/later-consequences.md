# Campaign Consequences - Later Implementation

## Major Story Beats & Environmental Changes

### Structural/Geographical Impact

**Destroyed Skullgorge Bridge**
- Significant infrastructure damage with regional economic/military consequences
- Trade route disruption affects multiple communities
- Military implications for troop movement and supply lines

**The Sunken Citadel Transformation**
- Completely cleared out, nature should be reclaiming it
- New creatures might move into the vacant complex
- Destroyed portal to Feydark prevented invasion - magical disturbance should attract druids, wizards, planar entities

**Blackbriar Monastery Consequences**
- Ancient Azarumme crypt opened, necromancer escaped
- Unleashed ancient evil should have regional ramifications
- The tragedy of the accidentally killed family should weigh on party

**Lady Dagger's Sealed Vault**
- Unopened, magically warded vault with zombie beholder beneath her caves
- Sealed by Kiri-Jolith's power and Saint Worlock's authority with warning about "evil kept away from the world"
- Bears two distinct markings: 3 triangles inside each other, and 6 mirrored letter T's across a long line with a circle around it
- No known keys or methods to open it - heavily protected by divine magic
- Contains unknown treasures or threats, but access method is a complete mystery

### Campaign-Wide Political Crisis

**The Arendur Political Meltdown**
- Duke Bartolo Volantis missing, taxes uncollected
- King selling noble titles to guilds
- Old nobles angry about "new money" gaining power
- Complete breakdown of traditional power structures
- Shadow Company's multiple faction ties should draw them into this crisis

**Academy Magic Shop Conversion**
- All magic shops became membership clubs
- Suggests wizards preparing for something major
- Changes how magical resources are obtained

**The Ruby Throne Double Agent**
- Infiltrator in Cautier School sowing discord
- Should create ongoing complications for Lord Davide
- Affects intelligence reliability
