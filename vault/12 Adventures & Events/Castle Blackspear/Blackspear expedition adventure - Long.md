>
>#### Minor encounter/obstacle
Costs 0.5 awards and reward
Small encounters with no need for minis or battlemat, speed bumps, just a couple of rolls.  
Quick to resolve, taking up little game time and space.  
Minor objectives are smaller goals, that either build up to the Major objective as intermediate steps or side quests or completely optional accomplishments the party may or may not discover along the way.  
Any difficulty  
>
>#### Major encounter/obstacle
Costs 1 awards and reward
Normal encounter, several die rolls and action passes.  
Use of miniatures and battlemats. Takes up a bit of time and space, there is only room for a few in a session.  
Any difficulty


# Session Structure

> Start with Motivation and build to a climax and resolution. 
> If the session is not the last session, then the climax and resolution are related to a Minor Objective. 
> 1 major objective - adventure resolution
> 2 minor objectives - session resolutions/optional goals
> 3 climactic encounters - one pr session
> 11 additional awards (objectives/encounters): minor=0,5 award or major=1 award
