### **Materials For Crafting the Lightning Rod Focus**

1. **Rare Metal (5 gp)**
    
    - **Material:** A refined **metal rod** made from **Silvered Copper** or **Electrum**, materials known for their affinity to electrical energy. This metal is used as the central shaft of the rod.
    - **Where to Find:** Can be bought from a reputable blacksmith or jeweler, or salvaged from ruined magical devices.
2. **Charged Crystal (15 gp)**
    
    - **Material:** A crystal infused with magical energy. **Quartz** or **Topaz** are ideal, but it must have been charged using an electrical or lightning-based enchantment.
    - **Where to Find:** These can be harvested from magical creatures or found in ancient ruins of magical importance, or purchased from a specialty mage's shop.
3. **Lightning Essence (10 gp)**
    
    - **Material:** **Essence of Lightning**, an alchemical substance created by distilling a magical lightning storm or harnessing the electrical charge of a natural thunderstorm.
    - **Where to Find:** Can be brewed by characters with **Alchemy Supplies proficiency**, requiring access to a storm or a location like the **Tower of Lightning** (or any area with heavy electrical activity). This could also be purchased from an alchemist specializing in rare reagents.
4. **Enchanted Wood (5 gp)**
    
    - **Material:** A small piece of **elm or ash wood** that has been lightly enchanted or imbued with natural elemental magic. This wood serves as the handle or grip of the focus and ensures stability and ease of handling for casting.
    - **Where to Find:** Commonly found in enchanted forests or purchased from woodworkers who deal with magical woods.
5. **Fine Silver Filigree (10 gp)**
    
    - **Material:** Delicate **silver filigree** used to inscribe runes or symbols of lightning upon the crystal and metal shaft of the rod. These filigree decorations help to channel and amplify the magic.
    - **Where to Find:** Can be crafted by a silversmith, or an artisan who specializes in fine jewelry or magical trinkets.
6. **Crafting Tools (5 gp)**
    
    - **Material:** A set of **Artisan’s Tools** (Tinker’s Tools or Smith’s Tools) required for shaping the metal, crystal, and wood, as well as inscribing the runes and making any necessary enchantments.
    - **Where to Find:** Can be found at any general artisan’s shop or used if the character has the proficiency in **Tinker’s Tools** or **Smith’s Tools**.

### **Crafting Process:**

- **Time Required:** 2 days (16 hours of crafting).
- **Skill Checks:**
    - **Arcana** (DC 15) to properly infuse the crystal with electrical energy.
    - **Smith’s Tools** (DC 14) to craft the metal rod and assemble the focus.
    - **Arcana or Alchemist’s Tools** (DC 15) to use the **Essence of Lightning** to activate the focus.

### **Final Product:**

- **Lightning Rod Focus**: Once completed, the focus allows the wielder to deal an additional +1 damage die (1d6) for any lightning-based spell cast through it.
