(1st) second floor has a 15foot wide hole in the eastern wall 
- [[Athletics skill]] DC 14 or [[Acrobatics skill]] DC 15  to climb 
(2nd) third floor has a hole in the wall on the wester side, narrower than the one below, small or tiny size

- [[Athletics skill]] DC 16 
(4th) upper floor has no roof 



#### **Ground Floor: The Arcane Vestibule**

- **Purpose**: A reception hall and study.
- - **Entrance**:    
    - _Main Double Doors_: The primary entrance to the tower, now partially ajar and scorched by acid.
    - _Trapdoor_: A hidden trapdoor under the debris in the southeast corner leads to a small, collapsed basement. This area doesn’t connect directly to the rest of the tower but could hold minor loot or clues about the wizard's activities.
- **Exit to the Second Floor**:    
    - _Spiral Staircase_: A partially collapsed stone staircase winds upward along the western wall. Characters must navigate the rubble to ascend (_Athletics DC 12_ or careful movement). Failing can cause noise or small debris falls.
- **Features**:
    - The walls are lined with broken shelves and acid-scarred tomes. Only a handful of books remain legible.
    - A central, cracked mosaic depicts a lightning bolt striking a tower, with the motto _"Harness the storm, wield its power"_ in Old Common.
    - The room hums faintly with residual energy.
    - one area of the floor is filled with large chunks of broken stone and fallen furniture 
- **Passive Skill Tests**:    
    - _Passive Perception (15)_: Notice faint scorch marks leading to a partially hidden trapdoor under the debris.
    - _Passive Investigation (13)_: Spot irregularities in the acid-scarred tomes, including a few pages that remain untouched (containing research notes on ley lines).
- **Loot/Clues**:
	- Damaged books on elemental magic and ley lines.
	- A shattered, non-functional crystal focus in a brass frame, used to harness storm energy.
	- Scorch marks in a wide arc suggest a fierce magical battle occurred here.
- **Active Skill Tests**:
	- _Investigation (DC 15)_: Search the shattered mosaic for hidden compartments. Success reveals a concealed slot with a damaged lightning rod (non-functional but valuable to collectors).
    - _Arcana (DC 14)_: Decipher the faint glow of the runes on the walls. Success reveals they are remnants of a protection spell disrupted by the attack.
    - _Athletics (DC 14)_: Move through the debris, lifting large chunks of broken stone or pushing fallen furniture out of the way to gain access to a hidden alcove.
- **Locked Skill Test**:    
    - _Background Prerequisite (Sage or Guild Artisan)_: Recognize the Old Common motto and identify its historical context, leading to insight into the tower's founding purpose and the wizard’s philosophy.
- **Loot descriptions**:
	- **"Artifice of Precision"**
		- **Author:** Master Artificer Jole Henthorn.
		- **Description:** A workshop guide for creating precision tools to assist with spellcasting and crafting. 
			- Includes schematics for crafting a **Lightning Rod Focus**, ideal for channeling electricity-based magic.
			- **Req.:** Proficiency in **Tinker's Tools**. Allows crafting of a **Lightning Rod Focus**. This focus grants +1 damage die on lightning spells when used as a spellcasting focus. Crafting cost: 100 gp, 2 days.
				- [[Formula for Lightning Rod Focus]]
- **Trapdoor**
	- a small stone staircase leads down into the basement 
	- this is the workshop, a big table has an unfinished construct lying on it, a shield guardian that was never finished. 
	- further down the basement is an open area, on the floor is the markings of a teleportation circle, however the sequence is covered with [[Illusory Script]] 
	- some of the markings in the teleportation circle can be manuevered, if done right they open up the wall at the end of the room, revealing a vault door. 
		- **Vault Door**: Once the adventurers enter the vault, they are confronted with a solid, ornate door made of black iron reinforced with gleaming silver runes. Upon the door is a large lightning bolt symbol engraved and in the middle of it is a keyhole. 
		- The door is resistant to magic, and only the correct incantation or a physical key — which might be somewhere else in the tower — will allow it to open.  (the dragon's hoard has the key now)
		- The door is protected by [[Arcane Lock]] 
		- The door is also protected by [[Glyph of Warding]] triggered by failing to pick the lock [[Chain Lightning]] DC 22
		- The door is also protected by [[Glyph of Warding]] triggered by targeting the door with a spell - [[Confusion]] DC 22
#### **Second Floor: The Laboratory of Storms**

- **Purpose**: The wizard’s main workspace.
- **Entrance**:    
    - _Spiral Staircase from Ground Floor_: The staircase emerges into this room, depositing characters near a broken alchemical workstation.
    - _Collapsed Wall_: A section of the wall is missing, opening to the outside. This gap allows flying creatures or creative climbers to enter or exit the tower here. A rogue or a barbarian could scale the wall (_Athletics DC 14_ or _Acrobatics DC 15_).
- **Exit to the Third Floor**:    
    - _Damaged Ladder_: A wooden ladder built into the eastern wall leads to the next level but is cracked and unstable. Climbing it without falling requires _Athletics DC 12_ or _Dexterity DC 15_.
- **Features**:
    - A large, broken contraption resembling a Tesla coil dominates the room, designed to channel lightning strikes into magical experiments.
    - Cracked windows overlook the swamp and forest, their frames charred by lightning.
    - The walls are adorned with diagrams of storms, runic inscriptions, and partially burned maps of the swamp and Blackwood Forest.
    - a doorway is blocked by a huge barrel of chemicals that looks very heavy 
    - **Loot/Clues**:
        - A small, intact jar labeled _“Condensed Lightning Essence”_.
        - The shattered remains of a homunculus, its frame corroded by acid.
        - Notes about _"temporal disturbances in the swamp"_ and suspicions of Red Hand activity.
	        - **The Wizard's Journal** (Damaged but Legible in Parts):  
	        - This charred and partially acid-damaged journal contains the wizard's observations. Some key entries might include:
	        - _“Temporal disturbances began roughly two months ago. Is this tied to planar shifts or mortal interference? Investigating reports of beasts aging rapidly and crops wilting unnaturally fast.”_
	        - _“Local lizardfolk seem agitated. They claim a ‘dark god’ has awakened. Are they mistaking mortal meddling for divine intervention?”_
	        - _“The Goblins have some item of power, It radiates immense temporal energy and may be causing these anomalies. How did they come by such a device?”_
- **Active Skill Tests**:   
    - _Arcana (DC 16)_: Analyze the broken Tesla coil-like contraption. Success explains how it functioned to channel storm energy, providing hints for repairing similar devices in the future.
    - _Survival (DC 14)_: Examine acid damage patterns on the walls. Success determines that a black dragon’s acidic breath caused them.
    - _Athletics (DC 13)_: Use raw strength to move a heavy barrel of chemicals blocking a doorway. Success opens up a new area with additional loot or clues.
- **Passive Skill Tests**:     
    - _Passive Perception (14)_: Notice a trail of strange, glowing footprints leading to a shattered window. These might belong to a draconic minion or a hag.
    - _Passive Arcana (13)_: Feel the lingering static charge in the air, which hints at unstable magical effects in this area.
- **Locked Skill Test**:     
    - _Tinker’s Tools Proficiency_: Examine and salvage functional parts of the storm conduit device. Success yields a small capacitor that can store a single charge of _lightning bolt_.

#### **Third Floor: The Arcanist's Study**

- **Purpose**: The wizard’s personal library and quarters.
- **Entrance**:    
    - _Damaged Ladder from Second Floor_: The ladder deposits characters onto the edge of the floor, near piles of debris and torn books.
    - _Collapsed Ceiling Access_: A jagged hole in the floor above suggests an alternative entrance for characters using climbing gear or spells like _Misty Step_.
- **Exit to the Fourth Floor**:    
    - _Metal Hatch_: A metal hatch in the ceiling once led to a sturdy access ladder, now twisted and broken. Opening the hatch requires brute force (_Strength DC 16_) or a creative tool like a crowbar.
    - _Collapsed Ceiling Climb_: The collapse makes it possible to climb between floors, but it’s risky (_Athletics DC 15_) due to unstable masonry.
- **Features**:
    - A large, acid-damaged desk with its contents scattered across the floor.
    - A toppled bookcase with singed books and scrolls.
    - A broken bedframe and scattered personal effects, such as a rusted ceremonial dagger and an empty wand stand.
- **Passive Skill Tests**:     
    - _Passive Perception (15)_: Spot a faint glow coming from the locked drawer, indicating the presence of a spell scroll (_Call Lightning_).
    - _Passive Investigation (13)_: Notice the pages of the wizard’s charred journal that survived the acid attack.
- **Loot/Clues**:
	- The wizard’s charred journal, containing details of the swamp's disturbances, with notes about wanting to reach out to the starsong elves hoping their strange relationship with time could mean that they could help him better understand what is going on.
	- A locked drawer (DC 15 to open) containing a single intact spell scroll (_Call Lightning_).
	- A faint, magical echo of the wizard’s voice whispers occasionally: _“The hourglass… seek the nexus...”_
- **Active Skill Tests**:    
    - _Sleight of Hand (DC 14)_: Manipulate the damaged desk’s hidden mechanisms to open a secret compartment. Inside are the wizard’s notes on the temporal disturbances.
    - _History (DC 12)_: Study the scattered personal effects to infer details about the wizard’s personality and motivations.
- **Locked Skill Test**:     
    - _Class Requirement (Wizard or Sorcerer)_: Decipher the runes on the bookcase to unlock a spellbook containing mid-tier spells like _Counterspell_ or _Dispel Magic_.
#### **Fourth Floor: The Conduit Chamber**

- **Purpose**: The tower’s storm-charging mechanism.
- **Entrance**:    
    - _Metal Hatch from Third Floor_: If successfully opened, the hatch provides direct access to the chamber.
    - _Collapsed Ceiling Climb_: Characters climbing up must deal with falling debris or risk lightning surges from damaged magical conduits.
- **Exit to Collapsed Upper Spire**:    
    - _Shattered Stairwell_: The remnants of a spiral staircase cling to the northern wall. Traversing it requires a _Dexterity (Acrobatics) DC 14_ or an _Athletics DC 12_ to avoid slipping or falling debris.
    - _Hole in the Roof_: A jagged tear in the roof allows flight-capable characters or climbers (_Athletics DC 15_) to reach the collapsed upper spire.
- **Features**:
    - A wide, circular room open to the sky, with jagged remnants of the tower’s peak above.
    - The floor is a cracked magical circle designed to draw and focus lightning strikes. It now pulses erratically with residual energy.
    - Twisted, blackened metal rods and conduits line the walls, some still sparking faintly.
    - The floor is filled with lots of jagged pieces of broken glass and other sharp debris, making searching the room perilous
- **Passive Skill Tests**:    
    - _Passive Investigation (14)_: Recognize that the lightning energy is reacting to an external magical influence, possibly the hourglass artifact.
    - _Passive Perception (15)_: Notice the faint outline of a hidden alcove in the wall containing elemental reagents.
- **Loot/Clues**:
	- A small cache of elemental reagents hidden in a wall alcove.
	- A charred skeleton, possibly the wizard’s, clutching a ruined staff.
	- Runic carvings on the walls warn of _“a storm too great to tame”_.
- **Active Skill Tests**:    
    - _Athletics (DC 15)_: Navigate the broken floor without triggering unstable sections. Failure could result in falling debris or a surge of residual lightning energy.
    - _Arcana (DC 16)_: Stabilize the erratically pulsing magical circle. Success temporarily suppresses the energy, allowing safe exploration.
    - _Dexterity (DC 14)_: Carefully maneuver between jagged pieces of broken glass and sharp debris to avoid injury while searching the room.
- **Locked Skill Test**:    
    - _Proficiency with Lightning-Based Spells_: Safely harvest the residual lightning energy from the broken circle. Success yields a vial of condensed lightning essence.
#### **Collapsed Upper Spire**

The upper spire lies shattered and scattered around the tower’s base, mingled with the debris of the dragon’s attack. This area might contain:
- **Entrance**:   
    - _Shattered Stairwell from Fourth Floor_: The precarious stairwell ends here, giving access to the uppermost remnants of the spire.
    - _Roof Hole from Fourth Floor_: Characters climbing through the hole emerge into the debris-laden spire.
- **Exit**:
     - _Leads Outside_: This level has no upward continuation; it is open to the elements. From here, characters must descend to leave, either via the shattered stairwell or through climbing down the exterior (_Athletics DC 14_ for walls, DC 12 for ropes).

- **Shards of Crystallized Lightning**: Fragments of the tower’s storm-harnessing apparatus.
- **Evidence of the Dragon’s Presence**: Acid-burned claw marks, black scales, or discarded minion gear hinting at the Red Hand’s involvement.
- **Passive Skill Tests**:    
    - _Passive Survival (14)_: Notice signs of recent movement, such as tracks from the Red Hand's minions scavenging the area.
    - _Passive Arcana (12)_: Sense faint magical echoes tied to the hags’ interference during the dragon’s attack.
- **Active Skill Tests**:    
    - _Investigation (DC 13)_: Search the debris for usable shards of crystallized lightning. Success yields valuable fragments that can be sold or used for crafting.
    - _Nature (DC 15)_: Analyze the dragon’s claw marks to confirm the size and age of the attacking black dragon.
    - _Strength (DC 16)_: Lift a large piece of rubble blocking an important section of the tower. This can reveal hidden areas or treasure caches.
- **Locked Skill Test**:    
    - _Artisan’s Tools Proficiency (Smith's or Tinker’s Tools)_: Salvage a functioning lightning rod from the spire debris. This item could help with magical experiments or defense mechanisms.
