## Room 4: Subterranean Lake
The tunnel opens up into a vast chamber with a subterranean lake at its center. The air is humid, and the sound of dripping water is replaced by gentle lapping waves. The lake shimmers with an otherworldly phosphorescence.

occupants:  Water Weird x2 

- Wisdom (Perception) - DC 16 To notice a hidden underwater passage, accessible by swimming through the phosphorescent lake. A successful DC 14 Strength (Athletics) check is required to navigate the strong underwater currents.
- Subterranean lake, Phosphorescent glow, Hidden underwater passage The phosphorescent glow of the lake provides an ethereal ambiance. The hidden underwater passage offers an alternative route and potential surprises for those who dare to explore. -GM Note The underwater passage connects to a secret area containing valuable loot and a clue about the cyclops's lair.
- Exits Aside from the entrance, the lake has a hidden underwater passage that leads to a secret area connected to Route E.
