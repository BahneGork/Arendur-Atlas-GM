# Zombies of Thanatos
Zombies from the realm of Orcus the prince of undeath.
Undead
HP 35
AC 8
Speed 30ft
immune: poison, necrotic, poisoned 
darkvision 60ft
undead fortitude: when reaching 0 hp, con dc 5+dmg drop to 1 hp
Slam, melee: +5 to hit 5ft one target 4 1d6+1 bludgeoning
Upon death: explodes 5ft radius in a cloud of spores, CON DC 13, fail = -1d4 on attacks and saves as the body withers. 
	last until long rest or [[Lesser Restoration]]
