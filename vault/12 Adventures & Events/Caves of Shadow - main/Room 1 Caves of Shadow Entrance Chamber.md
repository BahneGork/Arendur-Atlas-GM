# Room 1: Entrance Chamber
The air in this chamber is thick with the musty scent of earth, and the sound of dripping water echoes softly. 
The ground is uneven, covered in damp rocks and patches of slippery moss.
Faint bioluminescent fungi illuminate the area, casting an eerie glow. 
The corpses of 40 halfburned ghouls litter the floor.
### Features
- Stalacites above  
	- Wisdom (Perception) - DC 14 To notice that the stalactites above are not natural formations; they are cleverly disguised Giant Spider webs. 
	- A successful DC 16 Dexterity (Acrobatics) check allows a character to avoid stepping on a sticky strand.
- Bioluminescent fungi, Slippery moss, Giant Spider webs The bioluminescent fungi emit a dim light, revealing the web-covered stalactites. The slippery moss poses a potential hazard for those not cautious, leading to difficult terrain. The Giant Spider webs conceal a lurking danger overhead. 
	- -GM Note The webs are an indication that Giant Spiders inhabit the cave system. They can be lured out with prey or triggered by loud noises.

- **Exits**
- There are 2 visible exits
	- one exit leading deeper into the cave system straight ahead, connected to Route A -> Ghoulish Gallery 
	- Another clearly visible simple tunnel exit leads to a large tunnel, ceiling is 40ft up. 
		- This route is blocked by a magical barrier. 
- **Hidden exit:**
   - Perception DC 14 - (19 in the dark) - Hidden behind a thick wall of spider web silk, The concealed entrance to Route B is halfway up a wall and halfway into the floor nestled within the rocky wall, blending with the surrounding terrain.
   - A narrow passage, barely noticeable amidst the natural formations of the cave, leads into the depths of the mountain. 
   - the web burns easily
