# Room 3 - Labyrinthine Tunnels - Caves of Shadow
## Room 3: Labyrinthine Tunnels
The passage ahead splits into a confusing network of tunnels, creating a labyrinth. The air is cooler here, and faint echoes reverberate through the winding paths. The tunnels are dominated by lots and lots of heavy spider webbing. 

- Labyrinthine tunnels, Symbols on the walls, Faint footprints The labyrinthine tunnels create a disorienting environment. The symbols on the walls, when deciphered, provide a map that aids in navigating the maze. Faint footprints suggest recent passage, potentially leading to an undiscovered route or secret. -GM Note The labyrinth was created by the cyclops to confuse intruders, but it also serves as a trap for the unwary.
- Exits Several tunnels lead to dead ends, but one clear exit leads to Route D.
