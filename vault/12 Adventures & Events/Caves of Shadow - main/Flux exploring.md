# Flux Turns
Flux turn sequence 
```mermaid 
graph LR
A[chart] --> b[Deplete resources ]
b --> c[Resolve event die] 
c --> d[POI ]
d --> e[Choose next action] 
```
1 Flux turn is approximately 4 hours or what fits. 
6 Flux turns pr day 
During a Flux turn, the party moves carefully, dropping breadcrumbs, taking measurements, making notes, backtracking, making maps and all other hard work that comes with charting an area. 

## Choose Flux Turn Action
- Charting 
- Peregrinate - move to any previously discovered POI without depleting resources but still roll Event die 
- Make camp 
	- Spending more than 4 Flux turns pr day on activity causes all to gain 1 point of exhaustion pr extra turn after 4.
	- A rest is 2 flux turns
		- Take Watch action 
		- none on  watch = event rolls of encounter sign is instead an encounter 
		- 1 on watch = 1 point of exhaustion 
		- 2 on watch = no exhaustion 
	- Sleep action
		- Characters sleeping for 2 full turns may remove a point of exhaustion 

## Deplete Resources
# Event List
1. Encounter
2. Rest (+1 Negative Event Die) or gain 1 Exhaustion
3. Deplete all rations or lower all Supply by 1.
4. Local Effect
5. Encounter Sign
6. NPC Chatter

# Structure
## Structure To Do List
- Theme  (information)
- Encounters (list) 2d4 (7)
- Local effect  (list) 1-3
- POIs  (list) D6 shallow +3 deep 
- Special (information)

## Theme
a maze? an abandoned mine? massive clockwork? etc.. 
## Encounter List 2d4 or 2d6 (depending on Size of flux)
- 7 encounter entries, include a location 
	- d6 skeletons on a narrow staircase
	- alternatively another list with encounter locations/enviroments 
- put rare at each end of the list 
### Example List 2d4
2. Dragon
3. 2d6 Zippity Gloobs: Eyeballs with four razor talons protruding from around their retina. Fly by screaming, though they have no mouths. They gather in wasplike nests seen frequently around the bottomless pits.
4. 1d6+1 Cow Creature raiders. They are collecting loot from the bodies of three dead surface dwellers. Cow creatures can go up stairs, but not down them.
5. 1d6+1 Cow Creature raiders. They’re on the hunt for intruders. Cow creatures can go up stairs, but not down them.
6. A sludgebelly which wandered out of the Slime Mines, and is now lost. Reaction roll determines how long it has been lost and how hungry & frustrated it is.
7. Animated suit of armor left behind by a wizard with a bad disposition.
8. Wizard
## Local Effect (1 to 3 as They Can Be reused)
- Altered circumstance: a change that affects all future turns until local effect is rolled again
- Minor choice: obstacle that forces to choose between 2 or more costs "which held item do you drop?" - "Do you risk noise and attract danger or do you spend time to circumvent it" 
- Attrition: extra toll on the party's resources  
- Flavor/Hint:  expressions of the theme or secrets/clues 
## Points of Interest
- d6 shallow rooms 
	- After event die resolution, roll on shallow points of interest and cross them out as they have been found. If the die rolls an already found POI, instead go through the deep room list in the order first to last (dont roll)
- 3 deeper rooms 
- Once all POI's have been found the flux has been charted and is "solved" 
## Special
- Does the flux deplete special resources ?
- Does someone want the party to do something in the Flux?
- Any dangers in the Flux that may follow the party outside?
- are there any Faction in the Flux?
	- which of the encounters?
	- which POI do they reside in?
	- what do they want?
- Is one of the POI's a bottleneck that has to be dealt with every time they move between discovered POIs?
