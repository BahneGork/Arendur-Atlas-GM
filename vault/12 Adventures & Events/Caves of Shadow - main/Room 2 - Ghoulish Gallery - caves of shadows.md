## Room 2: Ghoulish Gallery
The chamber widens, revealing an eerie ossuary (benhus).
The air is thick with a pungent odor of decay, mold and the low hum of necrotic energy can be felt.
There is a rather huge ritualistic diagram made of bones on the floor, the bones making up the pattern seems to have melded with the cavern floor. One section of the diagram is obscured and covered by a pile of stones from a collapse of the roof.

- MOLD 
	- Wisdom (Nature) DC 14 The mold is dangerous to breathe, paralytic. 
	- CON DC 15 or become paralyzed 
	- Wisdom (Medicine) DC 14 The mold takes effect if breathed in, a cloth will provide advantage on con check 
- Intelligence (Arcane) - DC 15 To discern that the ritual was necromanctic in nature and about control.
- Strength (athletics ) DC 16 removing the pile of stones 
- (passive) Wisdom (Perception) - DC 12 the bones in the diagram appears to have writing on them.
- Intelligence (Investigation) - DC 15 the writing was burned into the bones 
	- Intelligence (Arcane) - DC 12 the burning of the writing was an effect of ritual. 
	- Intelligence (Arcane) - DC 15 the ritual was about transfering control, but it contains a curse within it. 
		- The more undeads under the masters control, the weaker he will be.
- A successful DC 16 Wisdom (Religion) check identifies the presence of residual necromantic magic.
- 
- Exits Two exits lead to different routes: one connects to Route B, and the other leads to Route C.
