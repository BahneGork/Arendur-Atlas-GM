# Zombie Hill Giant
>[!important]+ 
>*Striding out of the dark towards you, comes a massive hulk of bloated dead flesh, rotting and decaying. It's eyes glows with a sickly green light, like the skeletons outside. The thing is huge.
It looks like the intestines are squirwing around underneath the pale decaying flesh. 
>"looking at the thing, you notice distortions in the air around it. 
>Almost like heatwaves but with a tinge of a sickly green hue and small green lights flickering in and out. 
>The ground around it seems to crack ever so slightly and the cracks glow with the same sick green light." *
>

>[!hint]+
>- Passive Percetion DC 15 "the small green lights flickers in patterns, which forms various sigils" 
>-  Active Arcane DC 15 "These are arcane sigils with fiendish properties, they are related to death."
>- Passive Nature DC 13 "There is an unnatural lack of life surrounding it."
>- Passive Insight DC 13 "The thing is hostile, there is a sense of hate of life about it"
## Hulking Zombie Birther Statblock
type: Undead 
_Huge giant, chaotic evil_
**Armor Class** 13 (natural armor)
**Hit Points** 105 (10d12+40)
**Speed** 40 ft.

|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|21 (+5)|8 (-1)|19 (+4)|5 (-3)|9 (-1)|6 (-2)|
**Skills** Perception +2
**Senses** passive Perception 12
**Languages** Giant
**Challenge** 5 (1,800 XP)

**_Multiattack_**. The giant makes two greatclub attacks.
**_Greatclub_**. _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 18 (3d8+5) bludgeoning damage.
**_Rock_**. _Ranged Weapon Attack:_ +8 to hit, range 60/240 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 21 (3d10+5) bludgeoning damage.
*Marked for death*: Once a round in place of 1 attack, the hulk can spit a glob of acidic sludge at a target, +8 hit - 1d8+5 acid, that marks them as a target for the zombies. If the target is wearing non-magical armor that armor lose 1 AC. 
- The Zombies gets advantage on attacks vs marked target. The mark lasts until start of the hulk's next turn. 
**Extra Features**
- *undead fortitude*: if dmg reduce to 0, CONsav DC= 5+ DMG  (unless radiant or crit). Success = alive on 1hp
- *Withering Aura*: starting or ending a turn within 5 feet triggers a death saving throw. 3 successes makes you immune.
- *Birther*: at initiative 20 a portal to The wells of darkness opens and spews forth 1d6 zombies of Thanatos from its belly.
