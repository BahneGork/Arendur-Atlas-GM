# The Quest for the Sapphiretail Staff

## An Adventure Module for D&D 5th Edition (Levels 8-12)

*A necromancer's obsession with an ancient artifact leads to a race against time across the land. Little does anyone know that the artifact is tied to a temporal prophecy involving one of the heroes themselves...*

## Adventure Introduction for DMs

This module revolves around a powerful artifact called the Sapphiretail Staff—a magical implement crafted from the tail gem of an actual Sapphire Gemstone Dragon, one of the legendary keepers of time magic. The staff grants extraordinary control over time itself, allowing its wielder to manipulate temporal flows and potentially alter reality.

The primary antagonist is Magister Vothren, a necromancer who discovered ancient texts referencing an artifact that could grant mastery over time. His research led him to learn about the Sapphiretail Staff and a prophecy connected to Anton Keene, a human chronurgy wizard from the southern realm of Akacia. What Vothren doesn't initially realize is that he's being manipulated by an even greater threat—an Alternative Anton from another timeline who has already mastered his own version of the staff.

This Alternative Anton has been traveling between timelines, seeking out and merging with his other selves and their respective staffs to grow increasingly powerful. He has already absorbed three alternate versions of himself and their staffs, each merger increasing his control over the multiverse. His ultimate goal is to collapse all timelines into one perfect reality where he reigns as the singular master of time. To achieve this, he needs to collect and merge with as many versions of himself and their staffs as possible, with this timeline's Anton being his latest target.

The adventure begins with the party discovering that Vothren has been searching through the tomb of Durso Preiss, a legendary chronurgy wizard who first discovered the staff and recognized its dangers. Years ago, Durso worked with Anton's parents—Anna and Darragh O'Keene, a fighter and bard respectively—to hide the staff using three temporal anchors when they discovered their son was the subject of the prophecy.

The Prophecy of Dual Paths predicts that Anton Keene, due to his unique bloodline connection to Durso Preiss and his secret training in time magic (stolen from the teachings of his master Carthach ó Caskill at the Midnight Tower), is destined to either become the staff's greatest master or its ultimate destroyer. This destiny was foreseen by the Cult of Loz Tdrot, the god of secrets, of which both Anton and Carthach are members. The prophecy states that in infinite timelines, Anton faces the same choice—and in many, he chooses power and begins the path toward becoming like the Alternative Anton the party will eventually face.

As the adventure progresses, the party must race to find the three temporal anchors before Vothren does, while increasingly becoming aware of Alternative Anton's manipulation behind the scenes. During a crucial encounter at the Splintered Oak temporal anomaly, Alternative Anton will attempt to convince this timeline's Anton to willingly join with him, claiming it's the inevitable conclusion to their shared destiny.

In this conversation, Alternative Anton reveals that he began as a version of Anton who chose power, and with each merger, he gains all the knowledge, experiences, and magical might of his alternatives. He will appeal to Anton's dedication to the Cult of Loz Tdrot, arguing that ultimate knowledge and the greatest secrets of the multiverse can only be obtained through their union. He'll also reveal that some versions of Anton resist—those are the ones he must take by force—but that willing merger is painless and allows both consciousness to continue existing within the new, improved whole.

The adventure culminates in a final confrontation at the Grove of Convergence, where the party must face both Vothren and Alternative Anton as they attempt to claim this timeline's staff and complete another merger. Anton will face his ultimate choice—embrace the power offered by his alternative self, destroy the staff to end the cycle, or find a third path that preserves the balance between timelines.

## Adventure Synopsis

This adventure follows the player characters as they pursue a necromancer who seeks the legendary Sapphiretail Staff. Their investigation reveals connections to one of the party members—Anton Keene, a chronurgy wizard from Akacia—whose parents hid the staff years ago to prevent a dark prophecy. As the party gathers temporal anchors to find the staff before the necromancer does, they uncover evidence of an alternative timeline where Anton became corrupted by the staff's power and now seeks to merge with all versions of himself across the multiverse.

## How to Use This Module

This adventure uses a node-based structure with the Three Clue Rule—each location provides at least three clues pointing to other nodes, allowing players multiple paths to progress. The adventure begins at Node 1 (Dead Mountain Tomb) and culminates at Node 10 (Final Confrontation).

## Node Details

### Node 1: Dead Mountain Tomb

**Location Type:** Ancient burial site

**Core Information:**
- Durso Preiss's tomb contains temporal markings and evidence of the necromancer's studies
- First hints of connection between Durso, the staff, and Anton's parents

**Description:**
The tomb is carved into Dead Mountain's rocky face, adorned with arcane symbols related to time magic. Evidence of the necromancer's recent presence is abundant—disturbed artifacts, burnt offerings, and residual necromantic energy permeate the site. The central chamber houses Durso Preiss's sarcophagus with a stone tablet on its lid featuring inscriptions and carvings.

**Key Features:**
- Stone tablet with temporal riddles and symbols that occasionally shimmer with blue light
- Abandoned research materials from the necromancer
- A section of wall depicting Durso Preiss with a sapphire-tipped staff alongside other figures

**Encounters:**
- **CR 6:** 2 Wights (Guards of the tomb awakened by the necromancer's tampering)
- **CR 8:** 1 Time-Warped Mummy (A temporal guardian with the ability to age characters with its touch)

**Skill Challenges:**
1. **Investigation (DC 15):** Finding the necromancer's discarded journal fragments in the debris reveals his interest in the sapphiretail staff and mentions of "the Grand Archives of Kvalec."
2. **History (DC 18):** Recognizing the significance of Durso Preiss as a legendary chronurgy wizard (requires proficiency in History to attempt).
3. **Arcana (DC 16):** Identifying the temporal markings as protective wards against time-based scrying and recognizing their recent disturbance.

**Three Clues Leading to Other Nodes:**
1. **To Node 2 (Grand Archives):** A journal fragment reads: "The full extent of Preiss's research must be in the Grand Archives of Kvalec. The curator Maeven must be persuaded to grant access to the restricted section." (Found with Investigation check)
2. **To Node 3 (Parents' Home):** A faded image on the wall shows Durso Preiss standing with two other adventurers who Anton recognizes as his parents when they were younger. Inscription below reads "The Guardians Three." (Visible to all, but Anton must make a DC 12 Wisdom save or experience a brief temporal flash of Anna and Darragh O'Keene in the tomb)
3. **To Node 7 (Temporal Anomaly):** A small brass device left by the necromancer occasionally pulses with energy, causing momentary temporal disruptions—brief glimpses of alternative timelines. (Arcana DC 14 to safely contain it; triggers randomly if not disabled)

**Treasure:**
- Durso Preiss's Temporal Amulet: Allows the wearer to cast Slow once per day
- Spellbook fragment containing two time-related spells of the DM's choice
- 240 gp worth of ancient coins and burial offerings

### Node 2: Grand Archives of Kvalec

**Location Type:** Urban library/research facility

**Core Information:**
- Historical records about the staff's powers and dangers
- Evidence of the necromancer's recent research activities
- Documents linking Durso Preiss to time magic experiments

**Description:**
The Grand Archives of Kvalec is a towering structure in the port city's scholarly district. Multiple floors of bookshelves hold the accumulated knowledge of centuries. The Restricted Section requires special permission to access. Several areas show signs of recent, hasty research—pages torn out, books left open, and traces of magical scanning.

**Key Features:**
- Curator Maeven Briar's office, containing records of recent visitors
- Damaged section where the necromancer destroyed certain texts after reading them
- Hidden vault containing the most dangerous magical texts

**Encounters:**
- **Social:** Curator Maeven Briar (Neutral, suspicious of the party, requires convincing to grant access)
- **CR 5:** Animated books and scrolls (modified Flying Swords and Animated Armor stats) triggered if restricted texts are mishandled

**Skill Challenges:**
1. **Persuasion (DC 16):** Convincing Curator Maeven to grant access to the restricted section.
2. **Investigation (DC 17):** Reconstructing damaged or missing pages from the necromancer's research.
3. **History (DC 15):** Connecting historical references to the sapphiretail staff across multiple texts.

**Three Clues Leading to Other Nodes:**
1. **To Node 3 (Parents' Home):** Library records showing Anna and Darragh O'Keene checked out the same texts 25 years ago, listing their address in the city of Highvale. (Found with Investigation check)
2. **To Node 4 (First Anchor):** A map in "Artifacts of the Temporal Arcane" marking the Temple of Eternal Moment where Durso first discovered traces of the staff. (Requires access to restricted section)
3. **To Node 5 (Durso's Lab):** Reference in Durso's published works to his "sanctuary of temporal study in the Mistpeak Range." (Requires History check with proficiency to find relevant text)

**Treasure:**
- Scroll of Haste
- Spellbook containing Chronurgy spells (Temporal Shunt, Gift of Alacrity)
- Curator's token allowing future access to the restricted section

### Node 3: The O'Keene Family Home

**Location Type:** Abandoned residence

**Core Information:**
- Evidence that Anna (fighter) and Darragh (bard) O'Keene were adventurers seeking the staff
- Their discovery of the prophecy about their son Anton
- Their plan to hide the staff using temporal anchors

**Description:**
A modest but well-built home in the city of Highvale, now showing signs of neglect. Dust covers much of the furniture, suggesting the owners haven't been present for months despite both being in their 60s now. A hidden adventuring room contains maps, journals, and magical equipment. The house shows signs of recent intrusion—someone has searched it thoroughly.

**Key Features:**
- Hidden room behind a bookcase in the study
- Magical wards that recognize Anton Keene's blood/magical signature
- Family portraits showing Anna and Darragh O'Keene with Durso Preiss and others
- Secret correspondence with the Cult of Loz Tdrot, including references to Carthach ó Caskill of the Midnight Tower

**Encounters:**
- **CR 3:** 3 Shadows (remnants of a magical trap triggered by the necromancer)
- **CR 6:** 1 Spirit Naga (an old associate of the necromancer left to watch the house)

**Skill Challenges:**
1. **Perception (DC 14):** Finding the hidden lever that reveals the secret adventuring room.
2. **Arcana (DC 16):** Identifying specialized chronurgy components among the parents' equipment and symbols of the Cult of Loz Tdrot.
3. **Investigation (DC 18):** Piecing together journal fragments to understand the prophecy about Anton Keene.

**The Prophecy Fragments:**
Journal entries reveal that the Prophecy of Dual Paths was discovered by the Cult of Loz Tdrot, who identified Anton as its subject due to three factors: his bloodline connection to Durso Preiss (a distant ancestor), his innate temporal sensitivity, and his secret study of time magic from a tome stolen from his master Carthach ó Caskill. These elements created a unique confluence that made Anton the nexus of countless possible timelines—each diverging based on a critical choice he would eventually make regarding the staff.

**Three Clues Leading to Other Nodes:**
1. **To Node 4 (First Anchor):** A personal journal entry from Anna O'Keene: "The Temple's guardian accepted our offering. The first anchor is secure beneath the statue of the hourglass. Only blood of our line will reveal it now." (Found in hidden room)
2. **To Node 6 (Necromancer's Lair):** A magical sending stone with recent message fragments mentioning "Master Vothren's activities near the Darkfen Marsh." (Requires Arcana DC 15 to activate)
3. **To Node 7 (Temporal Anomaly):** A heavily warded box containing a crystal that shows visions of a temporal breach occurring at "The Splintered Oak where realms bleed together." (Requires Anton's touch to activate)

**Treasure:**
- Parents' magical adventuring gear (including a Cloak of Protection)
- 500 gp worth of rare components for chronurgy spells
- Family heirloom that resonates with Anton's magic (+1 to spell attack rolls when held)
- Sealed letter from the Cult of Loz Tdrot addressed to Anton

**Core Information:**
- First of three anchors needed to locate the staff

A temple built into a mountainside, where natural light enters through cunningly placed apertures to mark the passage of time on the floor. The central chamber houses a massive hourglass statue with sand that flows seemingly without end. Temporal magic permeates the site, causing occasional distortions in the flow of time.

**Key Features:**
- Central hourglass statue with hidden compartment
- Wall inscriptions detailing the creation of the sapphiretail staff
- Evidence of the necromancer's ritual attempts

**Encounters:**
- **CR 7:** Chronalis Sentinels (modified Clay Golems with time manipulation abilities)

**Skill Challenges:**
1. **Religion (DC 15):** Understanding the temple's purpose as a site where time is honored and studied.
2. **Arcana (DC 17):** Identifying the magical signatures of the anchor and how to retrieve it (requires proficiency).
3. **Athletics (DC 16):** Climbing the massive hourglass statue to reach the hidden compartment.

**Three Clues Leading to Other Nodes:**
1. **To Node 8 (Second Anchor):** An inscription reveals: "The second key lies where knowledge and time converge, in the halls where Preiss instructed his disciples." (Visible once anchor is retrieved)

3. **To Node 5 (Durso's Lab):** Ancient writing mentioning "Preiss's sanctuary where time's wheel was first broken and mended." (Religion DC 14 to translate properly)

**Treasure:**

### Node 7: Temporal Anomaly Site (The Splintered Oak)

**Location Type:** Natural temporal nexus

**Core Information:**
- Direct encounter with Alternative Anton and his timeline
- Understanding of the prophecy's true meaning
- Crucial conversation between Anton and his alternative self

**Description:**
A massive, ancient oak tree split down the middle by lightning, yet somehow still living. The space between the split halves shimmers with temporal energy, occasionally revealing glimpses of other timelines. The area around the tree experiences random temporal effects—accelerated growth, patches of decay, objects phasing in and out of existence.

**Key Features:**
- The split in the tree that serves as a window between timelines
- Crystalline formations that have grown in response to temporal energy
- Evidence of the necromancer's attempts to widen the breach

**Encounters:**
- **CR 8:** Temporal Parasites (modified Phase Spiders that feed on time energy)
- **CR 11:** Alternative Anton (hostile spellcaster if provoked, but will negotiate)
- **CR Variable:** Random creatures pulled from other timelines

**Skill Challenges:**
1. **Arcana (DC 19):** Stabilizing the temporal window to communicate safely across timelines.
2. **Wisdom Save (DC 16):** Resisting mental influence from Alternative Anton.
3. **Perception (DC 17):** Distinguishing truth from manipulation in visions shown by Alternative Anton.

**Alternative Anton's Conversation:**
During this encounter, Alternative Anton attempts to convince the current Anton to willingly merge with him. He appears as an older, more powerful version of Anton with streaks of white in his hair, eyes that occasionally flicker with temporal energy, and robes bearing symbols of the Cult of Loz Tdrot. Use the following points to guide this pivotal conversation:

- **Initial Approach:** "My brother in time, at last we meet. I've searched through many realities to find you." He explains that he was once exactly like this timeline's Anton, but chose to embrace the staff's power.

- **The Merger Process:** "I have already joined with three of our selves. Each adds their knowledge and power to our collective. The first resisted and was... consumed. The others joined willingly and their consciousness lives on within me—a unified existence of unparalleled understanding."

- **Appeal to Anton's Nature:** "You and I both sought knowledge Carthach ó Caskill would deny us. We both serve Loz Tdrot, the keeper of secrets. Think of the mysteries we could unravel together! The universal truths we could comprehend!" He will mention specific secrets that would tempt Anton specifically.

- **The Greater Purpose:** "The multitude of timelines is wasteful—countless versions of ourselves making the same mistakes, living the same limited lives. I seek to create one perfect timeline where we are the master of fate itself. Every merger brings this goal closer."

- **If Questioned About Motives:** He grows impatient and reveals his hunger for power: "Do not pretend you don't crave this too! I've seen into your heart—it beats with the same ambition as mine."

- **If Rejected:** His demeanor changes dramatically: "Then you will be the fourth to resist, and like the first, you will be taken by force when I claim the staff of this timeline. Your consciousness will be subjugated rather than embraced."

While he converses with Anton, Alternative Anton constantly studies the other party members, evaluating their strengths and weaknesses. He's confident but not foolish—if seriously threatened, he retreats through the temporal window rather than fighting to the death.

**Three Clues Leading to Other Nodes:**
1. **To Node 8 (Second Anchor):** Vision showing the Preiss Academy ruins and the exact location of the second anchor. (Granted through temporal window)
2. **To Node 9 (Third Anchor):** Psychic impression revealing that "the final key is bound to Anton's bloodline and can only be manifested at the site of his first temporal awakening." (Received from temporal energy)
3. **To Node 10 (Final Confrontation):** Revelation of Alternative Anton's ultimate plan to use the staff at the Grove of Convergence to merge timelines. (Direct information from Alternative Anton)

**Treasure:**
- Shard of Temporal Crystal (can be used to cast Blink once per day)
- Temporal Blossom (rare component for chronurgy magic)
- 500 gp worth of strange metals and gems from other timelines

### Node 10: Final Confrontation (The Grove of Convergence)

**Location Type:** Primal temporal nexus

**Core Information:**
- Where the sapphiretail staff is hidden
- Site of ultimate confrontation with necromancer and Alternative Anton
- Revelation of the full prophecy and Anton's choice

**Description:**
An ancient grove where massive trees form a perfect circle around a central clearing. The air shimmers with temporal energy, and occasional flashes reveal glimpses of other timelines. In the center, a stone pedestal holds the sapphiretail staff—a sleek, six-foot staff of dark wood topped with a glowing sapphire carved to resemble a dragon's tail. The necromancer and Alternative Anton are performing a ritual to merge timelines.

**Key Features:**
- Central pedestal with the sapphiretail staff
- Ritual circle being used by the villains
- Temporal windows showing glimpses of alternative histories
- Ancient standing stones with prophecy inscriptions

**Encounters:**
- **CR 12:** Magister Vothren (necromancer, full stat block)
- **CR 13:** Alternative Anton (chronurgy wizard with corrupted temporal abilities)
- **CR 8:** 3 Temporal Guardians (modified from Stone Golems)
- **CR Variable:** Temporal Rifts that spawn creatures from alternative timelines

**Skill Challenges:**
1. **Arcana (DC 20):** Using the three anchors to disrupt the villains' ritual.
2. **Wisdom Save (DC 18):** Resisting the mental influence of Alternative Anton who tries to persuade Anton to join him.
3. **Investigation (DC 17):** Deciphering the complete prophecy to understand how to properly use or destroy the staff.

**The Prophecy of Dual Paths:**
*"When timelines converge and the sapphire tail gleams,
Two fates will emerge from temporal dreams.
One path leads to power, dominion o'er time,
The other to balance, harmony sublime.
The wielder must choose which future to forge,
While standing between worlds at time's sacred gorge.
For only through choice may destiny part,
From darkness that waits in the temporal heart.
The blood of the dragon, the mind of the sage,
United in one who transcends time and age.
The Keene line descendant of Preiss ancient blood,
Must stem or unleash the temporal flood."*

**Climactic Decision:**
When Anton confronts his alternative self, he must make a crucial choice that determines the adventure's outcome:

- **Accept the Staff's Power:** Anton claims the staff, believing he can control it better than his alternative self. This grants him great temporal power but sets him on a path that may eventually lead to becoming like Alternative Anton.
- **Destroy the Staff:** Anton chooses to destroy the staff, severing the connection between timelines and preventing anyone from abusing its power. This requires a sacrifice—Anton loses his chronurgy abilities temporarily.
- **Seal the Staff:** Anton uses the three anchors to permanently seal the staff in a temporal bubble, preserving its power but making it inaccessible. This maintains the balance between timelines.
- **Merge with Alternative Self:** Anton chooses to merge with his alternative self but maintains control of the merger, creating a balanced being with the power of both but the moral compass of the original Anton.

**Possible Outcomes:**
1. **Victory:** The necromancer is defeated, Alternative Anton is banished back to his timeline, and the staff is dealt with according to Anton's choice.
2. **Partial Victory:** The necromancer is defeated, but Alternative Anton escapes with knowledge of this timeline, potentially setting up a future conflict.
3. **Alternative Victory:** If the party fails, Alternative Anton claims the staff and begins merging timelines, creating widespread temporal chaos that must be dealt with in a future adventure.

**Treasure:**
- The Sapphiretail Staff (if Anton chooses to claim it)
- Necromancer's spellbook and magical equipment
- Temporal artifacts from multiple timelines
- 1,500 gp worth of rare gems and magical components

### Magister Vothren (The Necromancer)

**Background:** A brilliant wizard who discovered evidence of alternative timelines and became obsessed with accessing their power. His research led him to discover the existence of Alternative Anton and the prophecy regarding the sapphiretail staff.

**Appearance:** A gaunt human man in his 60s with sunken eyes, long white hair, and elaborate black robes embroidered with silver symbols. Carries a staff topped with a crystal skull.

**Personality:** Cold, calculating, and utterly devoted to gaining power. Views himself as the rightful heir to Durso Preiss's temporal research. Despite his villainy, he genuinely believes his actions will benefit the world by giving him control over time itself.

**Motivation:** To claim the sapphiretail staff and use it to control the flow of time across all realities, choosing the optimal timeline and erasing others he considers inferior. He is unaware that Alternative Anton plans to betray him once the staff is found.

### Alternative Anton

**Background:** From a timeline where Anton discovered the staff in his youth and embraced its corrupting power. He has spent decades mastering temporal magic and has grown immensely powerful but at great cost to his humanity. He has already merged with three other versions of himself from different timelines.

**Appearance:** Physically identical to Anton but aged differently—his hair is streaked with white despite his youth, his eyes glow with temporal energy, and his skin occasionally shifts between appearing young and old. His robes bear prominent symbols of the Cult of Loz Tdrot.

**Personality:** Arrogant, condescending, and utterly convinced of his superior understanding. Views the main timeline's Anton as naive and weak. Despite his corruption, he still possesses twisted versions of Anton's core traits, particularly his thirst for knowledge and devotion to the Cult of Loz Tdrot.

**Motivation:** To merge with all versions of himself across the multiverse, gaining their knowledge and power, and ultimately to collapse all timelines into a single "perfect" reality where he reigns as the singular master of time.

### Curator Maeven Briar

**Background:** Head curator of the Grand Archives of Kvalec for over thirty years. Secretly a former student of Durso Preiss who has been monitoring his research materials for signs of misuse.

**Appearance:** A stern, elderly halfling woman with spectacles and immaculately kept robes. Always carries a magical ledger that tracks the location of every book in the archives.

**Personality:** Initially suspicious and protective of the restricted materials but warms to the party if they demonstrate genuine scholarly interest and respect. Has a dry sense of humor that emerges once she trusts someone.

**Motivation:** To preserve knowledge while preventing dangerous magical research from falling into the wrong hands. Particularly concerned about temporal magic, having witnessed its dangers firsthand as Durso's student.

### Carthach Ó Caskill

**Background:** A powerful wizard and Anton's former master at the Midnight Tower. A high-ranking member of the Cult of Loz Tdrot who recognized Anton's potential but kept advanced temporal magic from him, fearing the prophecy.

**Appearance:** A tall, thin man with a neatly trimmed white beard and piercing blue eyes. Dresses in midnight blue robes with silver embroidery depicting arcane symbols.

**Personality:** Stern, demanding, and secretive. Shows little emotion but has deep concerns about the stability of time itself. Has a complicated relationship with Anton, seeing him both as a promising student and a potential threat.

**Motivation:** To prevent the misuse of temporal magic while preserving the knowledge for those worthy of it. Knew of the prophecy regarding Anton and tried to guide him away from the path that would lead to becoming Alternative Anton.

## Appendix: Random Encounters

### Temporal Disturbances (d6)

1. **Time Bubble:** Area of accelerated or decelerated time affects a 30-foot radius. Creatures inside move at double speed or half speed respectively.
2. **Echo Encounter:** The party encounters spectral versions of themselves from potential futures or pasts. These echoes may provide warnings or cryptic advice.
3. **Age Flux:** Characters must make Constitution saves or temporarily age or de-age 1d20 years, affecting physical ability scores until the effect is dispelled.
4. **Déjà Vu:** Characters experience the same encounter twice in succession but can make different choices the second time.
5. **Timeline Bleed:** Creatures from an alternative timeline (use monsters of appropriate CR) appear and attack, believing the characters are invaders in their reality.
6. **Temporal Storm:** Violent distortion causes random teleportation within the area, wild magic effects, and difficult terrain as objects phase in and out of existence.

### Wilderness Encounters (d8)

1. 2d4 Temporal Parasites (use Phase Spider stats with additional time-manipulation abilities)
2. 1d3 Chronomatic Wolves (use Winter Wolf stats with time-based breath weapon)
3. A wandering time elemental (use Air Elemental stats with temporal abilities)
4. A merchant caravan that appears normal but is actually from 50 years in the past
5. A bivouac site where time moves at one-tenth normal speed
6. A temporal hunter (use Ranger stats) tracking creatures that slip between timelines
7. A confused traveler who is actually from an alternative timeline where history unfolded differently
8. An ancient battlefield where soldiers from different eras periodically phase into existence, still fighting their wars

## Appendix: Magic Items

### The Sapphiretail Staff

**Wondrous item, artifact (requires attunement by a spellcaster)**

A six-foot staff of polished dark wood with a large sapphire set at the top, carved to resemble a dragon's tail. The gem was harvested from an actual Sapphire Gemstone Dragon, one of the legendary keepers of time magic. The gem pulses with inner light that fluctuates between bright and dim in an irregular pattern.

**Temporal Mastery.** While attuned to this staff, you gain the following benefits:
- You can cast Haste, Slow, and Time Stop once per day each without expending a spell slot.
- You have advantage on initiative rolls.
- You can take an additional reaction each round.
- You have resistance to necrotic damage.

**Glimpse the Timelines.** As an action, you can use the staff to glimpse potential futures. This functions as the Divination spell, but you can see multiple potential outcomes depending on different choices. The DM provides three possible answers to your question, each tied to a different decision or path.

**Temporal Manipulation.** The staff has 10 charges. While holding it, you can expend 1 or more charges to cast the following spells: Blink (1 charge), Haste (2 charges), Slow (2 charges), Dimension Door (3 charges), Modify Memory (3 charges), or Temporal Shunt (3 charges). The staff regains 1d6+4 expended charges daily at dawn.

**Time Bubble.** As an action, you can expend 5 charges to create a bubble of altered time in a 30-foot radius centered on yourself. Choose one effect: time flows twice as fast, time flows at half speed, or time stops entirely for non-living objects. The bubble lasts for 1 minute or until you use an action to end it.

**Timeline Window.** The staff can be used to view and eventually access other timelines where different versions of the wielder exist. With practice, the wielder can communicate with or even merge with their alternative selves, gaining their knowledge and power but risking corruption.

**Corrupting Influence.** Each time you use the staff's Temporal Manipulation or Time Bubble abilities, you must make a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, you gain one level of temporal corruption. Effects of temporal corruption include:

1. Your appearance occasionally shifts between younger and older versions of yourself.
2. You begin experiencing memories from alternative timelines.
3. You can see glimpses of potential futures, which is disorienting and imposes disadvantage on Wisdom saving throws.
4. Your connection to your original timeline weakens; resurrection magic has a 25% chance of failure if you die.
5. You begin to physically phase between timelines, becoming incorporeal for brief moments at random times.

Levels of corruption can be removed through Greater Restoration or similar magic, but if you reach five levels of corruption, your consciousness begins to merge with your alternative selves, changing your alignment and personality. At this point, only a Wish spell can return you to normal.

## Adjusting the Adventure

### For Lower-Level Parties (Levels 5-7)

- Reduce the CR of encounters by 2-3 points.
- Remove some of the secondary enemies in combat encounters.
- Lower skill check DCs by 2-3 points.
- Provide NPC allies who can assist with more challenging aspects.
- Alternative Anton is only CR 8 and flees rather than fighting to the death.
- The necromancer relies more on minions than direct confrontation.

### For Higher-Level Parties (Levels 13-15)

- Increase the CR of encounters by 2-3 points.
- Add additional enemies to combat encounters.
- Increase skill check DCs by 2 points.
- The necromancer has already partially merged with an alternative version of himself, granting additional abilities.
- Alternative Anton has fully mastered the staff's powers and can manipulate time during combat.
- Add temporal rifts throughout the adventure that periodically spawn powerful creatures from other timelines.

### Adventure Hooks

1. **For Anton Keene:** Strange temporal disruptions begin affecting Anton's spellcasting, leading to investigation of their source.
2. **For Any Party:** Reports of a powerful necromancer stealing temporal artifacts lead to a bounty posting.
3. **Academic Interest:** Curator Maeven contacts known scholars or adventurers about a break-in at the Grand Archives.
4. **Mysterious Messenger:** A temporal ghost of Durso Preiss appears to the party, warning of imminent danger to the timestream.
5. **Timeline Bleed:** Characters experience visions or memories from alternative timelines, drawing them to investigate Dead Mountain.
6. **Cult Connection:** For characters connected to the Cult of Loz Tdrot, a senior member tasks them with investigating disturbances in the flow of secrets, leading them to Durso's tomb.

### Expanding the Adventure

This module can be expanded into a longer campaign with these additional elements:
- **Alternative Timelines:** Add missions to other timelines through the Splintered Oak.
- **Temporal Preservation Society:** Introduce an organization dedicated to protecting the timeline that may become allies or adversaries depending on the party's choices.
- **Consequences of Choices:** Develop follow-up adventures based on what Anton decided to do with the staff.
- **Other Temporal Artifacts:** The sapphiretail staff could be one of several artifacts created by Durso Preiss, each controlling different aspects of time.
- **The Temporal War:** Alternative Anton could be just one of many alternative versions of people seeking to claim dominance over the prime timeline.
- **Cult of Loz Tdrot:** Explore the cult's deeper involvement with temporal magic and their own plans for the staff.
- **Gemstone Dragons:** Introduce living Sapphire Dragons who sense the disturbances caused by the staff and intervene to protect the timestream.

## Final Notes for the DM

This adventure works best when Anton's player is fully engaged with the personal nature of the story. If your group doesn't include a chronurgy wizard named Anton, consider adapting the story to connect to one of your player characters, perhaps changing the school of magic or the nature of the artifact to better fit their character concept.

The timeline and alternate reality elements provide excellent opportunities for roleplaying and character development. Encourage players to consider how their characters might have turned out differently under alternative circumstances, perhaps even introducing alternative versions of themselves during the Splintered Oak encounter.

Above all, emphasize the weight of choice and consequence. The adventure's central theme of dual paths and potential futures resonates most strongly when players feel their decisions have meaningful impacts on the world and their characters' development.

## Campaign Integration Notes

This adventure can be seamlessly integrated into ongoing campaigns:
- **Forgotten Realms:** Place in the Sword Coast, with Dead Mountain in the Spine of the World and Kvalec as a major city like Waterdeep
- **Eberron:** The Staff could be a Draconic Prophecy artifact, with the Cult of Loz Tdrot as a shadow organization within the Library of Korranberg
- **Critical Role:** Set in Wildemount, with the Cult of Loz Tdrot connected to the Cerberus Assembly and the Academy located near Rexxentrum
- **Original Settings:** Adapt NPCs and locations to fit your world's lore and geography

### Design Philosophy Note:
This adventure emphasizes player agency and choice. The Three Clue Rule ensures players are never stuck, while the branching paths allow for multiple solutions. The ultimate decision rests with Anton's player, making this a deeply personal journey that impacts not just the character but potentially all of reality.
