
*[A letter sealed with dark blue wax bearing the symbol of an eye within a spiral. The letter is addressed to Anton Keene but has never been opened.]*

---

From the Sanctum of Hidden Truths  
CULT OF LOZ TDROT  
*Keeper of Secrets, Master of Hidden Knowledge*

---

To Initiate Anton Keene,

Your progress within our order has been noted with great interest. Your master, Carthach ó Caskill, reports that you have advanced far beyond his expectations, particularly in your unauthorized studies of temporal magic. While we do not typically condone the theft of knowledge, even from fellow members, Loz Tdrot values the pursuit of forbidden secrets above all.

It has come to the attention of the Inner Circle that you are the subject of the Prophecy of Dual Paths. This ancient prediction has long been guarded by our order, as it concerns the very nature of time itself—perhaps the greatest secret of all.

As a servant of Loz Tdrot, you must understand that knowledge is never without consequence. The temporal abilities that flow naturally through your blood—inherited from your ancestor Durso Preiss—place you at a crucial nexus of possibility. The Sapphiretail Staff, crafted from a gem freely given by the time dragon Prismaticus, is both your birthright and your greatest test.

Know this: in countless timelines, versions of yourself make the same choice when confronted with the staff's power. In most, you choose domination over time itself, believing that all knowledge and all secrets can be yours. These versions inevitably seek each other out, merging their consciousness into an increasingly powerful entity that threatens the very fabric of the multiverse.

We have observed a disturbing trend wherein a particularly powerful amalgamation of alternative Antons is systematically absorbing others across timelines. This entity has already consumed three alternative selves and grows exponentially more powerful with each assimilation.

The staff was hidden many years ago by your parents, though they knew nothing of our order. They used the Three Anchors Protocol developed by Durso himself, acting on his instructions to protect the multiverse. They did this not to deny you your destiny, but to ensure you would have the maturity to make the right choice when the time came.

That time approaches rapidly.

Should you discover this letter, seek the Inner Sanctum immediately. The Cult of Loz Tdrot does not typically intervene in matters of fate, preferring instead to observe and record. However, the potential collapse of all timelines into one threatens the very existence of secrets themselves—something our god cannot permit.

May the Hidden Path guide your steps,

Veiled Master Threnon
*Seventh Circle Keeper*
Cult of Loz Tdrot
