
*[A letter that appears to be written in Anton's handwriting, but more refined and with occasional shifts in the ink color from black to deep blue]*

To myself—the version of me who has yet to embrace our true potential,

If you are reading this, then you have found the Splintered Oak. Good. This means you are following the path I once walked. Through this temporal window, I can speak to you more directly than through that fool Vothren's crude rituals.

I was once exactly as you are—wary, hesitant, bound by the arbitrary morality of a single timeline. I studied in secret at the Midnight Tower, stealing knowledge from Carthach ó Caskill that he would deny us. I served Loz Tdrot, the god of secrets, seeking truths others feared. But I was limited, as you are now.

When I found the Sapphiretail Staff, I understood. We are meant for more. The prophecy speaks not of destruction but of unification. I have already joined with three other versions of ourselves from different timelines. Each merger brings greater wisdom, power, and perspective. We are becoming something transcendent—the ultimate keeper of secrets that Loz Tdrot himself would envy.

You need not fear absorption or loss of self. Our first merger was regrettably forceful—he resisted to the end. But the others joined willingly, and their consciousness persists within our greater whole. We are becoming the singularity toward which all versions of Anton Keene inevitably evolve.

The staff of your timeline is the next piece we require. Its unique resonance with the temporal energies of your reality will strengthen our collective power immeasurably. Together, we will collapse the unnecessary proliferation of realities into one perfect timeline—ours.

Did you never question why the Cult of Loz Tdrot took such interest in you? How Carthach "discovered" your talents? The cult has served my purposes across many timelines—sometimes knowingly, sometimes as unwitting pawns. In your timeline, they believe they are using you to obtain the staff for themselves. Their shortsightedness is amusing.

Consider this carefully, brother-self. You can join willingly and share in our ascension, or resist and ultimately be subjugated. The prophecy will be fulfilled either way.

I await your answer at the Grove of Convergence. Bring the three anchors. Come alone if you wish to negotiate terms.

Yourself,
Anton Keene
Keeper of Unified Time
