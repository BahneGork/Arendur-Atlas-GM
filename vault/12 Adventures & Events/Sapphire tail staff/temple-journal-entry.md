**15th of Greenleaf, Year of the Silent Moon**

We have finally secured the first anchor. The Temple's guardian accepted our offering, though not without testing our resolve. Darragh's music soothed the elemental while I engaged it in combat—just like our old adventuring days. The first anchor is secure beneath the statue of the hourglass. Only blood of our line will reveal it now.

The prophecy weighs heavy on my mind. Durso's warnings were clear—someday our bloodline will produce a child with unprecedented temporal sensitivity. This unborn child would be the nexus of power described in the ancient texts. I didn't believe it until I began experiencing the dreams—visions of a son we might someday have, wielding forces that could reshape time itself.

We must hide the staff where neither Vothren nor any other power-hungry mage can find it. The three anchors must remain separated until the prophecy's subject is ready to face the choice with wisdom rather than ambition. When the three anchors are united, the Grove of Convergence will reveal itself once more.
