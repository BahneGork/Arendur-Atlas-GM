*[A weathered map hidden behind a portrait in Durso Preiss's private office at the Academy. The map is drawn on parchment that occasionally shimmers with faint temporal energy.]*

# THE GROVE OF CONVERGENCE

*[The map shows a remote woodland area surrounded by three distinctive mountain peaks forming a perfect triangle. At the center is a clearing in the forest labeled "The Nexus." Various annotations and warnings surround the drawing.]*

**LOCATION:** Between the Three Sentinel Peaks, accessible only via the hidden path that appears when all three anchors are united. Conventional navigation will fail—the Grove exists partially outside normal spacetime.

**APPROACH WITH CAUTION:** The Grove of Convergence exists at a unique juncture where multiple timelines naturally intersect. Under normal circumstances, these intersections remain stable, but temporal artifacts can destabilize the entire nexus.

**WARNING:** Where timelines meet—approach only with all three temporal keys. Attempting entry without proper preparation will result in being lost between realities.

**RITUAL SITE:** The central clearing contains a stone pedestal where the Sapphiretail Staff was first discovered. Ancient standing stones encircle the area, each carved with segments of the prophecy.

**TEMPORAL ANOMALIES:** 
- Time flows differently within the Grove—hours outside may be minutes inside, or vice versa
- Glimpses of alternative timelines become increasingly frequent as one approaches the center
- Temporal echoes of past and future visitors may be encountered
- Reality becomes malleable near the pedestal

**CRITICAL WARNING:** The Grove is inherently unstable. Any use of the staff within its boundaries will have consequences across all connected timelines. DO NOT ATTEMPT TO MERGE TIMELINES HERE.

*[At the bottom of the map is a personal note in Durso's handwriting:]*

I have seen every possible outcome when my descendant confronts his destiny at the Grove. The choice he makes here will determine the fate of all realities. I can only hope that by the time the three anchors are united, he will understand what is truly at stake. Whatever power the staff offers is nothing compared to the responsibility it demands.

The darkest timeline I've glimpsed shows not Vothren, but my own blood corrupted and seeking to collapse all realities into one. If this comes to pass, the three anchors must be used to seal the staff away permanently rather than claim its power.
