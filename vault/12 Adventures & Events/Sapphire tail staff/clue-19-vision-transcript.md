# VISION TRANSCRIPT: PREISS ACADEMY

*[The vision shows a magnificent academy building, now partially in ruins. The scene focuses on the grand entrance hall where a life-sized statue of Durso Preiss stands. The vision then shifts to show a secret chamber beneath the statue.]*

---

*[A younger version of Durso Preiss and two companions—recognizable as Anna and Darragh O'Keene—enter a hidden chamber beneath the Founder's Hall. The vision has no sound, but their actions are clear.]*

Durso places an emerald crystalline key—identical in shape to the sapphire anchor from the Temple, but green—into a receptacle in the wall. The wall slides open to reveal a vault lined with temporal warding symbols.

Anna stands guard while Darragh performs what appears to be a musical sequence on a small harp. As he plays, temporal energy flows around them, and the wards on the vault shimmer in response.

Durso retrieves a scroll from within and opens it to show his companions. The scroll contains a detailed map of the academy with a specific location marked beneath the founder's statue. He points to a sequence of floor tiles surrounding the statue's base.

The three work together to press the tiles in a specific pattern. The statue rotates to reveal a hidden compartment in its pedestal. Durso places the emerald anchor inside, and Darragh adds what appears to be a drop of blood to a small basin beside it.

The anchor begins to glow and then fades from view, though it clearly still exists in the same space—now phased partially out of the current timeline.

Before leaving, Durso inscribes something on the inside of the pedestal. The vision zooms in to show the text:

"𝕿𝖍𝖊 𝖛𝖆𝖚𝖑𝖙 𝖇𝖊𝖓𝖊𝖆𝖙𝖍 𝖒𝖞 𝖔𝖋𝖋𝖎𝖈𝖊 𝖜𝖎𝖑𝖑 𝖔𝖓𝖑𝖞 𝖔𝖕𝖊𝖓 𝖙𝖔 𝖔𝖓𝖊 𝖜𝖍𝖔 𝖈𝖆𝖓 𝖘𝖔𝖑𝖛𝖊 𝖙𝖍𝖊 𝖕𝖆𝖗𝖆𝖉𝖔𝖝 𝖔𝖋 𝖙𝖎𝖒𝖊'𝖘 𝖆𝖗𝖗𝖔𝖜."

*[The vision shows the exact sequence of tiles to press and reveals that a small amount of blood from Durso's lineage must be applied to the hidden basin to retrieve the anchor.]*

---

*[As the vision fades, it briefly shows the current state of the academy—overgrown with vegetation but with the statue still standing in the Founder's Hall.]*
