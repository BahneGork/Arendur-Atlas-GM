**DESCRIPTION:**
The rubbing shows three figures standing side by side, carved in bas-relief. The central figure is a tall, dignified man in elaborate robes holding what appears to be a staff topped with a sapphire gem shaped like a dragon's tail. Intricate runes surround him, and temporal symbols are carved into his robes.

The figure on the left is a woman in armor with a sword at her side. Her stance is protective, and she bears a shield with a unique crest. The figure on the right is a man in lighter attire with what appears to be a musical instrument slung across his back.

Below the image, partially weathered text reads:

"𝕿𝖍𝖊 𝕲𝖚𝖆𝖗𝖉𝖎𝖆𝖓𝖘 𝕿𝖍𝖗𝖊𝖊 — 𝕯𝖚𝖗𝖘𝖔 𝕻𝖗𝖊𝖎𝖘𝖘, 𝕬𝖓𝖓𝖆 𝕷𝖊𝖞𝖑𝖆𝖓𝖉, 𝕯𝖆𝖗𝖗𝖆𝖌𝖍 𝕺'𝕶𝖊𝖊𝖓𝖊 — 𝖂𝖍𝖔 𝖘𝖊𝖆𝖑𝖊𝖉 𝖆𝖜𝖆𝖞 𝖙𝖍𝖊 𝖕𝖔𝖜𝖊𝖗 𝖔𝖋 𝖙𝖎𝖒𝖊 𝖚𝖓𝖙𝖎𝖑 𝖙𝖍𝖊 𝖕𝖗𝖔𝖕𝖍𝖊𝖘𝖎𝖊𝖉 𝖔𝖓𝖊 𝖎𝖘 𝖗𝖊𝖆𝖉𝖞."
