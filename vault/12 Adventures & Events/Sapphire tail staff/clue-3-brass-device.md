*[A small, intricate brass device found among the necromancer's abandoned equipment in Durso's tomb]*

**APPEARANCE:**
The device is approximately the size of a pocket watch but considerably more complex. It consists of multiple nested brass rings that rotate independently, each inscribed with temporal symbols and arcane formulae. The central portion contains a small crystalline chamber filled with swirling mist that occasionally pulses with blue light. Three adjustable dials on the outer edge appear to control its function.

**EFFECTS WHEN ACTIVE:**
When the device pulses (which happens at irregular intervals), anyone within 10 feet experiences brief, disorienting glimpses of alternative timelines. These visions last only seconds but show events that never happened in this reality—some showing minor differences, others depicting drastically different versions of the present.

**ARCANA CHECK RESULTS (DC 14):**
This is a crude prototype of a "Temporal Window Locator"—a device designed to find weak points between timelines. It appears the necromancer was using it to identify locations where the barriers between realities are thinnest. The erratic pulsing suggests it's malfunctioning or damaged.

Notably, the largest pulse produces a specific vision of a massive oak tree split down the middle, with shimmering energy visible between the split halves. Coordinates scratched onto the back of the device correspond to a location in the wilderness known as the Whispering Woods.

**ADDITIONAL NOTES:**
The craftsmanship is distinctive—combining elements of necromancy with temporal magic. The design appears to have been reverse-engineered from something more sophisticated, with certain components showing signs of improvisation.

*[If contained or deactivated successfully, the device goes dormant. If left unchecked, it will continue to cause random temporal disturbances that increase in frequency and intensity.]*
