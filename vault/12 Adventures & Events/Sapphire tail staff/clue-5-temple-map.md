## THE TEMPLE OF ETERNAL MOMENT
*Illustration and Notes by Durso Preiss*

*[The page shows a detailed map of a temple built into a mountainside. The structure follows the natural contours of the rock face but contains clearly artificial chambers and passages. Various annotations and symbols mark specific locations.]*

**PRIMARY ENTRANCE:** Eastern approach, marked by twin obelisks that cast shadows forming a perfect doorway outline at equinox sunrise.

**CENTRAL CHAMBER:** Circular hall with apertures cut through the mountain to allow sunlight to enter at precise angles. The light tracks across the inlaid floor throughout the day, marking the passage of time. At the center stands a massive hourglass statue (15 ft tall) filled with glowing temporal sand that appears to flow both upward and downward simultaneously.

**HIDDEN COMPARTMENT:** *[This notation is written in a nearly invisible ink, visible only under special archive lamps]* The statue's base contains a secret chamber accessible only when the correct temporal frequency is applied. The first anchor lies within, responding only to the bloodline signature of the Guardians.

**WARNING:** The Temple is protected by ancient constructs attuned to temporal energy. They will activate if unauthorized individuals attempt to disrupt the temporal flows or access protected areas. The Guardian Elemental that resides within the hourglass itself exists partially outside normal time—conventional weapons may prove ineffective.

**HISTORICAL SIGNIFICANCE:** It was here that I first discovered traces of the Sapphiretail Staff's power. The residual temporal energy patterns led me to theorize the existence of alternative timelines and eventually to the prophecy itself. The temple predates known civilization and appears to have been built by those who first communed with the Sapphire Dragons.
