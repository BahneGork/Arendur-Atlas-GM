# Dead Mountain Raid - GM Cheat Sheet - Street Obstacles & Church

### **Passive Checks**
- **Perception DC 13**: Notice movement, disadv. dim light [[Vision and Light]]
- **Investigation DC 14**: Spot organized undead positioning 
- **Insight DC 12**: Recognize ghast is controlling other undead
- **Arcana DC 16**: Detect necromantic energy connecting undead to cemetery
## USEFUL DCs
- **Climb rooftops:** DC 13 Athletics
- **Slippery cobblestones:** DC 12 Dex save or fall prone
- **Spot civilians in Milly Bell:** DC 15 Perception
- **Rally civilians:** DC 15 Persuasion (bonus action, +10 feet for 1 round)

### **Environmental Features**
- **Overturned Carts**: Half-cover (+5 AC and Dex saves)
- **Oil Barrels**: Spilled oil creates difficult terrain in 15-foot radius
  - If ignited (DC 10 fire damage source): 2d6 fire damage, burns for 1d4 rounds
- **Narrow Alleyways**: Two 5-foot wide flanking routes
- **Rooftops**: 10-15 feet high, DC 13 Athletics to climb
- **Rain-Slick Cobblestones**: DC 13 Acrobatics for sharp movements or running, moving diagonally or running. 

## ESCORT MISSION
**15 min** to move 300 feet to church

### Civilian Rules (Simplified)
- **12 civilians** move as one group, 3 children (6-10)
- **Formation**: Civilians occupy 10x10 foot area, party must stay within 30 feet to protect
- **20 feet per round** (normal speed)
- **Party must stay within 30 feet** or civilians stop
- **If civilians take damage:** movement halved for 2 rounds
- **Loud explosions/roars:** civilians freeze for 1 round

### **Escort Complications**

#### **Panic Conditions**
- **Loud Combat**: Thunder damage, explosions, roars cause group to freeze 1 round
- **Civilian Injury**: Any civilian damage halves group movement for 2 rounds  
- **Separation**: If party moves >30 feet away, civilians stop until rejoined

#### **Morale Boosts**
- **Successful Protection**: No civilian damage grants +5 feet movement for 3 rounds
- **Leadership**: DC 15 Persuasion (bonus action) grants +10 feet for 1 round
- **Healing**: Treating injured civilians restores normal movement speed

## Lysandra Roleplay
- Former temple defender, now corrupted
- *"I defended this place once... now I claim it for the dark!"*
- Hates holy symbols and divine casters
