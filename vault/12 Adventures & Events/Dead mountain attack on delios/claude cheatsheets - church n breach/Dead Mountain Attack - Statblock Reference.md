# Boss-Tier Enemies

## Sister Lysandra (Oathrot Wraith) - CR 6 (2,300 XP)
**AC** 15 | **HP** 82 | **Speed** 0 ft, fly 60 ft (hover)  
**STR** 18 | **DEX** 14 | **CON** 15 | **INT** 12 | **WIS** 16 | **CHA** 18

**Saving Throws** Wis +6  
**Damage Resistances** Acid, cold, fire, lightning, thunder; bludgeoning, piercing, slashing from nonmagical attacks  
**Damage Immunities** Necrotic, poison  
**Condition Immunities** Charmed, exhaustion, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone, restrained  
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft, passive Perception 12

**Actions:**
- **Necrotic Sword**: +8 to hit, reach 5 ft, 4d8+2 necrotic damage
- **Broken Vows**: One creature within 30 ft makes DC 15 Cha save or become cursed. Cursed creatures have disadvantage on CON saves for concentration and can't add proficiency bonus to ability checks and saves.

**Features:**
- **Incorporeal Movement**: Can move through creatures/objects as difficult terrain (5 force damage if ending turn inside object)
- **Desecrated Ground**: Within 20 feet of Lysandra, healing spells are halved
- **Sunlight Sensitivity**: Disadvantage on attack rolls and Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight while in sunlight
- **Chained to their Oaths**: If convinced to read her oath-book and reaffirm her vows, the curse ends and her soul is freed

**Roleplay Notes:**
- Former Sister of Silence who died defending the temple
- *"I defended this place once... now I claim it for the dark!"*
- Becomes enraged at holy symbols and divine casters

- **Magic Weapons**: Attacks are magical

# Quick Combat Reference

## Challenge Rating Summary
- **CR 1/4 (50 XP)**: Skeletons, Zombies - Basic minions
- **CR 2 (450 XP)**: Ghasts - Mid-tier commanders  
- **CR 3 (700 XP)**: Wights - Elite undead
- **CR 5 (1,800 XP)**: Flesh Golem - Heavy bruiser
- **CR 6 (2,300 XP)**: Sister Lysandra - Encounter boss
- **CR 8 (3,900 XP)**: Cyclops - Final boss

## Common Undead Immunities
**All undead share:**
- Immune to poison damage and poisoned condition
- Immune to exhaustion (except skeletons/zombies)

## Tactical Patterns
- **Skeletons**: Ranged harassment, use cover
- **Zombies**: Slow advance, rely on Undead Fortitude
- **Ghasts**: Commander role, stench area denial
- **Wights**: Life drain focusing on isolated targets
- **Flesh Golem**: Berserker when bloodied, lightning heals
- **Sister Lysandra**: Flying terror, desecrated ground, incorporeal movement
- **Cyclops**: Area control with minion support, reanimation command

## Damage Type Notes
- **Bludgeoning**: Extra effective against skeletons (vulnerability)
- **Necrotic**: Many undead resist, some immune
- **Radiant**: Standard effectiveness (no special undead weakness)
- **Lightning**: Heals Flesh Golem completely
- **Fire**: Causes Flesh Golem disadvantage on attacks
