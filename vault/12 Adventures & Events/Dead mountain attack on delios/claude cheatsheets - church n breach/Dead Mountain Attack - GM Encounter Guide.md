# Encounter 1: Clear Path to Church
**Type**: Minor Objective | **XP**: 450 per character | **Duration**: 30-45 minutes

## Mission Brief
12 civilians are trapped behind overturned carts near The Milly Bell. They need escort to Gaed's Sanctuary (300 feet away) where Father Belderone can activate protective wards.

### Critical Plot Element
- **Jorik's corpse** in street contains **Baron Hexton's documents** (political subplot)

## Stage 1: Breaking the Barricade
**Enemies (CR 6 total)**:
- 6 Skeleton Archers (behind 3/4 cover, AC 18 with cover)
- 4 Zombies (street level around barricade) 
- 1 Ghast (commander giving simple orders)

**Environmental Features**:
- Overturned carts provide half-cover (+2 AC, +2 Dex saves)
- Oil barrels create 15ft difficult terrain if spilled
- Rain-slick cobblestones: DC 12 Acrobatics for sharp movements

## Stage 2: Civilian Escort Mission

### The Civilian Group
- **Total**: 12 civilians (includes 3 children ages 6-10)
- **Movement**: 20 feet/round as clustered group
- **Must stay within 30 feet of party** or civilians stop moving
- **Route**: 300 feet northwest to church (15 rounds normal speed)

### Ongoing Threat: Rooftop Harassment
- **4 Skeleton Archers** pursue along rooftops
- Range 80/320 feet, +4 to hit, 1d8+2 piercing
- Focus fire on exposed civilians, relocate when engaged

### Three Timed Ambushes

#### Ambush 1 (Rounds 5-6)
**Location**: Narrow residential street
- **Enemies**: 1 Ghast + 4 Zombies from damaged houses
- **Special**: Ghast's stench affects whole civilian group (DC 10 Con save, stunned 1 round)

#### Ambush 2 (Rounds 10-11) 
**Location**: Four-way intersection
- **Enemies**: 1 Wight + 6 Zombies surrounding from side streets
- **Special**: Wight targets children with life drain; civilians scatter 10 feet randomly

#### Ambush 3 (Rounds 14-15) - FINAL BOSS
**Location**: Church approach (50 feet from doors)
- **Primary Enemy**: Sister Lysandra (Oathrot Wraith, CR 6)
- **Support**: 3 Wights + 6 Skeletons
- **Terrain**: Desecrated ground within 20 feet of Lysandra (healing spells halved)

### Sister Lysandra Roleplay
- Former Sister of Silence who died defending this temple
- *"I defended this place once... now I claim it for the dark!"*
- *"The Silver Flame burns no more - only cold death remains!"*
- Becomes enraged at holy symbols and divine casters
- **Special Mechanic**: Chained to her oath-book - if convinced to read and reaffirm her oaths, curse ends

### Victory Conditions
- **Complete Success** (0 casualties): +2 Town Tracker, reputation boost, Father Belderone's blessing
- **Partial Success** (1-2 casualties): +1 Town Tracker 
- **Poor Success** (3+ casualties): -1 Town Tracker, reputation damage

# Encounter 2: Western Palisade Breach
**Type**: Major Encounter | **XP**: 1200 | **Duration**: 45-60 minutes

## Timeline & Hook
*10-15 minutes after securing church, thunderous CRACK from western lumber district*

**Father Belderone**: *"That came from the lumber district! Someone's blown a hole in our defenses!"*

## What Really Happened
**Miha** (assassin) used alchemical explosives to create strategic breach:
1. Draw undead horde into town for maximum chaos
2. Force heroes to respond, creating ambush opportunity
3. Create personal escape route if needed

## Approaching the Breach
*Half-timbered houses show hasty abandonment. Acrid smell of explosives mingles with rain-soaked timber and death. A jagged, smoking 20-foot gap in the palisade - too precise for undead work.*

### Evidence of Sabotage (Investigation Opportunities)
- **DC 12**: Precisely placed explosives, deliberate sabotage
- **DC 15**: Alchemical residue suggests stolen local supplies, knowledge of construction
- **Perception DC 14**: Fresh boot prints, small precise steps
- **Insight DC 13**: Timing too perfect - waited for undead attack as cover

## The Breach Combat

### Enemies
- **1 Flesh Golem** (drawn by explosion)
- **12 Skeletons** (pouring through in waves)
- **4 Wight Archers** (positioning on warehouse rooftops)
- **2 Ghasts** (attracted by concentrated death energy)

### Tactical Setup
- Undead pour through 3-4 per round for first 3 rounds
- Wight archers take rooftop positions on lumber warehouses
- Every 3 rounds, 1d4 additional skeletons drawn to commotion

### Environmental Elements
- **Rain/Wind**: Light obscurement, ranged attacks disadvantage beyond 60ft
- **Explosive Debris**: Difficult terrain in 15ft radius around breach
- **Unstable Palisade**: Sections can collapse (DC 15 Athletics, 2d6 damage in 10ft line)
- **Lumber Stacks**: Can be toppled for cover or improvised weapons (1d4 damage)
- **Alchemical Residue**: Slippery patches near blast site

### Momentum Opportunities
- Use unstable palisade sections against clustered enemies
- Quick evidence gathering reveals Miha's methods
- Explosion scattered useful materials (rope, tools, lumber)
- Gaining rooftop control provides tactical advantage
- Fast breach sealing reduces undead reinforcements

## Post-Combat: Cemetery Discovery
*With breach secured, sickly green pillar of light visible 200 yards toward cemetery. Massive silhouette moves within luminescence. Red moon Krisma pulses in rhythm with green light.*

## Emrik Varrow's Arrival
*Disciplined footsteps through rain. Emrik appears with three of Lord Castilla's guards in formation.*

**Emrik's Assessment**: *"Professional sabotage. They made a mistake - revealed the real threat."* (Points to cemetery light)

### Player Choice: Emrik's Role
- **Tactical Coordinator**: Accompanies party to cemetery
- **Town Defense**: Organizes remaining defenses and evacuation
- **Pursuit Team**: Tracks saboteur with guards

# Encounter 3: Graveyard Battle - Final Showdown
**Type**: Climactic Encounter | **XP**: 1350 | **Duration**: 60+ minutes

## The Approach
*Sickly green light emanates from central grave pit. Tombstones and mausoleums create maze-like battlefield. Undead emerge from ground itself.*

### Primary Enemy: Cyclops Necro-Tyrant (Boss)
- Holds Amulet of Necromantic Tyranny
- **When defeated or loses amulet**: All undead turn to dust
- 30ft Necrotic Miasma aura: DC 15 Con save or 7 necrotic damage

### Supporting Forces
- 5 Skeletons, 2 Shadows, 2 Wights
- 1 Oathrot Wraith (lieutenant)
- **Grave Pit Spawning**: Initiative 20, roll 1d4
  - 1: 2 Ghouls
  - 2: 3 Skeletons  
  - 3: 3 Zombies
  - 4: 1 Wight

### Battlefield Features
- **Tombstones/Mausoleums**: 1/2 to 3/4 cover
- **Uneven Ground**: Difficult terrain throughout
- **Central Grave Pit**: Undead spawn point every round
- **Seal Option**: Mausoleum door blocks pit (DC 18 Strength)

### Tactical Notes
- Cyclops wears **Javelin of Lightning**
- **Reanimation Command**: Bonus action to revive destroyed undead at 50% HP
- **Corpse Bolt** (Recharge 5-6): 60ft range, 8d6 necrotic, 10ft radius, DC 15 Dex half

# GM's Toolkit

## Civilian Management (Encounters 1 & 2)
### Formation Rules
- 12 civilians occupy 10x10 foot area
- Party must stay within 30 feet or civilians stop
- 20 feet/round normal movement

### Panic Conditions
- **Loud combat/explosions**: Group freezes 1 round
- **Civilian injury**: Movement halved 2 rounds
- **Separation from party**: Stop until rejoined

### Morale Boosts
- **No civilian damage**: +5 feet movement for 3 rounds
- **Leadership** (DC 15 Persuasion bonus action): +10 feet for 1 round
- **Healing injured**: Restores normal movement speed

## Town Defense Tracker Management
**Starting Value**: 5 (palisade and two towers)

### Success Thresholds
- **0**: Town fallen
- **1-4**: Partial failure
- **5-7**: Partial success  
- **8-10**: Full success

### Tracker Modifiers
- **Full encounter success**: +2
- **Partial encounter success**: +1
- **Partial encounter failure**: -1
- **Full encounter failure**: -2
- **Important NPC casualty**: Additional -1
- **Critical location overrun**: Additional -1

## Pacing & Flow
- **Keep civilians moving** - don't let escort bog down
- **Balance protection with engagement** - make threats meaningful but not overwhelming
- **Encourage creative solutions** - reward tactical thinking
- **Track rounds flexibly** - maintain tension without rigid timing
- **Lysandra as dramatic climax** - make her memorable and tragic
