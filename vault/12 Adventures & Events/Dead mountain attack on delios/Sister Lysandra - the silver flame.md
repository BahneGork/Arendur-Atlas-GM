#### **Oathrot Wraith - Lady Sister Lysandra the Silver Flame**

**Background**: Former Sister of the silver flame, who fought alongside the Black Eagle Knight during the Orc Wars. Died defending this very temple, now corrupted into an oathrot wraith. She was manipulated into breaking her oath and causing tragedy by a devil. 

**Stats** (CR 6):
- **AC**: 15 (Natural Armor), **HP**: 82 (11d10 + 22)
- **Speed**: 0 ft., fly 60 ft. (hover)
- **Resistances**: Acid, cold, fire, lightning, thunder; bludgeoning, piercing, slashing from nonmagical attacks
- **Immunities**: Necrotic, poison
- **Condition Immunities**: Charmed, exhaustion, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone, restrained
- **Senses**: darkvision 60ft., passive perception 12
- proficiency +2

**Abilities**:
- **Necrotic Sword**: +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., 4d8+2 necrotic
- **Broken Vows**: DC 13 charisma, begins within 30ft, become cursed -> disadv. CON saves concentration & can't add prof.bonus to ability checks and saves. success = immune for 24hours
- **Incorporeal Movement**: Can move through creatures and objects as difficult terrain (5 force damage if ending turn inside object)
- **Sunlight sensitivity**: disadv. attacks, wisdome perception (sight).
- **Chained to their Oaths**: Oathrot wraiths stand out from other wraiths by the texts that are tethered to them. These tomes bear the oaths they once swore and later betrayed. If a wraith chooses to read the text and reaffirm its oaths, its curse is ended and its soul is freed from undeath. However, most oathrot wraiths are too consumed by self-loathing, pride, or anger to read the texts, seeing the books as constant reminders of their failure.

**Roleplay Elements**:

- _"I defended this place once... now I claim it for the dark!"_
- _"The Silver Flame burns no more - only cold death remains!"_
- Recognizes holy symbols and becomes enraged at clerics/paladins
- Father Belderone may recognize her name from temple history

**Tactical Notes**:

- **Shattered Faith**: Her presence makes church doors harder to open
- **Protective Instinct Twisted**: Specifically targets anyone approaching church doors
- **Historical Connection**: She and the Black Eagle Knight were comrades-in-arms
