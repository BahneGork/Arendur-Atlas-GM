# The Cemetery's Wrath
 
## A Boss Encounter for 5 Level 8 Characters at the Town Cemetery

## Background Context

The necromancer came to Dead Mountain seeking an old tomb and knowledge. Rather than fight the cyclops [[Thurumbaba]] who made his lair there, he offered the "gift" of a powerful amulet in exchange for safe passage. The amulet indeed granted [[Thurumbaba]] control over the dead, but it also slowly corrupted his mind and soul.

After finding what he sought, [[THE necromancer]] departed, leaving behind an unwitting servant in [[Thurumbaba]]. The heroes came to Dead Mountain, where they defeated 40 ghouls and an undead hill giant with terrible necrotic powers— [[Thurumbaba]]'s most prized creation.

Now the cyclops seeks revenge, but the amulet's influence drives him to more extreme measures than he might otherwise take.

## Encounter Statistics

**Difficulty:** Hard to Deadly (For 5 level 8 characters)

### [[Thurumbaba]], Corrupted Cyclops
*Huge giant, chaotic evil*

**Armor Class** 15 (natural armor)
**Hit Points** 225 (21d10 + 105)
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR     | DEX     | CON     | INT    | WIS    | CHA     |
| ------- | ------- | ------- | ------ | ------ | ------- |
| 22 (+6) | 11 (+0) | 20 (+5) | 8 (-1) | 6 (-2) | 10 (+0) |
**Saving Throws** Con +9, Wis +6, Cha +7
**Skills** Intimidation +7, Perception +6
**Senses** passive Perception 16
**Languages** Giant, Common
**Challenge** 10 

***Necrotic Amulet.*** An amulet with a sickly green gemstone is embedded in [[Thurumbaba]]'s chest. The amulet can be targeted separately, with disadvantage (AC 16, 30 HP) and has resistance to all damage except radiant. If destroyed, [[Thurumbaba]] loses his Necrotic Surge and Mass Reanimate abilities and takes 4d10 psychic damage.

***Corrupted Form.*** When [[Thurumbaba]] drops below half his hit points, the amulet's power surges through him. His eye glows with the same green energy seen in the skeletons, and his attacks deal an additional 8 necrotic damage.

**Actions:**

***Multiattack.*** Thurumbaba makes two attacks with his greatclub.

***Greatclub.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +10 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 19 (2d8 + 10) bludgeoning damage plus 4 (1d8) necrotic damage.

***Necrotic Surge (Recharge 5-6).*** The amulet releases a wave of green energy. Each creature in a 30-foot cone must make a DC 16 Constitution saving throw, taking 21 (6d6) necrotic damage on a failed save, or half (10) as much on a successful one. Undead creatures in the area instead regain hit points equal to half the necrotic damage dealt.

#### Villain actions
***Command Undead (1/Encounter after a player turn).*** Thurumbaba directs one undead creature he can see within 60 feet. The creature can use its reaction to move up to its speed or make one attack.

***Mass Reanimate (1/Encounter after a player turn).*** Thurumbaba channels the amulet's full power into the ground. 2d4 corpses within 60 feet burst from their graves as skeletons with glowing green eyes. They act on Thurumbaba's initiative but after his turn.

#### Legendary Actions

Thurumbaba can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Thurumbaba regains spent legendary actions at the start of his turn.

***Crushing Strike.*** Thurumbaba makes one greatclub attack.

***Life Drain (Costs 2 Actions).*** Thurumbaba targets one creature he can see within 30 feet. The target must make a DC 16 Constitution saving throw, taking 10 (3d6) necrotic damage on a failed save. Thurumbaba regains hit points equal to the damage dealt.

***Summon Shadow Tendrils (Costs 2 Actions).*** Shadow tendrils erupt from the ground in a 10-foot radius centered on a point Thurumbaba can see within 60 feet. Each creature in that area must succeed on a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw or take 10 (3d6) necrotic damage and be restrained. A restrained creature can use its action to make a DC 14 Strength check to free itself.

### Green-Eyed Skeletons (4-6)
*Medium undead, neutral evil*

**Armor Class** 13 (armor scraps)
**Hit Points** 26 (4d8 + 8)
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 10 (+0) | 14 (+2) | 15 (+2) | 6 (-2) | 8 (-1) | 5 (-3) |

**Damage Vulnerabilities** bludgeoning
**Damage Immunities** poison
**Condition Immunities** exhaustion, poisoned
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 9
**Languages** understands commands in Giant but can't speak
**Challenge** 1 (200 XP)

***Necromantic Link.*** When a green-eyed skeleton within 30 feet of Thurumbaba is reduced to 0 hit points, Thurumbaba takes 5 necrotic damage that ignores resistance and immunity.

***Eerie Glow.*** The skeleton's eyes emit dim green light in a 10-foot radius.

**Actions:**

***Shortsword.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) necrotic damage.

***Necrotic Gaze (Recharge 5-6).*** The skeleton's eyes flare brightly. Each creature within a 15-foot cone must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution saving throw or take 7 (2d6) necrotic damage and have disadvantage on its next attack roll.

### Revenant Gravediggers (2-3)
*Medium undead, neutral evil*

**Armor Class** 12 (tattered clothes)
**Hit Points** 45 (6d8 + 18)
**Speed** 20 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 16 (+3) | 10 (+0) | 16 (+3) | 3 (-4) | 6 (-2) | 5 (-3) |

**Damage Immunities** poison
**Condition Immunities** exhaustion, poisoned
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 8
**Languages** understands the languages it knew in life but can't speak
**Challenge** 2 (450 XP)

***Gravekeeper's Resolve.*** If damage reduces the gravedigger to 0 hit points, it must make a Constitution saving throw with a DC of 5 + the damage taken, unless the damage is radiant or from a critical hit. On a success, the gravedigger drops to 1 hit point instead.

***Burial Tools.*** The gravedigger carries assorted burial tools that it uses as weapons. Some carry shovels (as greatclubs), others picks or hand scythes.

**Actions:**

***Multiattack.*** The gravedigger makes two attacks with its burial tools.

***Burial Tool.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (2d6 + 3) bludgeoning or slashing damage (depending on the tool).

***Grasping Earth (Recharge 5-6).*** The gravedigger stomps the ground, causing the soil in a 10-foot radius around it to shift and grasp at creatures. Each creature in that area must succeed on a DC 13 Strength saving throw or be restrained until the end of its next turn.

### Shadow Tendrils
*Medium undead, neutral evil*

**Armor Class** 12
**Hit Points** 18 (4d8)
**Speed** 0 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 6 (-2) | 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 1 (-5) | 10 (+0) | 1 (-5) |

**Damage Resistances** acid, cold, fire, lightning, thunder; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks
**Damage Immunities** necrotic, poison
**Condition Immunities** charmed, exhaustion, frightened, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone, restrained
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10
**Challenge** 1/2 (100 XP)

***Shadow Stealth.*** While in dim light or darkness, the shadow tendril can take the Hide action as a bonus action.

***Sunlight Weakness.*** While in sunlight, the shadow tendril has disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.

***Limited Existence.*** A shadow tendril is anchored to the spot where it emerged and cannot move from that space. It lasts for 1 minute before dissipating back into the earth.

**Actions:**

***Strength Drain.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 10 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 9 (2d6 + 2) necrotic damage, and the target's Strength score is reduced by 1d4. The target dies if this reduces its Strength to 0. Otherwise, the reduction lasts until the target finishes a short or long rest. If a non-evil humanoid dies from this attack, a new shadow rises from the corpse 1d4 hours later.

## Encounter Phases and Tactics

### Phase 1: The Desecration
When the party arrives at the cemetery, they witness Thurumbaba in the middle of a dark ritual. Several graves have been exhumed, and green-eyed skeletons patrol while revenant gravediggers continue disinterring more corpses.

#### **Initial Positioning:**
- Thurumbaba stands in the central ceremonial area, completing a ritual circle
- Gravediggers work at fresh graves
- Skeletons patrol the perimeter
- The party likely approaches from the town side (south)

#### **Thurumbaba's Tactics:**
- Attempts to complete the ritual (requires 3 more rounds)
- Commands undead to intercept and delay the party
- Uses Necrotic Surge if multiple party members group together

#### **Minion Tactics:**
- Skeletons use ranged attacks from behind tombstones
- Gravediggers attempt to restrain party members with Grasping Earth

### Phase 2: Direct Confrontation
Once the ritual is interrupted or Thurumbaba is attacked:

**Thurumbaba's Tactics:**
- Engages strongest-looking opponents directly
- Uses Command Undead to coordinate attacks
- Prioritizes spellcasters who might have radiant damage

**Minion Tactics:**
- Two skeletons always try to stay near Thurumbaba to enhance his attacks
- Gravediggers focus on grappling and restraining mobile characters

### Phase 3: Amulet Ascendancy
When Thurumbaba drops below 50% HP:

**Transformation:** The amulet flares with intense green energy that courses through Thurumbaba's body. His eye glows the same eerie green as the skeletons'. His attacks now deal additional necrotic damage.

**Thurumbaba's Tactics:**
- Uses Mass Reanimate to summon reinforcements
- Becomes more aggressive, using legendary actions for damage
- Uses Summon Shadow Tendrils to control battlefield areas

**Minion Tactics:**
- Newly risen skeletons prioritize weaker party members
- Shadow tendrils target characters with high Strength scores

## Environment and Terrain Features

### Cemetery Layout
- **Central Ceremonial Area:** Slightly elevated stone platform with partially completed ritual markings
- **Mausoleums:** Two larger structures providing full cover
- **Grave Rows:** Organized paths between rows of tombstones
- **Caretaker's Chapel:** Small building with tools and a bell tower
- **Iron Fence:** Surrounds the cemetery, with main gates to the south (town side)

### Tactical Elements
- **Tombstones:** Provide half cover
- **Mausoleums:** Can be entered (doors DC 13 Strength to force open if locked)
- **Exhumed Graves:** Difficult terrain, 5-10 ft. deep pits
- **Corrupted Ground:** Areas glowing with faint green energy deal 1d6 necrotic damage to good-aligned creatures that start their turn there
- **Ritual Circle:** If completed, would allow Thurumbaba to raise twice as many undead; can be disrupted with holy water or radiant damage

### Interactive Features
- **Chapel Bell:** Can be rung (requires an action) to cause all undead within 60 feet to make a DC 13 Wisdom save or be stunned until the end of their next turn
- **Holy Water Font:** Contains enough holy water for 1d4 vials
- **Grave Soil:** Loose piles that can be toppled to create difficult terrain (DC 10 Strength check)
- **Ancient Tree:** Can be climbed (DC 12 Athletics) to gain high ground

### Environmental Effects
- **Evening Mist:** Provides light obscurement in low areas
- **Green Wisps:** Small manifestations of necromantic energy that indicate where shadow tendrils might appear
- **Unearthly Sounds:** Moans and whispers create an unsettling atmosphere and might mask the approach of enemies

## Runtime Dynamics

### Triggering the Encounter
- The party might hear screams from the town as the undead raid begins
- Townsfolk could alert them that something strange is happening at the cemetery
- The heroes might see an eerie green glow emanating from the graveyard

### Escalation
- If Thurumbaba completes his ritual before being interrupted, double the number of skeletons he can summon with Mass Reanimate
- For each round the battle continues, 1d4 zombies might appear at the cemetery gates, drawn by the necromantic energy
- Townsfolk might arrive to help or need rescuing if they foolishly came to investigate

### Retreat and Pursuit
- If reduced to 50 HP or fewer, Thurumbaba might attempt to flee toward Dead Mountain
- Fleeing might be part of the amulet's programming, drawing the heroes to another encounter site
- If Thurumbaba escapes, the remaining undead become frenzied and fight to the death

## Treasure and Rewards

### Immediate Loot
- **The Necromancer's Amulet:** If not destroyed during battle, this becomes a magic item:
  - *Amulet of Undead Control*: Allows the wearer to cast Animate Dead once per day without requiring material components. While wearing it, the user can use a bonus action to command one undead creature they can see within 60 feet. Each time the amulet is used, the wearer must make a DC 15 Charisma saving throw or be influenced by the necromancer's lingering control (essentially receiving a suggestion spell effect to further the necromancer's goals).

- **Thurumbaba's Eye:** Can be preserved and sold to certain alchemists or wizards interested in giant physiology (250 gp).

### Hidden Treasures
- **Caretaker's Chapel:** Hidden beneath a loose floorboard is an iron box containing emergency funds for cemetery upkeep (75 gp, 130 sp).
- **Fresh Noble's Grave:** One of the recently exhumed graves belonged to a local noble who was buried with a gold ring set with a small diamond (250 gp).

## Running the Encounter

### Adjusting Difficulty
- **Easier:** Reduce Thurumbaba's hit points to 180, remove one gravedigger
- **Harder:** Add 2-3 more skeletons, give Thurumbaba resistance to nonmagical weapons
- **Adding Tension:** Include trapped townsfolk hiding in mausoleums who need rescuing

### Roleplaying Thurumbaba
Before his corruption, Thurumbaba was a territorial but reasonable creature. Now, the amulet's influence has twisted his mind toward cruelty and destruction. His speech alternates between moments of clarity and rage-filled declarations.

**Sample Dialogue:**
- *"Your town will feed my army of bones!"*
- *(Momentary clarity)* "What... what is this power making me do?"
- *"The master promised me strength... he delivered!"*
- *(When amulet takes control)* "ALL WILL SERVE IN DEATH!"

### Dramatic Moments
- When first damaged, have Thurumbaba clutch at the amulet as if in pain
- When dropping to half health, describe the amulet taking greater control
- If a character lands a critical hit, describe them cracking the amulet slightly
- When destroyed, have the amulet release a torrent of trapped souls
