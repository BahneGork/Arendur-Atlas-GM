# Dead Mountain Attack: The Western Breach

## Timeline & Setup

### The Explosion
*About 10-15 minutes after securing the church, a thunderous CRACK echoes from the western side of town, followed by the groaning of splintering timber and distant screams.*

**Father Belderone's Reaction:** *"That came from the lumber district! Someone's blown a hole in our defenses - but who would do such a thing during an attack?"*

**Passive Perception DC 12:** The sounds of undead moaning and shuffling are now coming more intensely from the western direction - they're being drawn to the explosion.

**Passive Investigation DC 15:** That wasn't the sound of undead breaking through - that was deliberate destruction, precisely timed.

## What Actually Happened

Miha used alchemical explosives to blow a strategic hole in the western palisade. Her opportunistic goals:
1. **Draw the undead horde** toward the breach and into town for maximum chaos
2. **Force the heroes** to respond to the breach, creating an ambush opportunity  
3. **Create an escape route** for herself if needed
4. **Sow additional chaos** to cover her assassination attempt

*This is not coordination with Thurumbaba - this is an assassin exploiting a crisis.*

## Approaching the Breach

*The half-timbered houses of the lumber district show signs of hasty abandonment - doors left ajar, belongings scattered in muddy streets. The acrid smell of alchemical explosives mingles with the earthy scent of rain-soaked timber and the growing stench of death. Through the driving rain, you can see a jagged, smoking gap in the wooden palisade wall - but this destruction is too precise, too clean to be the work of shambling undead.*

## The Breach Encounter
**Major Encounter - 1200 XP**

### Enemies
- **1 Flesh Golem** (drawn by the explosion)
- **12 Skeletons** (pouring through in larger numbers due to the breach)
- **4 Wight Archers** (taking positions on warehouse rooftops)
- **2 Ghasts** (attracted by the concentrated death energy)

### Tactical Setup
- The blast created a 20-foot wide breach with unstable edges
- Undead pour through in waves of 3-4 per round for the first 3 rounds
- Flesh Golem lumber forward, drawn by the noise and activity
- Wight archers position themselves on lumber warehouse rooftops
- Ghasts lurk among explosive debris and lumber stacks

### Environmental Elements
- **Rain and Wind:** Light obscurement, ranged attacks at disadvantage beyond 60 feet
- **Explosive Debris:** Scattered around breach (difficult terrain in 15-foot radius)
- **Unstable Palisade:** Sections could collapse with enough force (Athletics DC 15, 2d6 damage in 10-foot line)
- **Lumber Stacks:** Can be toppled for cover or weapons (1d4 improvised damage)
- **Warehouse Access:** Ladders and loading areas provide rooftop access
- **Alchemical Residue:** Slippery and potentially flammable patches near the blast site

## Evidence of Sabotage

**Investigation DC 12:** The explosion was precisely placed to maximize structural damage - this was deliberate sabotage timed with the undead attack.

**Investigation DC 15:** Alchemical residue and blast patterns suggest someone with knowledge of explosives and the palisade's construction. Possibly stolen from local sources.

**Perception DC 14:** Fresh boot prints in the mud near the blast site - small, precise steps that don't match undead movement. Someone was here very recently.

**Insight DC 13:** The timing is too perfect - whoever did this waited for the undead attack to provide cover for their sabotage.

**Medicine DC 15:** The undead show no coordination with whoever created this breach - they're simply drawn to the noise and activity.

## Inertia Elements (Opportunistic Chaos)
- Undead pour through in greater numbers than they could have managed naturally
- The explosion damaged nearby warehouse supports, creating unstable structures
- Miha's sabotage makes the breach more difficult to seal quickly
- Every 3 rounds, 1d4 additional skeletons are drawn to the commotion
- Rain is washing alchemical residue around, making footing treacherous

## Momentum Opportunities (Rewards for Smart Play)
- **Blast Site Tactics:** Using unstable palisade sections against clustered enemies
- **Evidence Trail:** Quick investigation reveals Miha's involvement and methods
- **Structural Advantages:** Explosion scattered useful materials (rope, tools, lumber) for creative solutions
- **Rooftop Control:** Gaining high ground provides significant tactical advantage
- **Quick Sealing:** Fast action to block the breach reduces undead reinforcements


## Miha's Ambush Opportunity

### Her Potential Positions
- **Warehouse Rafters:** Hidden with clear sightlines to the breach area
- **False Civilian:** Disguised among panicked townspeople, waiting to get close
- **Chaos Strike:** Using the peak of undead combat to attack when they're distracted
- **Escape Route:** Positioned to flee through her own breach if the ambush fails

### Miha's Tactical Options
- **Assassination:** Wait for a PC to become isolated or vulnerable
- **Poisoned Weapons:** Strike during combat when healing is difficult
- **Environmental:** Use the unstable structures and explosive residue to her advantage
- **Misdirection:** Blend with the chaos, strike, and disappear before retaliation

## Emrik Varrow's Arrival

*Through the rain and smoke, you hear disciplined footsteps. Emrik Varrow appears from between the warehouses, his Triangle-with-eye symbol gleaming wetly. Behind him, three of Lord Castilla's guards maintain formation despite the chaos.*

**Emrik's Assessment:** *"This was sabotage."*


## Sealing the Breach

### Options for PCs
- **Emergency Repair:** Use blast debris and lumber (15 minutes, DC 15 Survival)
- **Magical Reinforcement:** Spells can stabilize the damaged structure
- **Tactical Collapse:** Deliberately collapse unstable sections to create barriers
- **Community Effort:** Rally surviving townspeople for faster repairs (advantage on checks)
- **Leave Strategic Gap:** Maintain controlled access while preventing mass undead flow

## Investigation Opportunities

**Alchemist's Tools DC 14:** The explosive components could have been sourced locally - possibly from Ramon Chartrand's supplies or similar sources.

**Survival DC 13:** The saboteur's tracks lead both to and away from the blast site, suggesting they remained to observe the results.

**Perception DC 16:** Scratch marks on nearby warehouse walls suggest someone climbed to get a better vantage point of their handiwork.

**Insight DC 15:** This wasn't random destruction - someone studied Delios's defenses and chose this specific weak point for maximum impact.

## Potential NPCs in Area

**Hendrick Ferrand** (Ranch Beltath lumberjack) - Injured in the blast, can provide information about who had access to explosives and palisade construction details.

**Warehouse Workers** - May have seen suspicious activity before the explosion or can provide insight into the building layouts.

**Surviving Woodriders** - Professional trackers who might help follow Miha's trail or identify her methods.
