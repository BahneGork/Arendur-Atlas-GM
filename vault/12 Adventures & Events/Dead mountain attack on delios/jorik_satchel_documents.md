# Jorik's Satchel - Tax Collector Documents

## Physical Description
- Well-worn leather messenger satchel with brass buckles
- Baron Hexton's seal stamped on the flap
- Interior has organized compartments for different document types
- Some papers water-stained from the rain during the attack
- Contains quill, ink bottle (cracked), official sealing wax, and Jorik's personal seal

## Document 1: Official Orders from Baron Hexton's Chamberlain

**Seal**: Baron Hexton's official seal (intact)
**Date**: 23rd of Mirtul, 648 Z.E.
**To**: Chief Tax Assessor Aldric Brennan
**From**: Chamberlain Marcus Thorne

*"By order of Regent Hexton of Bedegaarn:*

*The upcoming negotiations with Duke Alvares' representatives require immediate liquidity. You are authorized to implement Emergency Collection Protocols as follows:*

*1. Increase all land assessments by 40% citing wartime necessity*
*2. Implement immediate collection of timber tithes - no seasonal delays*  
*3. Focus priority collections on: Ranch Falca (Martelli holdings), Ranch Beltath (Paget holdings), and merchant district properties*
*4. Any resistance to emergency assessments should be referred to the Moon Knight's authority*
*5. All excess collections beyond Arendur tribute to be held in the Bedegaarn strongboxes pending Duke's arrival*

*The Duke's agents arrive on the 28th of Mirtul. Ensure adequate reserves to demonstrate the Barony's value and our commitment to the proposed alliance.*

*Failure to meet collection targets will be considered dereliction of duty.*

*By His Authority,*  
*Marcus Thorne, Chamberlain to Regent Hexton"*

## Political Implications for Campaign

### Immediate Revelations
1. **Baron Hexton's Desperation**: He's mortgaging the Barony's future for short-term political gain
2. **Duke Alvares' True Plan**: Not an alliance, but an annexation attempt
3. **Local Resistance**: Multiple factions already opposing Baron's policies
4. **Shadow Company Recognition**: They're being watched and considered a potential threat
5. **Economic Crisis**: Emergency taxation is unsustainable and creating enemies

### Long-term Campaign Hooks
1. **Lord Vermil's Mystery**: What arrangement does he have with Ranch Falca?
2. **The Cautier School's Interest**: Lord Davide wants to meet about "regional stability"
3. **Ruby Throne Opposition**: Major guild building case against the Baron
4. **Informant Network**: Someone ("A Friend", "M") is working against the Baron from within
5. **Military Preparations**: Baron expects to need force to maintain control

### Player Options
- **Support Local Resistance**: Help ranchers and merchants oppose illegal taxation
- **Leverage Information**: Use documents to negotiate with various factions
- **Warn Baron Hexton**: Reveal Duke Alvares' true intentions (if they choose)
- **Contact Duke Alvares**: Potentially switch sides in the coming political shift
- **Investigate Lord Vermil**: Follow up on mysterious "arrangements"
- **Ally with Cautier School**: Strengthen relationship with Lord Davide Castilla

This satchel provides multiple layers of political intrigue while respecting the established factions and NPCs in your campaign.
