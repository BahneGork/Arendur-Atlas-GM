# Clear Path to Church - Dead Mountain Raid Encounter

## Encounter Overview
**Type**: Minor Objective (0.5x Medium Difficulty XP Award)
**Duration**: 30-45 minutes
**Party Level**: 7-8 (5 characters)
**Total XP**: 450 XP per character

## Mission Objective
Create a safe evacuation route for civilians from The Milly Bell to Gaed's Sanctuary, then escort them safely to the church where Father Belderone can activate protective wards.


# Stage 1 - crashed carts
Set the scene: 12 people are cowering behind some crates and stuff, the way ahead of them towards the church is blocked by undeads. Skeletons are shooting at them from behind knocked over carts and crates, the street itself is filled with zombies waiting for them to come running.  

### Important
- **Jorik's corpse** in street has **Baron Hexton's documents** (political subplotc)

### **Enemies (CR 6)**
- **6 Skeleton Archers** - positioned behind barricade/carts/crates
	- 3/4 cover +5AC
	- AC 18(cover) - 13, HP 13, Light Crossbow +4 to hit, 1d8+2 piercing
- **4 Zombies**  - at street level around barricade
	- AC 8, HP 22, Slam +4 to hit, 1d6+1 bludgeoning
	- Undead Fortitude (DC 5 + damage taken)
- **1 Ghast** (CR 2) - commander 
	- simple commands 
	- AC 13, HP 36, Bite +3 to hit (2d8+3 piercing), Claws +5 to hit (2d6+3 slashing)
	- Stench: DC 10 Con save or poisoned until start of next turn

# Stage 2: Civilian Escort Mission

### **The Civilian Group**
- **Total**: 12 civilians including Millicent and rescued children
- **Key NPCs**: Millicent, 3 children (ages 6-10), elderly couple, injured shopkeeper, local families
- **Movement Speed**: 20 feet per round, move as single clustered group
## Tactical Considerations for GMs
- **Pacing**: Keep civilians moving, don't let escort bog down
- **Tension**: Balance protecting civilians with engaging threats
- **Resources**: Encourage smart spell/ability usage for crowd control
- **Choices**: Present meaningful decisions about positioning and priorities
- **Stakes**: Make civilian safety feel important and achievable
## GM REMINDERS
- **Keep civilians moving** - don't let escort drag
- **Make civilian safety feel important** but achievable  
- **Encourage creative positioning** and teamwork
- **Track rounds during escort** but be flexible with timing
- **Lysandra is the dramatic climax** - make her memorable

### **Escort Route**
- **Total Distance**: 300 feet from Milly Bell to Gaed's Sanctuary
- **Movement Time**: 15 rounds at normal speed
- **Route**: Northwest along Gaed's Way through residential area

### **Ongoing Threat: Rooftop Harassment**
**4 Skeleton Archers** pursue along rooftops
- **Range**: 80/320 feet with Light crossbow (+4 to hit, 1d8+2 piercing)
- **Tactics**: Focus fire on exposed civilians, relocate when engaged
- **Counter-Tactics**: Party can climb to engage or provide mobile cover


### 2 Ambush Points
MAPS: streets, sewers, cemeteries 3-4, 5-6

#### Ambush 1 (Rounds 5-6)
**Location:** Narrow residential street
- **1 Ghast + 4 Zombies** from damaged houses
- **Ghast's stench** affects whole civilian group (DC 10 Con save) "stunned" for 1 round

#### Ambush 2 (Rounds 10-11)
**Location:** Four-way intersection 200 feet in, 100 feet left, small four way cross road
- **1 Wight + 6 Zombies** surround the group from side streets
- **Wight targets children** with life drain
- **Civilians scatter** 10 feet randomly, need herding back

MAP: cells & shrines s.1-2

##### **Primary Enemy: Oathrot Wraith -  [[Sister Lysandra - the silver flame]]**
**Background**: Former Sister of Silence (warrior nun) who fought demons alongside the Black Eagle Knight during the Orc Wars. Died defending this very temple, now corrupted into an oathrot wraith.

###### **Stats** (CR 6):
- **AC**: 15 (Natural Armor), **HP**: 82 (11d10 + 22)
- **Speed**: 0 ft., fly 60 ft. (hover)
- **Resistances**: Acid, cold, fire, lightning, thunder; bludgeoning, piercing, slashing from nonmagical attacks
- **Immunities**: Necrotic, poison
- **Condition Immunities**: Charmed, exhaustion, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned, prone, restrained
- **Senses**: darkvision 60ft., passive perception 12
- proficiency +2

###### **Abilities**
- **Necrotic Sword**: +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., 4d8+2 necrotic
- **Broken Vows**: DC 13 charisma, begins within 30ft, become cursed -> disadv. CON saves concentration & can't add prof.bonus to ability checks and saves. success = immune for 24hours
- **Incorporeal Movement**: Can move through creatures and objects as difficult terrain (5 force damage if ending turn inside object)
- **Sunlight sensitivity**: disadv. attacks, wisdome perception (sight).
- **Chained to their Oaths**: Oathrot wraiths stand out from other wraiths by the texts that are tethered to them. These tomes bear the oaths they once swore and later betrayed. If a wraith chooses to read the text and reaffirm its oaths, its curse is ended and its soul is freed from undeath. However, most oathrot wraiths are too consumed by self-loathing, pride, or anger to read the texts, seeing the books as constant reminders of their failure.

##### **Supporting Enemies**:
- **3 Wights** (CR 3 each) - her corrupted sister-warriors
- **6 Skeletons** (CR 1/4 each) - former temple guards

**Terrain**:
- Church doors are barred and need to be opened (30 feet ahead of group)
- **Desecrated Ground**: Within 20 feet of Lysandra, healing spells are halved

**Roleplay Elements**:
- *"I defended this place once... now I claim it for the dark!"*
- *"The Silver Flame burns no more - only cold death remains!"*
- Recognizes holy symbols and becomes enraged at clerics/paladins
- Father Belderone may recognize her name from temple history

**Tactical Notes**:
- **Shattered Faith**: Her presence makes church doors harder to open
- **Protective Instinct Twisted**: Specifically targets anyone approaching church doors
- **Historical Connection**: She and the Black Eagle Knight were comrades-in-arms

## Environmental Hazards
- **Slippery Cobblestones**: DC 12 Dex save for civilians or fall prone (lose 1 round)
- **Debris Fields**: Difficult terrain, civilians need assistance (half movement)
- **Broken Glass**: 1 damage to barefoot civilians unless path cleared

## Victory Conditions & Consequences

### **Complete Success** (All Civilians Reach sanctuary)
- **Political Capital**: Major reputation boost with Delios residents
- **Father Belderone's Blessing**: +1 to next saving throw for each PC
- **Sanctuary Established**: Secure rally point for rest of adventure

### **Partial Success** (2+ Civilian casualties)
- **Mixed Reaction**: Some gratitude, some blame from survivors
- **Sanctuary Functional**: Still provides safe haven

### **Poor Success** (4+ Civilian casualties)
- **Political Damage**: Reputation suffers with local population
- **Sanctuary Compromised**: Fewer civilians trust church safety

## Transition to Next Phase
**Upon reaching sanctuary**:
- Father Belderone completes protective ward around church
- Civilians provide intelligence about other threats (western palisade breach)
- Church becomes secure base of operations
- Political NPCs may arrive to assess situation and Shadow Company's actions


## Connection to Larger Adventure
This encounter establishes the church as a safe zone while introducing political elements (Jorik's documents) and demonstrating the Shadow Company's heroic reputation to local NPCs who will remember their actions in the political aftermath of the raid.
