# The Mask of Bor Undar

The mask hangs on a stone pillar, it reflects light like metal and has a red sheen that moves as light or your perspective moves.

![](https://s3.amazonaws.com/files.d20.io/images/282286331/gcrVrdLyNvgkr5eyt8-E_g/med.jpg?1650908249)

it feels coarse to the touch almost like bone and chilly.

INT (history/smith) proficient no action 
smith DC 15:  Every other check is gated behind learning the name of the metal.
- This material has all the properties of the mythical Oxisol metal. Only a handful of smiths has ever worked with this material, some say you need special tools to work it.
- Electrum-Steel infused with bone and porcelain, accentedc with quicksilver on the "string" pieces. It appears as if just underneath the hard surface, is some kind of liquid that has a red sheen.

	dwarf DC 12:   Oxisol is rumoured to be mined from the bones of a dead god deep in the earth.

	smith DC 18:  Weapons forged with Oxisol deal extra necrotic damage.  
	smith DC 20:  Armor forged with Oxisol reduce necrotic damage.
	smith DC 25:  Legend has it that the Oxisol is one of the ingredients used in the creation of the lifestealing sword "Abactor"  

	arcane DC 16:   Oxisol supposedly has necromantic qualities and is highly sought by necromancer.  
	arcane DC 18:  Weapons forged with Oxisol deal extra necrotic damage.
	arcane DC 20:  Armor forged with Oxisol reduce necrotic damage.
	arcane DC 25:  Legend has it that the Oxisol is one of the ingredients used in the creation of the lifestealing sword "Abactor"  

	history DC 18: this is the mask "bor undar", many rumours swirl about this mask. 
		1. the face of a demon that was torn off by a celestial in the war of the ravenous
		2. the remains of a banshee turned defeated by the steel dwarves in the age of creation
		3. a mask worn by the leader of a cult of Orcus, the demonlord of undeath in the age of chaos
		4. all that remains of a dead god, known as the forgotten.
		5. a dwarven king went mad and created this mask when his capitol was under siege.
		6. the mask of the queen of hags
	history DC 23:  A fanatical cult sought out all pieces of Oxisol several hundreds years ago and left a bloodbath behind.

Appraisal:   Smith DC 15  800-1300gp but possibly a lot more as it's a superior quality (value: 3580)
