## Set the Scene
**visibility**: dim light from the main sarcophagy
**smells and sounds:** sulfur 
**Objects to interact with:** Door, 4 standing sarcophagy, 1 main glas sarcophagy
**Exits/pathways:** the hallway you entered from and the door.
**Other senses:** Detect magic - the door and all the sarcophagy.
	door - abjuration, 4 lesser sarcophagy - evocation, main sarcophagy - evocation & abjuration.
**Passive senses**
wisdom (perception) no action *passive* DC 16: you hear a slight soft crackle like a match taking fire but much much lower in volume.
	a magical string of sigils above the doorway ignites and glows purple as they walk through it.
intelligence (arcana) *active action* DC 16: 
	it is a sort of warding, that triggers upon someone entering the doorway and opens "something".

## Call for Action
As they enter the room, the magical writing lights up and 1 round later the lids of the 4 standing sarcophagy spring open. 1 round after, 4 mummies steps out of them brandishing Flamberge swords. 

The mummies are wearing the same regalia, that either signals a station in life or a rank in military. Their movements are very rigid like royal guards changing the guard and so forth. They turn towards the main sarcophagy with the swords held infront of their torsoes. They walk to either side of the door 2 by 2 and stand to attention sword at the ready to strike anyone coming within 5ft.

"what do you do?"

The oath from the main pillar will send them back to their coffins & a deep voice will speak in the air "Who invokes my name?"
