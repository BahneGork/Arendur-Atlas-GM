# Brainstorming the Raven King
The raven king fought off an aberration invasion by entering the gate/rift with an army of undead and the death knight at his side.
They managed to hold off the attack long enough to set and activate a magical crystal then retreat and close the gate. 

On the other side he activated the Forsaken Oozing Obelisk, bonded with the magical crystal on the other side of the gate, the obelisk keeps the gate shut and the crystal shielded.

Using his powers stolen from the aberration realm the raven king forged his second seal, massive Aberrant chains held together by 'Horror' the raven king's sword the chains seals the doors to the Obelisk. 

Before entering the gate the raven king had constructed 2 separate tombs ontop of the structures harboring the gate, one tomb to serve as a fake decoy to keep looters away from the real tomb where he would serve as the third and final seal for infinity. When he retreated from his march into the gate, several aberrations followed through before he could activate the Obelisk.

As he entered his stasis he erected an aberration barrier so they could not leave the tomb and could in a happy accident also serve as tomb guardians making sure no fool would disrupt the seals and break open the gate once again.
