[[Fountain of Pythas acid]]
A strange fountain is also located in this room, it has a central pillar that does not have any holes for liquid to come up out of. The fountain is half filled with purple acid. Next to the fountain is what looks like a lever and a large wheel stuck on top of a rod going into the floor.  The fountain is 10ft wide, 30ft deep into the floor, the central pillar stop 5ft from the floor edge and there's 10ft of air down to the surface of the opaque acid. 15ft of fountain is filled with acid currently.

The edge of the fountain is the rooms floor, it is lined with elaborate stones/tiles each engraved with a [[blindfolded skull of the raven king cult]].

A huge chain goes into the room through a hole in the ceiling corner closest to room 1 and into the fountains bottom.

The lever moves a plate that functions as a plug/gate to the pythagoras siphon system, it is currently blocking (closed). 

The wheel operates flow of liquid(acid), turning it counter-clockwise releases liquid and clockwise slows the flow of liquid. However this wheel controls flow to the similar fountain in room 3. 

If the lever has not been moved in either room, turning a wheel will flood the fountain and the floor will also be filled with acid.
