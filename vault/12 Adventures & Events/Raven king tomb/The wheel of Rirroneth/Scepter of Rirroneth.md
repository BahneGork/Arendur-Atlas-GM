>[!infobox]+
>   ![[Scepter of Rirroneth.jpg|100]]

The Scepter of Rirroneth is a purple/black/gold marble obelisk with teal glowing eldritch far realm runes running down each facing.

Reading the runes can cause madness, WIS DC 16. 

Instead you have to use your spell ability check DC 25 to activate the runes. 

check results:
All results will spawn 1 Adult Oblex in round 1, 2 Chuul's in round 2.

25: gateway is fully active in 2 rounds
20-24: gateway is fully active in 3 rounds
16-20: gateway is fully active in 4 rounds
10-15: gateway is fully active in 5 round + 1d4+1 gibbering mouthers
