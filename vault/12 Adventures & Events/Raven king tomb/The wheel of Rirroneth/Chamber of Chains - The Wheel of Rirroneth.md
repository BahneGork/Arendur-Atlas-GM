# Chamber of Chains - The Wheel of Rirroneth
#wheelofRirroneth #dungeonroom 
Related: [[The wheel of Rirroneth]]

Two huge chains form an X infront of a set of double doors, in the middle of the chains they fixate a weapon "[[Avenger]]" in a sort of mantle of holding on the doors. Until it is picked up, is it just a brilliant source of light.

The chains has to be severed at room [[Chamber of Rage - The Wheel of Rirroneth | 2]] and [[Chamber of turning - the wheel of Rirroneth|3]].

When the chains are severed the [[Avenger | Weapon]] can be pulled from mantle in the door.

A staircase leads up to the door, which is a double door. 
The door is covered in more occult carvings and embellishments. see murals
alittle down from the doors and staircase is a block of stone, that has been used as a sacrificial altar centuries ago. It is stained by ancient blood and so has the floor around it. Bones and skull remains still litter the floor around the altar. Alot of tentacle design covers the sides of the altar. 

## NPCs
The Queen of Bones is here waiting. [[Second encounter with Queen of bones]]


## Poisoned Dart Trap
The wall opposite the chains has a large mural, elobrate carvings, painted with many colors. 
Several features such as eyes and circles in diagrams are pushable. But the sequence has to be correct otherwise poisoned darts shoots from the ceiling. [[Poisoned darts trap]]  DEX DC 15 to avoid for ½ dmg 3d6  [[Poisons 5e#^malice]] 

The correct sequence can be discovered on a note somewhere else.
