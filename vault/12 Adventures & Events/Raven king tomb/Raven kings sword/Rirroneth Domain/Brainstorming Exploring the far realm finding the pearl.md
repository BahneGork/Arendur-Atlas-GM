body horror: flesh golem
statue is a cage for the flesh golem, which is in fact a lost traveller who was the victim of a whim and got turned into this bodily horror.
An amulet coupled with a ritual can set it free and reverse it's transmutation.
however, failing the ritual causes the ritualist to hear strange whispers and must succeed a DC 15 WIS or be charmed focusing on creating blasphemeous designs from the whispers in its mind. Using what ever mediums are available, ink, charcoal, mud, its own blood. Unless restrained it completes the design after 1 hour and a death slaad appear within 30ft and attack anyone in sight.
