# Warped Hostile Flesh Plains
- The fleshy terrain has become soft and warped for miles.
- Sporadically the terrain changes from muddy to quicksand, other times it grows into grabby tentacles.
- Many mouthes of all shapes and sizes, from tiny to huge, filled with razorsharp teeth, snaps at the air in eager anticipation of something to bite into.
- The air is filled with mad indecipherable voices, talking over each other and threatening to to drive sanity away.
- The smell of old blood hangs in the air
## Warped Flesh Plain Challenges
- Your feet sink into soft flesh that wraps around your leg, threatening to pull you further down.
- Tentacles sprout instanteneously forth from the land, agressively slamming against you and grabbing what it can.
- You sink into the fleshy ground to your waist and slogging onwards is very taxing.
- Everywhere you put your foot, mouthes gnash and snap eager to take a bite of you.
- As you progress through the area, you are assaulted by a barrage of maddening voices in all kinds of pitches. Nothing makes sense and you struggle to hold on to your sanity.
- At times it seems as if the ground just spontaneously opens up holes beneath your feet threathening to trip you up.
