# Purple Haze
- a slow rolling haze moves acros the land, 30feet high, miles wide and deep.
- the air is filled with a taste of iron and the smell of ozone
- there is a slight acidic sting to the air where the haze moves.
- at times there are growling, mewing, snarling, sniffing noices coming from within the haze.

## Purple Haze Challenges
- the air turns acidic and burns your skin
- seeing anything in this haze is challenging
- sometimes alien creatures move inside the mist
- The haze has left a maddening poisenous rash on your skin
