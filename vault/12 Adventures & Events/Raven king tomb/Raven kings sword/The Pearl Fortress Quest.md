# The Pearl Fortress
The pearl fortress was created by "[[01 GM/Campaigns/The Arendur Rising Campaign/12 Adventures & Events/Raven king tomb/Raven kings sword/Rirroneth Domain/Bittersweet lovers anticipation]]" to contain "horror" and the ritual of law. 

They removed their heart and from that grew the pearl. The celestial now resides in the pearl unable to leave it and unable to do anything to the sword but contain it. As long as the sword remains in the ritual of law, the pearl remains to contain it. 

The celestial's power is now confined to inside the pearl and can therefore not call upon help from the outside.

# The Swords Situation
the celestials sword: avenger seals the gate, can pierce the heart of the celestial.
the raven kings sword: horror is the key to  the ritual of law in the pearl fortress.

avenger can kill the heart, and thus bittersweet lovers anticipation for good.

If the heroes promies bittersweet lovers anticipation to disrupt the ritual of law, they will bestow upon them a [[gift of the world]], an orb of liquid sapphire is to be touched and will randomly gift them one temporary power. 

If the ritual of law is disrupted, the celestial is reunited with its heart. The raven kings sword can be claimed.

if the heart is destroyed, the ritual of law will complete and the sword of the raven king can be claimed.

# Resoultions
1. If the heart is not destroyed the raven king will reclaim his sword and become a sworn enemy of the company. The celestial will be in their favor and free again, the court of arcadia will be friendly. The court of the seven cities will be hostile.

2. If the heart is destroyed, all elves will feel the wrongness of the company, as they will forever hear the death scream echoing across all time. The court of arcadia will be hostile, the court of seven cities will be friendly. The sword avenger will be destroyed in the process of destroying the heart and the raven kings sword will be offered as a reward.
