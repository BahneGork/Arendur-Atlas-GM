# Avenger
The weapon of [[01 GM/Campaigns/The Arendur Rising Campaign/12 Adventures & Events/Raven king tomb/Raven kings sword/Rirroneth Domain/Bittersweet lovers anticipation]]

Currently held as part of the lock that bars a gate to the far realm.

When nobody wields the weapon it is just light, it is not until someone grabs it's hilt that it takes on a specific weapon shape and this depends on the wielder.

Fighter: Spear
Cleric: warhammer
Wizard: quarterstaff
Barbarian: greataxe
Sorcerer: dagger
Paladin: Morningstar
Druid: Sickle

+2, +1d6 radiant
Versatile (next dice level)
Finesse

Sentient [[Sentient Magic]]
