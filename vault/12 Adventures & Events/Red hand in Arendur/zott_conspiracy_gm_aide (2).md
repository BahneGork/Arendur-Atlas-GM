## Operational Summary

**How This Investigation Works**: Assumi starts with one of six entry points into a web of interconnected conspiracy nodes. Each node contains multiple clues pointing to other nodes, ensuring she can always find new leads. The conspiracy operates on three levels:

1. **Information Suppression**: Preventing Red Hand intelligence from reaching decision-makers
2. **Corpse Collection**: Gathering bodies for ritual purposes using red dwarf gold detection
3. **Divine Orchestration**: Lord Zott manipulating events to birth his avatar

The investigation reveals these layers progressively, with the Vampyramid escalating responses as Assumi gets closer to the truth. Every node is a center of gravity with its own compelling reason to investigate, and the modular structure lets you adapt to any player approach.

**Player Agency**: Never force specific approaches. If Assumi creates unexpected connections, incorporate them. If she bypasses a node, redistribute its clues. The conspiracy is robust enough to survive any investigation path.

**Player Goal**: Uncover the conspiracy preventing proper siege preparations while researching time magic.

# The Vampyramid: Escalating Responses

## Level 1: Reflex Responses
*Triggered when players investigate Level 1 nodes*

### Response 1A: Bureaucratic Delays
**Trigger**: Official inquiries about missing intelligence or body collection
**Implementation**: 
- Forms require additional signatures and approvals
- Key officials are "unavailable" or "in meetings"
- Paperwork gets "lost" and must be resubmitted
**NPCs Involved**: Clerks, administrators, low-level officials
**Duration**: 1-2 days of delays per attempt

### Response 1B: Misdirection
**Trigger**: Investigation into time magic restrictions or cult activities
**Implementation**:
- False leads pointing to legitimate druid organizations
- Planted evidence suggesting academic politics rather than conspiracy
- Reliable sources suddenly become uncertain about information
**NPCs Involved**: Node 2C (Sylas), Node 1A (Fenwick)
**Goal**: Waste time and resources on false trails

### Response 1C: Minor Surveillance
**Trigger**: Multiple Level 1 investigations
**Implementation**:
- Subtle watchers noting player activities
- Questions about player asked to their contacts
- Unusual "coincidences" of people appearing near player
**NPCs Involved**: Node 2D (Whisper), street-level informants
**Mechanical Effect**: Passive Perception DC 15 to notice surveillance

## Level 2: Probe Responses
*Triggered when players penetrate Level 2 nodes*

### Response 2A: Social Pressure
**Trigger**: Confronting Level 2 NPCs with evidence
**Implementation**:
- Node 2E (Henrik) mobilizes noble connections against House Avalande
- Node 2B (Marcus) publishes article questioning player's mental state
- Official complaints about player's "disruptive" investigations
**Effect**: House Avalande faces increased political pressure
**Mechanical**: Disadvantage on social interactions with nobles for 1 week

### Response 2B: Professional Discrediting
**Trigger**: Breaking into official locations or stealing information
**Implementation**:
- Node 3C (Valdris) questions player's Academy affiliations
- Node 2A (Aldwin) reports "break-ins" to city guard
- Node 2C (Sylas) restricts player's library access entirely
**Effect**: Official organizations become hostile or uncooperative
**Duration**: Until player clears their reputation somehow

### Response 2C: Magical Interference
**Trigger**: Using magic for investigation or breaking magical defenses
**Implementation**:
- Node 3D (Madame Vex) uses temporal magic to cause confusion
- Scrying attempts to monitor player activities
- Minor curses or magical harassment
**Mechanical**: Random spell failures (DC 12 Constitution save or spell fails)
**Duration**: 3 days unless detected and dispelled

## Level 3: Warning Responses
*Triggered when players threaten Level 3 nodes*

### Response 3A: Direct Threats
**Trigger**: Exposing Level 3 operations or NPCs
**Implementation**:
- Anonymous threatening messages delivered to player
- "Accidents" happen near player (falling objects, runaway carts)
- Contacts receive warnings to avoid helping player
**NPCs Involved**: Hired thugs, corrupted city watch
**Encounter**: 2-3 **Thugs** stage "random" attack with non-lethal intent

### Response 3B: Economic Pressure
**Trigger**: Threatening Node 3B (Ruby Throne agent)
**Implementation**:
- House Avalande's financial accounts face "audits" and freezes
- Merchants refuse service or charge excessive prices
- Property taxes and fees suddenly come due
**Effect**: Player's resources become limited
**Mechanical**: Lifestyle costs double, certain services unavailable

### Response 3C: Isolation
**Trigger**: Multiple Level 3 nodes compromised
**Implementation**:
- Key contacts become "unavailable" or refuse meetings
- Player's reputation suffers from coordinated rumors
- Official channels close down entirely
**Effect**: Player must rely on underground networks
**Duration**: Until conspiracy is exposed or major success achieved

## Level 4: Pressure Responses
*Triggered when players penetrate Level 4 nodes*

### Response 4A: Frame Job
**Trigger**: Discovering cult's public front operations
**Implementation**:
- Evidence planted suggesting player is Red Hand agent
- Node 3C (Valdris) provides "intelligence" supporting frame
- City guard issued warrant for player's arrest
**Encounter**: **Captain** + 4 **Guards** attempt arrest
**Effect**: Player becomes wanted fugitive
**Escape Options**: Underground networks, political allies, clearing name

### Response 4B: Assassination Attempt
**Trigger**: Threatening information suppression network
**Implementation**:
- Professional killers hired to eliminate threat
- Attack staged to look like Red Hand operation
- Node 3B (Robert Abrami) provides funding and planning
**Encounter**: **Assassin** + 2 **Spies** attack at opportune moment
**Tactics**: Ambush, poison, hit-and-run if unsuccessful
**Evidence**: If captured, assassins know nothing of ultimate employers

### Response 4C: Magical Compulsion
**Trigger**: Exposing noble house infiltration
**Implementation**:
- Attempt to magically dominate or charm player
- Memory modification to forget discovered information
- Magical surveillance to monitor all activities
**Encounter**: **Enchanter** (use **Mage** with enchantment focus) attempts magical control
**Save**: DC 16 Wisdom saving throw to resist initial effect
**Duration**: 24 hours if successful, requires Remove Curse to fully cleanse

## Level 5: Violence Responses
*Triggered when players threaten Level 5 nodes*

### Response 5A: Open Warfare
**Trigger**: Confronting Kalarel or discovering Black Obelisk
**Implementation**:
- All cult resources mobilized against player
- Undead sent to attack player's allies and contacts
- No more pretense of secrecy maintained
**Encounters**: Multiple waves of **Cultists** and **Undead**
**Targets**: House Avalande properties, player's known associates
**Goal**: Complete elimination of threat before exposure

### Response 5B: Accelerated Timeline
**Trigger**: Threatening obelisk operations
**Implementation**:
- Rush Son of Zott birth ritual with available corpses
- Sacrifice current cult members to boost power
- Attempt to claim Arendur immediately even if incomplete
**Effect**: Son of Zott manifests early but at reduced power
**Timeline**: Ritual completion within 24 hours
**Opportunity**: Less powerful avatar is more vulnerable

## Level 6: Revelation Response
*Triggered when Lord Zott's involvement is discovered*

### Response 6: Divine Intervention
**Trigger**: Direct discovery of Zott's involvement
**Implementation**:
- Lord Zott manifests avatar immediately regardless of preparation
- Reality begins warping around obelisk site
- Temporal distortions affect entire district
**Encounter**: **Avatar of Zott** (Modified **Planetar** with necromancy focus)
**Environmental**: Time magic effects, reality distortions
**Stakes**: Future of Arendur and prevention of demonic incursion

- **Early Sessions**: Normal political dysfunction masks conspiracy activity
- **Mid Sessions**: Increased tensions as evidence emerges, factions polarize
- **Late Sessions**: Crisis atmosphere as truth revealed, emergency measures activated

### Session 1-2 Encounters
- **Focus**: Social challenges and investigation, establishing the mystery
- **Opposition**: Single opponents or small groups, mostly social obstacles
- **Tone**: Subtle resistance, bureaucratic delays, mysterious coincidences
- **Escape Priority**: Stealth and social solutions preferred over direct combat

- **Focus**: Mix of social, investigation, and action; conspiracy fights back
- **Opposition**: Professional opponents (guards, spies, corrupted officials)
- **Tone**: Active interference, threats, and pressure; stakes increase
- **Consequences**: Direct confrontation brings serious retaliation

### Session 5-6 Encounters
- **Focus**: High-stakes investigation and combat; final revelations
- **Opposition**: Magical opponents, supernatural threats, organized resistance
- **Tone**: Open warfare, desperate measures, cosmic horror revelation

### Information Management
- **Level 1-2 Nodes**: Establish that something is wrong, basic patterns emerge

- **Early**: 70% investigation/social, 30% action/combat

- **Session 3**: First evidence of Red Hand coordination

# Player Agency & Reactive Guidelines

## Core Reactive Principles

**Never Force Solutions**: The conspiracy structure provides multiple paths to every revelation. If Assumi approaches a problem unexpectedly, let her approach work and adapt the results.

**Failure Forward**: Failed investigations don't stop progress - they create complications, attract attention, or reveal different aspects of the conspiracy. Use the Vampyramid to turn failures into new opportunities.

**Clue Redistribution**: If Assumi bypasses a node entirely, redistribute its essential clues to nodes she does investigate. The conspiracy's information network is interconnected enough to justify this.

**Player-Generated Connections**: When Assumi suspects connections between elements that weren't originally linked, consider making those connections real. Player theories often create more interesting story than prepared plots.

## Adaptation Techniques

### When Plans Go Sideways
- **Unexpected Alliance**: If Assumi allies with an NPC you planned as neutral/hostile, make them an inside source with valuable intelligence
- **Wrong Node, Right Questions**: If she investigates the "wrong" location but asks good questions, place relevant clues there anyway
- **Creative Approaches**: If she uses magic, contacts, or methods you didn't anticipate, reward creativity with additional information

### Improvisation Framework
- **Every NPC Has**: A secret fear, a secret desire, and one piece of information they shouldn't know
- **Every Location Contains**: One hidden element, one overlooked detail, and one connection to another node
- **Every Document Includes**: One useful truth, one misleading detail, and one unexpected revelation

### Escalation Control
- **De-escalation**: If Assumi backs off or tries diplomatic solutions, reduce Vampyramid pressure temporarily
- **Acceleration**: If she pushes hard and fast, compress timeline and increase stakes accordingly
- **Lateral Movement**: If she explores unexpected angles, expand conspiracy into new areas organically

## Node-Based Flexibility

### Adding Nodes On-the-Fly
When Assumi's investigation creates new leads, add them as temporary nodes:
1. **Give them 2-3 clues pointing to existing nodes**
2. **Create one new revelation that enhances the conspiracy**
3. **Add appropriate Vampyramid response when threatened**

### Fractal Investigation
Any node can be expanded into sub-nodes if Assumi focuses intensely:
- **NPC Becomes Network**: A single corrupt official reveals connections to family, associates, superiors
- **Location Becomes Complex**: A simple shop reveals basement operations, supplier networks, customer lists
- **Organization Becomes Hierarchy**: A cult front reveals multiple cells, competing factions, hidden leadership

### Cross-Node Connections
The conspiracy is interconnected enough to justify unexpected links:
- **Financial**: Red dwarf gold creates connections between seemingly unrelated operations
- **Personal**: NPCs have histories and relationships beyond their conspiracy roles  
- **Magical**: Divine influence means patterns repeat across different areas
- **Political**: Arendur's factions touch every aspect of city life
