Great Warmaster, 

I present to you here with this shipment, 2 examples of your secret weapon. The “**Razorfiends**”. **Regilarix** has been in dominating Basharix, we have managed to procure 3 clutches in total from both. Getting Ozziandrix on our side was fortunate.

We have successfully taken over contact with the smugglers after Ratbag’s demise. The sky iron will now flow through Blackspear instead of the Citadel. We have enlisted the Lizardmen who have flocked to the swamp to worship **Regilarix** to transporting in the swamp. We have sent the first shipment of sky iron items to **Glitterhame**, I got word that our allies there finally worked out the secret of working the **forge of fury**. Our warlocks have begun work on a portal, so we can get both the Razorfiends and the fury weapons to the horde. 

[[**The Daughters of Meg**]] takes less and less interest in our work, they seem to be working on projects of their own. One of those i learned had to do with Bedegaarn Keep, they sent Sunkiller there on business. At least they are not as meddling anymore. I wonder if **Ripper** and **Stormshadow** are done with them? 

We are still escorting packs of Stormshadows prisoners to the Aunties, these **Ogre Plant monsters** are even bigger and stronger than the first ones. Remind me not to be caught as a criminal in Takarnn! 

The swamp is growing still, Regilarix enjoys it and any potential wanderers wont be stumbling upon us easily. I have let loose a few Razorfiends to take care of any nosy adventurers wandering too close to the Castle. We shall not share the fate of Ratbag. 

We have taken in a **nearby goblin tribe** to act as eyes and ears in the blackwood, they are VERY good spies. The nearby elves keep out of the swamp, Regilarix is constantly arguing for razing their city. Mostly i think it’s because she found out they have one of **the lost staves of Illnor**. But unless they cause any trouble i dont want to waste resources on them and their giant owls.

Our scouts have returned from the sunken citadel, the Oztters have cleaned up and the corruption we started there has taken root. The daughters said that the heart of the forest is infected and once it is entirely consumed by the corruption, the blackwood will live up to its name. They seem very happy about it, I don’t see how that helps our war, maybe it makes it easier for our allies in the Feywild to get through? I would ask the Daughters, but i really don’t like them or being around them for too long. I am sure they are plotting to cook and eat me, maybe my dragon keeps them in check or maybe they are just pre-occupied with something else. I mean why are they sending minions to Mercadia? 

I shall send word on the sending stone when we are ready to open the portal.

Warchief Saarvith
