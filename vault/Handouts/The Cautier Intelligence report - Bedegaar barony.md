# The Star Chamber - [[The black lilly order]]
The home of the Black lilly order, Regent Hexton's knight order in Bedegaarn. 
If Hexton or the Knight order has any secrets, this must be where they keep them.
Maybe if you can get inside, you could uncover some secrets or take out one or more lieutenants?
- The starchamber is also their temple, sanctuary and mystic source of power. 
- They closely guard the rituals they conduct. 
- Very few outsiders are allowed inside the building.
- They frequently purchase supplies for their rituals from a guild of arcanists and herbalists, known for wearing cowled green robes.
- The bedegaarn prison  also periodically brings priosners to the star chamber and they never leave. 
- Since this is their head quarters, there might be important information. Battle plans, alliances or other secrets.

# Feyfolk Court
%% fey court %%
There are rumours of a fey court inside Blackwood. 

We do not known if they have any alliances or where exactly they are located or even what they call themselves. 

We know there are elves in Blackwood, they are often seen flying over the forest on giant owls.

But ever since the dragon showed up 5 years ago, sightings have been less frequent. 

There is one who visits Delios and other settlements on a winged white owlbear.

- Finding the elves is most likely not the challenge, it is likely they will find you first if you go looking.
- Negotiating with elves might be hard, since the last alliance between elves & men in the region was broken by men centuries ago 
- Fey folks are known to be mercurial of temperament, changing with the seasons. Chaotic in the spring, joyful in the summer, contetmplating and melancholic in the autumn and fatalistic in the winter. Winter is never the time for new beginnings (or alliances) but it can be a time of war.

# White Tusk Orcs
This is a relative new faction, they are normally based in the southern mountain chain, but recently they wandered into the blackwoods where they roam like nomads.

The Orcs value strength. It is important for a white tusk leader to show the clan that either she or the clan gained something of value as part of a negotiation.

The steel knights came across corpses of Orcs mauled by a massive beast. The injuries and tracks would indicate something akin to a giant ape. If you wish to deal with the white tusks, perhaps defeating this beast can buy you goodwill with them. 

- We believe them to be nomadic and in the blackwoods 
- We are not aware of them being in any known alliances 
- defeat the giant ape to gain favor and goodwill 
- alternatively grant the clan a gift of a magic item
- an elite orc unit to fight for us could prove very useful

# [[The charnel house]]
Not long ago a group calling themselves the Charnel House settled in the region, 
After they defeated the wizard Zhakius Stormborn they seized his tower as salvage and spoils of war.
A perpetual storm rages around the top of the tower, which sits atop a rocky hilltop. 
The tower once guarded deep catacombs, which no one remembers the origin of.

The Charnel house has a bad reputation, they will just as soon set you on fire as look at you.

Their leader is Irae Firetouched a halfling fire sorcerer 
the other members are known as 
- Kryx Blodgnash, Beast of the dark wood 
- Athlonia the moon's eclipse
- Uldun the silent death 

They are mercenaries and are known to hire local brigands, spies, cult fanatics, murderers and regular mercenaries to bolster their ranks.
