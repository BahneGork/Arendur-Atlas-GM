Someone summons the pcs to the future, to help with a crisis.

# Premise
Coming of the black moon
Zott wants to remove himself form the heavens to be closer to the world of diamor. duplicating the feat of nuitari from krynn, manifesting himself as a black moon. This will allow him to walk amongst mortals again. 

# How
His avatar, chosen one needs to do the following:
- Reclaim control of Arendur
- Eradicate the cults of chaos worshippers
- Gain access to the Ruby prison
- Use the powers of the Ruby prison to alter reality to manifest Zott as a black moon 
- Surrender the avatar to Zott
