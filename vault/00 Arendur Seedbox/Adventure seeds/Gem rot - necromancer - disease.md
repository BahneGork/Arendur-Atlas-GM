the PCs arrived at a town which was seeing a breakout of a disease I called gemrot.

The disease would crystalize a person's blood which would cause internal bleeding, organ failure, and hemorrhaging. The disease could spread through airborne particles or contact with an infected person's blood. The PCs didn't feel they could do much, so they were going to get the hell outta there as quickly as possible, but one of them got sick and the race was on to find a cure. They came to learn, through a great deal of sleuthing, that a necromancer that had set up his lair nearby had unleashed the disease on the town, hoping to earn himself some highly virulent zombies. What the necromancer underestimated, however, was how virulent the disease actually was, and became infected himself. In this setting, getting access to disease curing spells and abilities wasn't easy, and the preparations the necromancer had set up so as not to become infected himself failed, so he was desperately attempting to devise a cure for himself until the PCs arrived, merked him, took his notes, realized the necromancer was actually pretty close to devising the cure, and used his lair to finish it and distribute it to the town.

 part of solving how the disease spread and where it came from was that animals could also become infected and spread it, which led them to investigate the surrounding area. (edited)

And thus found the necromancer's lair when they had to fight disease spewing animal zombies (edited)
