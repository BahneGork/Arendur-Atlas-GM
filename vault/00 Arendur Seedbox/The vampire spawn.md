# Brainstorming Things in the Lab
Books and scrolls on alchemical recipes and techniques, including ones specifically related to the production of the worker elixirs used by Ranch Falca. The lab could also have notes and experiments on refining the elixirs or testing their effects on different individuals.

- A collection of different herbs, plants, and animal parts used in alchemical concoctions. This could include vampire blood, Sort Kvalm (an herb with magical properties), as well as other rare and exotic ingredients.

- Various alchemical tools and equipment, such as mortar and pestles, distillation apparatus, alembics, and vials for storing potions.

- Potions and elixirs of various kinds, both finished products and works in progress. These could include healing potions, potions of invisibility or strength, or experimental concoctions with unknown effects.

- A locked cabinet or storage area containing dangerous substances or volatile potions that are too risky to keep out in the open. This could add an element of danger if the players were to accidentally stumble upon it or try to break into it.

- Hidden compartments or secret passages within the lab that lead to other parts of the estate or underground chambers. These secret areas could hold additional treasures or secrets related to Lord Vermil's true nature or his involvement with Ranch Falca.

- Notes or correspondence related to Lord Vermil's suspicions about Ranch Falca and his possible involvement with vampire spawn. This could provide valuable information for the players if they are investigating Ranch Falca themselves.

- Personal belongings and mementos that provide insight into Ramon Chartrand's past and interests. This could include family photos, letters from loved ones, or trinkets from his travels.

- Alchemical experiments gone wrong: broken vials, spilled liquids that have caused unusual effects in the lab. These accidents could have created mutated creatures lurking around the lab, adding a combat encounter for the players.

- Clues or hints about the ancient Azarumme empire and Lord Vermil's possible connection to it. This could be in the form of ancient artifacts, dusty tomes, or cryptic symbols that need deciphering.

- A hidden study or library within the lab, filled with rare books and texts on various subjects such as history, magic, and alchemy. This could provide lore or clues to guide the players' next steps in the campaign.

Remember to tailor these ideas to fit your specific campaign and adjust them as needed for a balanced and engaging gameplay experience.
