You could run a time loop adventure.  The Adventure Zone did one for an arc in their first campaign - it was a blast.  Kind of riffing on Zelda Majora’s Mask.  They came to a town that was stuck in a time bubble, where after X time a disaster would happen, destroying the town - and then time resets.  The first time the PCs died and then reset blew their mind.  Time was too limited to go to all the places in one go, and there were events (like a bank heist) that would go off at certain times.  So they had to use different loops to go to different places and learn information that would be useful next time.  Eg there was an elemental construct as the town guard.  First few times through they needed to RP their way past it, but later they learned it’s control password.  Ultimately they needed to find and stop the source of the disaster.

So your Caves of Time could be like that - there is something going on that the C Wizard needs to use their time magic to understand and unravel, and doing multiple time loops is the key to getting all the info they need.

Someone on Reddit wrote up the TAZ adventure as notes to run your own campaign - the time loop arc is "The Eleventh Hour": https://www.reddit.com/r/TheAdventureZone/comments/54mu8k/start_your_own_taz_campaign_today/ (edited)
