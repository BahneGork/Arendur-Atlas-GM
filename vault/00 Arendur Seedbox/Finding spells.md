Contrive acid puddles and explosive runes to destroy most of any spell book the PC gets their hands on, then give them 1-4 spells. Fewer if they go for utility, more if they just like interchangable damage dealers. You can even give them an option between several spells- "which page do you save?" is a little bit meta, but you can explain that they watch enemy wizards like a hawk and have a sense of where anything they saw cast would sit in the book (and nobody is gonna call you on the enemy wizard having had these memorized since that morning, but you can say that the guy had a tick where he'd put his thumb at that bit of the book on his hip or w/e,) and generally the player is gonna have a better time trying to salvage a spell off a half burnt page if they actually want to use it, than merely being taxed until they transcribe every spell in a book over to their own.
