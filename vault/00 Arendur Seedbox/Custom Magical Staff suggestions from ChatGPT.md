1.  Ray of Enfeeblement (1 charge) - As per the spell from the Player's Handbook

As the wielder advances in level, the Raven King's Staff grows in power, gaining new abilities and charges. Here are some examples of how the staff could improve as the wielder advances:

Level 10: The staff gains the ability to cast the spell Shadow of Moil (4 charges) - As per the spell from Xanathar's Guide to Everything

Level 14: The staff gains the ability to cast the spell Circle of Death (5 charges) - As per the spell from the Player's Handbook

Level 18: The staff gains the ability to cast the spell Power Word Kill (6 charges) - As per the spell from the Player's Handbook

Additionally, the wielder of the staff gains resistance to necrotic damage, and they are able to command any undead creatures within 60 feet of them as if they were under the effects of the spell Command Undead (as per the spell from the Player's Handbook).

However, the Raven King's Staff is not without its drawbacks. The staff has a will of its own and will occasionally act independently of its wielder, seeking to further the Raven King's agenda. The staff also drains the life force of its wielder, dealing 1d10 necrotic damage to the wielder every time a spell is cast using charges from the staff.

The Raven King's Staff is a potent artifact, and any adventurer who wields it will be able to unleash devastating magic upon their enemies. However, they must also be wary of the staff's dark influence and the toll it takes on their own life force.
# Raven Kings Staff #2
**Raven King's Staff**

_Artifact (Requires Attunement)_

The Raven King's Staff is a long black rod made of an unknown material that feels cool to the touch. It is decorated with silver inlays and topped with a stylized raven head. The staff resonates with powerful magic and whispers arcane secrets to its wielder.

At level 8, the Raven King's Staff has the following properties:

-   +2 to attack and damage rolls made with the staff
-   The staff has 10 charges that can be used to cast any spell from the wizard spell list of 4th level or lower (the spell must be of a level the user can cast). The staff regains 1d6+4 expended charges each day at dawn. A spell costs its level in charges.
-   The staff can be used as a spellcasting focus for the wielder's wizard spells.
-   The wielder has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
-   The wielder can use the staff to cast the spell _Feather Fall_ once per day without expending a charge.

At level 12, the Raven King's Staff gains the following properties:

-   The staff's bonus to attack and damage rolls increases to +3.
-   The staff has 20 charges that can be used to cast any spell from the wizard spell list of 6th level or lower. The staff regains 1d10+5 expended charges each day at dawn.
-   The staff can be used to cast the spell _Levitate_ once per day without expending a charge.
-   The wielder can use the staff to cast the spell _Fly_ once per day without expending a charge.

At level 16, the Raven King's Staff gains the following properties:

-   The staff's bonus to attack and damage rolls increases to +4.
-   The staff has 30 charges that can be used to cast any spell from the wizard spell list of 8th level or lower. The staff regains 1d12+6 expended charges each day at dawn.
-   The wielder can use the staff to cast the spell _Telekinesis_ once per day without expending a charge.

At level 20, the Raven King's Staff gains the following properties:

-   The staff's bonus to attack and damage rolls increases to +5.
-   The staff has an unlimited number of charges that can be used to cast any spell from the wizard spell list of 9th level or lower. The staff regains all expended charges each day at dawn.
-   The wielder can use the staff to cast the spell _Time Stop_ once per day without expending a charge.

Additionally, the Raven King's Staff has the following properties:

-   The staff can be used to cast the spell _Feather Fall_ or _Levitate_ without expending a charge if the wielder is falling or has fallen.
-   The staff can be used to cast any spell that the wielder has prepared without expending a charge if the wielder is in danger and has no more spells available.
-   The wielder can use the staff to cast the spell _Arcane Eye_ at will without expending a charge.

Finally, the Raven King's Staff has a powerful curse that binds the wielder to the Raven King's will. If the wielder ever disobeys or betrays the Raven King, they suffer 10d10 psychic damage and lose the ability to attune to magical items for 1d10 days.

# Raven Kings Staff #3
**Raven King's Staff**

_Artifact, requires attunement by a spellcaster_

This twisted blackwood staff is topped with a shining crystal that glimmers with an inner light. The staff has 20 charges, and it regains 2d8+4 expended charges daily at dawn.

While holding the staff, you can use an action to expend some of its charges to cast one of the following spells from it:

-   **Arcane Bolt:** You can use an action to spend 1 charge and cast _magic missile_ from the staff as if using a 5th-level spell slot.
    
-   **Darkness:** You can use an action to spend 2 charges and cast _darkness_ from the staff as if using a 2nd-level spell slot.
    
-   **Dimension Door:** You can use an action to spend 4 charges and cast _dimension door_ from the staff.
    
-   **Hold Monster:** You can use an action to spend 5 charges and cast _hold monster_ from the staff as if using a 5th-level spell slot.
    

In addition, the staff has the following properties:

-   **Spell Absorption:** While holding the staff, you have advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects, and you can use your reaction to absorb a spell targeted at you. When you do so, the staff regains a number of charges equal to the level of the absorbed spell.
    
-   **Spell Resistance:** You have resistance to damage from spells while holding the staff.
    

**Secrets Unveiled:**

If you devote yourself to the Raven King's god of secrets and complete a task of great significance to the god's interests, the staff gains additional powers. The staff gains the following additional properties while you remain attuned to it and continue to serve the god of secrets:

-   **Divine Insight:** Once per day, you can use an action to cast _detect thoughts_ from the staff without expending any charges.
    
-   **Aura of Dread:** As an action, you can spend 5 charges to create an aura of fear in a 60-foot radius centered on yourself. Creatures within the aura must make a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened for 1 minute. A creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. Once you use this property, you can't use it again until the next dawn.
    
-   **Secrets Unveiled:** You gain proficiency in one Intelligence (Investigation) or Wisdom (Insight) skill of your choice. Additionally, once per week, you can use an action to ask the staff a single question related to a secret, mystery, or enigma. The staff will provide an answer to the best of its ability. This property can only be used while you are in a place where the god of secrets holds sway, such as a temple or holy site.

# Raven Kings Staff #4
**Regular Attunement:**

-   +2 to attack and damage rolls made with the staff
-   The staff has 20 charges for casting spells. It regains 2d8 + 4 expended charges daily at dawn.
-   You can use an action to expend 1 or more charges to cast one of the following spells from it: darkness (1 charge), detect magic (1 charge), dispel magic (3 charges), fear (3 charges), fireball (5th-level version, 5 charges), fly (3 charges), ice storm (4 charges), invisibility (2 charges), knock (2 charges), lightning bolt (5th-level version, 5 charges), mage armor (1 charge), magic missile (1 charge), ray of enfeeblement (1 charge), shield (2 charges), or spider climb (2 charges).
-   The staff can also be wielded as a magic quarterstaff. When you make a melee attack with the staff, you can use one of the following properties:
    -   On a hit, the target takes an extra 1d6 cold damage.
    -   On a hit, the target takes an extra 1d6 lightning damage.

**Devotion to the God of Secrets Attunement:**

In addition to the regular attunement powers, if the wielder devotes herself to the God of Secrets, she gains access to the following powers:

-   You gain proficiency in the Deception and Stealth skills, and you can cast the pass without trace spell once per day without expending a charge from the staff.
-   You can use an action to cast detect thoughts (2 charges) or suggestion (3 charges) from the staff without expending a spell slot. When you cast detect thoughts using the staff, you can target up to three creatures within range.
-   Once per day, you can cast the spell modify memory (7 charges) from the staff. When you cast this spell using the staff, it requires no material components, and the spell's duration is increased to 24 hours.
-   When you successfully hit a creature with an attack using the staff, you can choose to deal psychic damage instead of the normal damage type.
