# Vortex Portal Beast
Main:
Related: [[portal beast brainstorm]]

Lovecraftian Portal Beast
Medium aberration, neutral evil
Armor Class 19 (natural armor)
Hit Points 120 (16d8 + 32)
Speed 40 ft.

STR 20 (+5) DEX 18 (+4) CON 14 (+2)
INT 18 (+4) WIS 16 (+3) CHA 18 (+4)

Saving Throws Wisdom +7, Charisma +8
Damage Immunities poison, psychic
Damage Resistances bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical weapons, acid, cold, fire, lightning, thunder, force
Condition Immunities charmed, frightened, paralyzed, stunned
Senses darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13
Languages Deep Speech, telepathy 120 ft.

Multiattack. The beast makes two attacks: one with its tentacles and one with its claws.

Tentacles. Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (1d8 + 5) bludgeoning damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 17). Until this grapple ends, the target is restrained, and the beast can't use its tentacles on another target.

Claws. Melee Weapon Attack: +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 10 (1d8 + 5) slashing damage and the target takes 5 (1d10) piercing damage at the start of each of its turns until it is stabilized or receives magical healing.

Eldritch Blast. Ranged Spell Attack: +8 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. Hit: 12 (4d6) force damage.

Portal Warp (Recharge 5-6). The beast uses its portal to transport itself or a creature it can see within 60 feet of it to a different location within 60 feet of it.

Eldritch Storm (1/day). The beast creates a storm of eldritch energy, engulfing all creatures within 20 feet of it. Each creature in the area must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking 28 (8d6) force damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

Summon Lovecraftian (1/day). The beast uses its portal to summon 1d4 other Lovecraftian creatures of CR 5 or lower. These creatures appear within 60 feet of the beast and act immediately on the beast's turn.

Time Warp (1/day). The beast uses its magical abilities to warp the fabric of time, affecting all creatures within 30 feet of it. Each creature in the area must make a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, the creature's speed is halved, it can't take reactions, and it can only perform 1 action, no effects that grants additional actions functions. These effects last for 1 minute. On a successful save, the creature is unaffected by the time warp.
