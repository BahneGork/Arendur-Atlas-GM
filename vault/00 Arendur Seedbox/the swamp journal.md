Day 1: Delios, Ready for Departure We've gathered all our supplies and are setting out for the swamp today. We heard of a hag deep in the swamp and we're hoping to find their lair. The locals in Delios don't know much about the swamp, but the lumberjacks at the biggest lumber ranch have seen signs of lizardfolk and heard strange noises in the distance. They told us to be careful and to watch out for quicksand.

Day 2: Into the Depths We've been traveling for a day now and the swamp is even more treacherous than we expected. We've had to avoid quicksand and swamp creatures every step of the way. The mosquitos are relentless.  We were lucky enough to find some fresh rain water in a stoop to refill our supplies.

Day 3: Lizardfolks!  we encountered a group of lizardfolk today, they were hostile and we had to defend ourselves. We learned that they won't hesitate to attack if they feel threatened. We've marked the area on our map to avoid it in the future.

Day 3: Dragon Sighting We saw a dragon in the distance, flying over the treetops. It was massive and awe-inspiring, we will have to avoid it's attention if we want to survive.

Day 9: Closer to the Lair. We stumbled upon what seems to be the entrance to the hags' lair today. It's a narrow opening in the side of a hill, covered in vines and brambles. We couldn't get close enough to see what's inside, but we've marked the location on our map. We'll have to come back prepared for whatever lies ahead.

Day 9: Camp for the Night We've set up camp for the night, our journey has been exhausting but we're close to finding what we came for. We'll rest up and prepare for the final push tomorrow.

Notes on Hag's Lair Entrance:

-   Located in the side of a hill, covered in vines and brambles
-   Marked by a tall tree with a distinctive knot
-   Approximately 2 miles south of the dragon sighting
